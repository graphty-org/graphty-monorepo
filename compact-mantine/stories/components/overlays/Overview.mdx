# Overlays

Everything that floats above the panel: pop-out panels, the info bubble, menus, tooltips,
dialogs and toasts. The pop-out family (`Popout`, `PopoutButton`, `InfoCircle`) is this
package's own and shares one floating layer, `PopoutManager`, with one set of dismissal rules.
Menus, tooltips and modals are Mantine's own components, restyled by the theme; the package adds
the small pieces Mantine lacks (`ContextMenu`, `MenuCheckItem`, `TooltipShortcut`,
`ModalFooter`) and a toast (`Toast`, `ToastProvider`, `useToast`).

Two looks, as in Figma: the light popover (pop-outs, the info bubble, Mantine `Popover` and
`HoverCard`) follows the page's light or dark scheme; menus, tooltips and toasts are dark in both
schemes. Nothing animates: every overlay opens and closes in one frame.

## Reach for

- **[Popout](?path=/docs/components-overlays-popout--docs)** when settings that belong to
  something on screen need more room than the 240px column: a non-modal panel beside its
  trigger, draggable, and able to open another from inside it.
- **[PopoutButton](?path=/docs/components-overlays-popoutbutton--docs)** as the trigger of a
  pop-out: a 24px ghost icon button that stays lit while its panel is open.
- **[InfoCircle](?path=/docs/components-overlays-infocircle--docs)** when a name needs an
  explanation the reader can ask for: a circled "i" that opens a bubble on hover, focus or tap.
- **[Menu](?path=/docs/components-overlays-menu--docs)** for commands: `ContextMenu` for a
  right-click (or Shift+F10), Mantine `Menu` from a button, `MenuCheckItem` for an on/off row.
- **[Tooltip](?path=/docs/components-overlays-tooltip--docs)** for the name (and, with
  `TooltipShortcut`, the shortcut) of an icon-only control.
- **[Modal](?path=/docs/components-overlays-modal--docs)** for a short task the reader must
  finish or cancel first; end it with a `ModalFooter`.
- **[Toast](?path=/docs/components-overlays-toast--docs)** to confirm something that just
  happened, with at most one action.

Outside this group: choosing a value from a list is a `Select`
([Components/Inputs](?path=/docs/components-inputs-overview--docs)), and a button with a default
action plus a menu of alternatives is a `SplitButton`
([Components/Actions](?path=/docs/components-actions-overview--docs)).

## Start with the providers

**Put one `PopoutManager` high in your tree, above anything that opens a pop-out.** It owns the
stacking order, the layer the panels render into, and the dismissal rules they all obey. Without
one, anything that opens a pop-out throws
`usePopoutManagerContext must be used within a PopoutManager` on its first render. That includes
`CompactColorInput`, which opens its picker in a pop-out. `InfoCircle` is the one exception: it
brings a manager of its own when there is none above it.

Two more wrappers are optional and go around the whole app once: Mantine's `Tooltip.Group`, for
Figma's warm hand-off between tooltips (the theme gives it the right delays; do not nest another
inside), and `ToastProvider`, if you show toasts with `useToast`.

```tsx
import { Tooltip } from "@mantine/core";
import { Popout, PopoutButton, PopoutManager, ToastProvider, UiGlyph } from "@graphty/compact-mantine";

<PopoutManager>
    <Tooltip.Group>
        <ToastProvider>
            <aside>
                <Popout>
                    <Popout.Trigger>
                        <PopoutButton icon={<UiGlyph name="gear" />} aria-label="Display settings" />
                    </Popout.Trigger>
                    <Popout.Panel width={240} header={{ variant: "title", title: "Display settings" }}>
                        <Popout.Content>Anything at all</Popout.Content>
                    </Popout.Panel>
                </Popout>
            </aside>
        </ToastProvider>
    </Tooltip.Group>
</PopoutManager>;
```

## What the manager decides for you

- **Escape** closes the innermost open panel.
- **A click outside** closes everything.
- **One root pop-out at a time**, as in Figma: opening a panel closes its siblings, so a column
  of buttons produces one panel rather than a pile. A panel opened from inside a panel is not a
  sibling and stays open beside its parent.
- **Closing a panel closes everything opened from it.**
- **Focus returns to the trigger** when a panel closes.

Two hooks reach the manager from your own code. `usePopoutManager()` returns `closeAll()` and
`hasOpenPopouts()`, for the routes a click-outside does not cover: a keyboard shortcut, a command
palette row, a full-page sheet that replaces the page. `PopoutRegion id="..."` names a part of
your shell, and `usePopoutRegion()` reads that name from anywhere inside it, including from a
pop-out opened there. See the Popout page's `RegionsAndCloseAll` story.

## Where a panel opens

Two independent props on `Popout.Panel` decide it, which is what lets a panel meet a sidebar's
edge while still opening level with the row that was clicked.

- `anchorX` -- which edge the panel lines up with: `"panel"` (the containing `Popout.Anchor`),
  `"trigger"`, `"parent"` (the pop-out this one was opened from), or a ref of your own.
- `anchorY` -- how far down it opens, from the same set of choices.

The defaults are what most sidebars want: flush with the anchored panel's edge, level with the
trigger's own row. A pop-out opened from inside another steps out from its parent, so a nested
stack reads one level at a time. Panels follow what they are anchored to when it moves or is
resized, or when the window is; a panel the reader has dragged stays where they put it.

## Controlled panels

Pass `opened` and `onOpenChange` to drive a pop-out (or an `InfoCircle`) from your own state --
to restore it on load, open it from a menu elsewhere, or close every panel at once. Leave both
out and it keeps its own state.

```tsx
<Popout opened={settingsOpen} onOpenChange={setSettingsOpen}>
    ...
</Popout>
```

## Pop-outs, not Mantine's Popover

The theme draws Mantine's `Popover` and `HoverCard` with the same light shell, so they look
right. But a Mantine `Popover` opened alongside the pop-outs has its own dismissal rules, which is
how a page ends up with two overlays that disagree about what Escape does. Use `InfoCircle` for
an explanation bubble and `Popout` for anything larger.

Mantine's other floating components -- `Select`, `Menu`, `Tooltip` and the rest -- work normally
*inside* a pop-out panel: their dropdowns render above it and do not dismiss it.

## Accessibility notes

- Give `Popout.Trigger` a real button. It attaches the click handler and the ARIA wiring but adds
  no keyboard handling of its own, so a `div` would leave the panel unreachable from the keyboard.
- Give every icon-only trigger (`PopoutButton`, an `ActionIcon` under a tooltip) an `aria-label`.
  An icon on its own has no accessible name, and a tooltip is a description, not a name.
- Give `Popout.Panel` a `label` when it has no visible title, so the panel is announced by name.
- `ContextMenu` also opens from the keyboard (Shift+F10, the ContextMenu key), so nothing is
  reachable only by a right-click.
