Look at: Show (None, All, Selected only, Top N by a value, Members of a Set), By [Bridges] with the count, Text from [label] (any column, or the id); then the text's size, colour and backing, Place (right, below, centre), Wrap at 20 characters on 2 lines (longer names are cut with an ellipsis), Face the camera (for 3D), Fade with distance, and Avoid overlaps, whose caption says what yields.
