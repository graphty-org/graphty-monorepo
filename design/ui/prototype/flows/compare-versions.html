Compare with the previous data version

After April's transfers replaced March's under the same analysis, the analyst asks the question the swap exists for: did the ring's community really change, or is Louvain, a random algorithm, just grouping the accounts differently this time? She compares the Louvain result on April with the run kept on March and reads the answer beside the numbers that qualify it.

The screens are the weekly-return mocks, drawn from the kit's March and April data: Compare with another run... on the Louvain result and the comparison surface, which draws both months, each with its own positions and the same community colors. This flow comes after Update with new data, apply a recipe.

Not buildable yet. graphty-element does not have these today, and the app must not compute any of them: data versions with the runs kept on each; Compare with another run... over two runs of one result; a partition-similarity measure (AMI); the seeded re-runs that measure how firmly each group holds; one drawing budget and one color scale across both sides.

Step by step

1 Compare with another run...

On the Louvain row, Compare with another run... lists the run on March data first, because Update with new data kept it for this question; then other partitions on April. Two steps from the result. Screen

Usage data: off. graphty asked once, at first use, and she did not say yes, so it does not ask again and nothing about this comparison is sent. Preferences > Usage data changes that.

2 The comparison surface

March and April side by side, with one selection matched by account id and one color per community on both sides. Above them: AMI 0.45 over the 2,961 accounts in both months; 39 only in March and 132 only in April, left out of that number; a re-run on the same March data agrees at 0.76. Screen

3 The group she cares about

Sorted by change, Community 33 grew from 22 to 32 accounts, 7 of them new in April, and it holds together in all 5 re-runs on April. The groupings as a whole differ more than two runs on the same data do, and this group is not one run's accident. Save comparison keeps it; Done keeps nothing. Screen

Errors and ways out

No earlier run to compare

If March's run was squashed out of history, the picker's Earlier runs group says none is kept. Not designed yet: whether it also offers re-running Louvain on the March version.

Done

The surface closes, nothing is kept, and focus returns to the Louvain row's Compare with another run.... Esc never leaves: it closes the innermost popover or menu, and with none open it moves focus to Done, because leaving discards the pairing.

Low agreement between re-runs

If re-runs on the same data agree little, the comparison says so before the statistic can be read as change: the grouping itself is unstable, and resolution is the parameter to explore.

What she can say after"The April grouping differs from March's more than two runs on the same data do (AMI 0.45 against 0.76). Community 33 grew from 22 to 32 accounts, 7 of them new, and holds together in every re-run."
Where the numbers come fromAMI covers only the 2,961 accounts present in both months. The baseline is a re-run of Louvain on March with another seed. Five seeded runs are cheap on 3,000 accounts, so they run with every Louvain result and nobody starts them; they are kept in the result's Details. A partition-level score says nothing about one group, so the per-group line is its own measure: how often the group's members stay together across the re-runs.

Where this flow departs from the framework

  • The trust checks name their basis: AMI over the accounts in both months, and the baseline as a re-run on the same data. The framework's example claim read "larger than a re-run on the same data produces" for one group; a partition-level score cannot say that of one group, so the claim is split into the partition's and the group's.
  • A fourth trust check, per group: how firmly each group holds across the re-runs.
