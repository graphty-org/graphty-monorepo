Sets and paths between two accounts

How a fraud analyst keeps a group of accounts as a set, combines two sets, asks how two accounts are connected, sees every equally short chain of transfers at once, takes the transfers out as rows with their amounts and dates, and checks what the path's length counted before believing it; and how she follows what an account sent out, hop by hop, inside a date window, with every transfer that goes back in time marked. Every path and edge table on this page shows the transfer's time right after its two accounts. The data is the kit's March transfers sample: 3,000 accounts, 9,113 directed transfers and one 14-account mule ring. Every account, count and amount on this page is written into kit/fixtures.json (transactions.setsAndPaths, and for the dated neighbors scenarios.setsAndPathsTrace, written by flows/sets-and-paths-numbers.mjs).

The case

Sarah, a fraud analyst, has the 14 flagged accounts on screen. Every one of them paid between $9,400 and $9,900 to the same merchant, ACC-893168. She wants three answers: which other risky accounts paid that merchant; how an ordinary business, ACC-271813, is connected to the ring's highest-risk account, ACC-233575; and, the one her reviewer asks first, where money went out of ACC-233575 in the two weeks after the chain started, and whether it could have happened in that order.

What she can say today, with the steps graphty-element can already do:

"16 accounts with riskScore 70 or more paid ACC-893168: the 14 flagged ones and two new leads, ACC-982215 (riskScore 77, paid $259.74) and ACC-437087 (75, $191.58). Kept as a frozen set. ACC-271813 reaches ACC-233575 in 3 transfers, starting with a $3,530.28 payment on 4 March into the ring, counting transfers, not dollars. ACC-233575's transfers out, and the next accounts' transfers out, are in the attached table with their times; I checked the order by eye."

What she can add once the dashed steps below exist in graphty-element:

"...kept as a rule set that follows both lists as they change. There are 2 equal paths, and both go through ACC-946224, a flagged ring account. Every hop runs forward in time, from 4 March to 9 March, and the search was told to follow time order. The search ran on the full graph and left the filtered graph only at its start, ACC-271813. The five transfers, in path order with amounts and dates, are attached as a CSV. Out of ACC-233575, Mar 4 to 17: 3 transfers, $28,559.99, to 3 accounts; then 7 transfers out of them after the one that reached them, $56,018.09, to 4 more accounts, within 140 hours. 2 transfers are earlier than the hop before and are not counted."

How to read the diagrams

Blue shapes open the screen mock of that step. Gray-filled shapes are failures. A dashed magenta outline is a step graphty-element cannot do yet; it is drawn where it belongs, never routed around. Each table's last column names the graphty-element call behind the step, or quotes the missing piece it waits for.

1. Keep and combine sets

Two ways to keep accounts: a frozen set keeps exactly the accounts selected now; a rule set keeps a rule and follows the data. Intersecting two sets makes a rule set that names both, so it changes when either changes.

Errors and cancellations

  • Create set with nothing selected: unavailable, with the reason as its tooltip (graphty.command.disabledReason): "Select nodes or edges".
  • Esc while naming: the set is still made, with its automatic name "Set 1"; the undo entry reads "Create set Set 1". Naming later is its own entry, "Rename set Set 1 to Paid ACC-893168".
  • A name already in use: the rename is reverted and closed, and the field error reads "Set names must be unique" (graphty.name.duplicate, spoken assertively, repeated as a notice). A new set keeps its automatic name.
  • Only one set row focused: the focus bar is not shown; in the row's context menu Union, Subtract, Intersect and Exclude are disabled with the reason "Focus two or more sets".
  • No overlap: Intersect still makes the set, with 0 members, so the answer "these two lists share no one" is on record and undoable. Its row reads 0.
  • A rule set whose members later drop to 0 (the scores change, or an operand is edited): the row stays and reads 0; nothing is deleted. Freeze is unavailable with the reason "Has no members".
  • An operand deleted later: the intersection is detached, not deleted; the notice "1 set detached" offers Undo (graphty.delete.detached).

Keyboard

Tab reaches Sets and paths as one stop. Up and Down move; Shift+Up and Shift+Down extend the focus to the next row. With two or more rows focused, the focus bar is the next Tab stop, its buttons stepped with Left and Right; Shift+F10 opens the same operations as a context menu. The new row is selected and takes keyboard focus, and the screen reader hears "High risk and Paid ACC-893168, rule set, 16".

2. Paths between two accounts

Two commands answer "how are these two connected?". Path to..., on the inspector of one selected account and in Quick actions, starts a pick mode: a prompt over the canvas reads "Pick the end node: click, or find it by name (Ctrl+K). Esc cancels." Picking the end runs the search at once when there is nothing to ask first. Paths between..., on the inspector of two selected accounts and behind the Path tool, opens the run's small form, From and To. Both draw every equally short route at once and say which account the routes go through ("2 equal paths; both go through ACC-946224"), with the routes listed on request. Direction follows the data: on directed transfers the search follows the money. Each hop shows its date when the transfers have a date column, the hops stay in path order, and the state line says whether they run forward in time. A hop earlier than the hop before is drawn dashed with a clock mark, and its line and its table row say "earlier than the hop before". Follow time order, a checkbox in the form whenever the edges have a date column, finds only routes in which every hop is later than the one before (proposed to graphty-element). By default the search counts transfers; Weight by reads an edge column only once that column's meaning is answered.

Errors and cancellations

  • Esc in pick mode: the prompt goes, nothing runs and nothing is recorded, and focus returns to the Path to... button that started it. Started from Quick actions, focus returns to where it was before Ctrl+K.
  • Picking the start again, an edge, or empty canvas: pick mode stays on and the prompt reads "Pick a node other than ACC-271813." or stays as it was. Only a node other than the start ends it.
  • Both ends inside the filter (the usual case): picking the end runs the search at once with the form's defaults, and the state line states them. The form's settings are one click away on the result, Edit options.
  • Path to... with nothing selected: the command is unavailable, with the reason "Select the start node" (graphty.command.disabledReason). With two accounts selected, Paths between... is offered instead.
  • Esc in the form closes it. Nothing runs and nothing is recorded; focus returns to the control that opened it (Path to..., when the form opened after a pick).
  • From and To the same account: Run is unavailable with the reason "Choose a To other than From".
  • An end outside the filtered graph (step 2): the form names it and points to Scope, its one scope control; Run waits until Scope reads Full graph.
  • Don't use amount: a real answer. The run counts transfers, and amount reads "amount, not used" wherever it is offered.
  • amount answered as a capacity: paths do not read a capacity. Run refuses in the same shape: "Can't weight paths by amount: amount is set up as a capacity. Max flow reads it.", with Count transfers, which sets Weight by to none.
  • No path along the direction of transfers: reversed, from ACC-233575 to ACC-271813, there is none. The form stays open and reads "No path along transfers; one exists ignoring direction, 3 hops" (graphty.query.noPath) with Ignore direction, which sets Direction and runs again as "Paths from ACC-233575 to ACC-271813, ignoring direction".   • No date column on the edges: no dates beside the hops and no time-order line; nothing is claimed about time.
  • No path inside the filtered graph: "No path in filtered graph", with Search full graph, which sets Scope and runs again as one step. Offered only on a result; before a run the Scope field is the only way to change the scope.
  • Different components: "No path: different components", with no action, because none would help.
  • A search that runs long: the state line shows "Running Shortest path" with progress and Cancel (graphty.run.running); Cancel leaves no undo entry and the form stays open.
  • Very many equal routes: the line counts them in full ("4,096 equal paths; 1,024 go through" the account most routes share) and the list shows them 50 at a time; what is drawn is the accounts and transfers on them, which cannot outgrow the graph.
  • A $0 transfer read as a stronger link becomes no connection at all; the run record counts those transfers. The March sample has none (its smallest transfer is $5.00).
  • Not kept: the query stays under its result in Results and can be re-run; nothing appears in Sets and paths.

5. Neighbors out of one account, in a date window

A path needs both ends. "Where did the money go from here?" has only one, so it is asked with Neighbors, the one name for the neighbors action everywhere (inspector, context menu, Quick actions, the filter chip's step names). Its menu gains two things: a Direction (In, Out or Both; on directed data the default follows the data, Out) and an optional Window on any date column of the edges ("Any time" by default). Nothing in it is about money: a window on timestamp works the same on emails, shipments or citations. The result is an ordinary filter step with one computed summary line, and every hop that goes back in time is marked, on the canvas and in its row.

Errors and cancellations

  • Esc in the menu: it closes; nothing changes and focus returns to the Neighbors button.
  • No date column on the edges: the Window row is not shown, no hop is dashed, and the summary counts every transfer without claiming anything about time.
  • Two date columns: the Window row names the column it reads, with a picker; it never guesses silently.
  • A window with nothing in it: each hop's size reads 0 and Filter to neighbors is unavailable with the reason "No transfers out of ACC-233575 between Mar 4 and Mar 17"; Widen is the row's link.
  • Direction Both: no hop has a before and after in the money's direction, so nothing is dashed, and the summary counts transfers only.
  • Direction In: the hops are read in the money's direction, towards the account, so "earlier than the hop before" keeps its one meaning: a transfer that happened before the one that passed the money on towards the account.
  • Keep top N, then neighbors: past the drawing limit, the ranking step is "Keep top N" (N set by the reader) and neighbors are a second, separate step, "Add their neighbors". The two are never one step, so either can be undone alone.
  • Undo: Ctrl+Z unticks the filter step, as every filter step; its summary line goes with it.

Keyboard and focus

The Neighbors caret is the Tab stop after the Neighbors button; Enter or Down opens the menu. Up and Down move through Direction, the hop counts and Window; Left and Right change Direction; Enter on Window opens the two date fields. Each hop's size is read with it ("2 hops, 10 accounts"). Filter to neighbors returns focus to the Neighbors button; the step's summary line is read after its name, and a dashed hop is read with its account ("ACC-593226, earlier than the hop before").
