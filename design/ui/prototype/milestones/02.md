# Milestone 2: the first user study, and the mocks revised after it

Fifteen simulated personas, built from our persona files and real analysts' forum posts and
videos, tried 18 tasks on the mocks: 71 sessions and 4 focus groups. 63 of 71 finished
the task, but only 1 did so without a wrong turn or a hint, and the average ease was 3.65 of 7.
Participants are simulated: treat findings as leads to confirm with real people.

The worst problems: a count that does not say what it was counted over reads as a contradiction.
Nobody could say how sure a ranking is. Community detection has no finished result and no way to
compare groups. A print figure cannot be checked in gray or saved as a vector file. The weekly
refresh stacks both weeks instead of replacing the old one. An investigation cannot start from an
alert: nobody finished the flagged-account task. The easiest tasks were where graphty already
beats other tools: sharing a setup without the data, and comparing two rankings.

About half the friction came from the mocks themselves (the wrong dataset after Load, dead
controls, hand-typed numbers that disagreed); those get fixed before round 2. About 20 pages
were revised. Two undo designs and two "where is my data" designs go head to head in round 2.
Your two answers are in: the walk uses Shift+Arrow; messages publish as a key plus values.
