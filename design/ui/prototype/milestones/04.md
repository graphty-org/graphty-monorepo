# Milestone 4: the third user study, your navigation review, and the mocks revised after both

Nineteen simulated personas ran 45 tasks (30 repeated, 15 new): 136 sessions and 4 focus groups. 127 of 136 finished, 16 without a wrong turn or a hint (round 2: 8 of 105), and average ease rose from 4.20 to 4.35 of 7. Counting five tasks the screens could only partly support as partial, 83% fully met the task. Participants are simulated: compare rounds with each other, not with published norms.

What worked: undo. Getting back rose from 3.4 to 4.8 in ease. What failed worst, each now redrawn but not yet re-tested: the edge weight was described five ways and "no weight" read as lost data, so every page now uses one wording; money paths had no dates and could not be traced forward, so every transfer is dated and hops back in time are marked; the Print look made the biggest decreases the palest, so up and down now stay apart in gray; a recipe looked finished before your own table was in; past the drawing limit a row was a dead end.

Your navigation review is now drawn: the rail lists what a project owns (Graph, Data, Notes, the Assistant), styles and results move to the right panel, the avatar is gone, and data coming in, its versions and its exports share a new Data panel. None of it is tested yet. Three results were spoiled by mock errors and must be re-run. The design has not converged.
