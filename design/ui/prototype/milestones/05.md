# Milestone 5: the fifth user study, and what lands before the sixth

The round met none of its five bars. The main reason: most decisions from round 4 were never drawn on the screens before the sessions ran (among them weighted degree, a weight chosen per run, the cleared-selection notice and style files folded into recipes). So the sessions re-tested the old design, and seven repeated tasks and two new ones do not count. Only the text tree test tested the decided design. Participants are simulated: compare rounds with each other, not with published norms.

What the numbers say:

- Tree test (16 personas x 16 tasks): 90% correct, 67% direct; 11 of 16 tasks pass. Picture export and next month's file went to 100% direct (round 4: 13% and 38%).
- First click (16 x 14): 72%; 7 of 14 pass. Picture export rose from 2 of 16 to 14 of 16.
- Sessions: 135 over 26 tasks; 94% finished, mean ease 4.10 of 7.
- A team's colors file fails: 12 of 16 went to the style stack's "+", not to Recipes. Getting back a cleared selection: every participant pressed Ctrl+Z first; nobody picked the notice.
- Where results live: only 4 of 16 reached a run's settings directly, so under the rule fixed before the round, Results move to the rail.

What changed: Results is a rail place again. Two feedback-log items marked done are not: the avatar and top-right Export are still on 8 pages, and the participant view on the navigation page is blank. Both are on the list of fixes that must land, checked, before round 6. Round 6 tests Results on the rail (against a "Runs" label), Ctrl+Z against the drawn notice, "Use these styles" from the style stack's "+", and a weight chosen per run with nothing preselected.

One loss: a filing error overwrote most of round 4's raw answers and sessions, which were not in git. Round 4's findings page and tree-test summary survive.
