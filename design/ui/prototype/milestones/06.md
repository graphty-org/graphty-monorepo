# Milestone 7: the sixth user study, and what round 7 re-tests

Round 6 did not pass: it met one of its five bars, ease. The check that the pages were ready failed, and the sessions ran anyway. Still on the pages then: the Note tool, "on no bridge", a bare "Re-run", no Weighted degree, and a task check that checked 0 pages yet passed. Participants are simulated: a failed task is a strong signal, a passed one weak until real people confirm it.

What the numbers say:

- Tree test, 16 personas: 93% correct, 66% direct; 9 of 16 tasks pass. An opened run's Top nodes and a path's Members pulled two tasks to 44% and 25% direct.
- First click: 69%; 6 of 14 pass. Cheapest route: 1 of 16. The Loaded line's "amount not used yet. Change..." drew 15 of 16 clicks away from the weight chosen per run, and the path's weight editor opens filled with the opposite of cheapest.
- Sessions: 211, 94% finished, 24 confirmed major design findings (mock defects excluded). Ease on the 25 repeated tasks rose from 4.13 to 4.78 of 7, but nine summaries misreported ease, so it is no headline; transcript values are used.
- Stacked degree filters silently give 40 where the answer is 41.
