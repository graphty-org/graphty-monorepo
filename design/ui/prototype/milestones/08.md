# Milestone 9: the eighth user study, tiered around a first-time user

Round 8 put simulated personas through the clickable refined B skeleton in 264 sessions. The round met none of its four bars, but it moved a long way: 83% of sessions succeeded or succeeded with difficulty (90% without four tasks a skeleton defect decided), and mean ease rose from 3.54 to 4.28 of 7 (bar 5.5).

- Tier 1 (a first-time user's core path from an empty app): 75% overall, 86% without the whole-first-session task. Three tasks fail: the whole first session (0 of 21), names on every node (5 of 12) and naming one character's neighbors (0 of 12). Every tier 2 task passes (96%).
- Tree test: 99.7% correct, 68% direct; 8 of 15 tasks reach 70% direct. Since round 7: picture 19% to 90%, a colleague's style file 13% to 95%, layout 6% to 86%; select by a rule stays at 10%.
- First click: 87%; export the data 10%, neighbors 57%, rearrange 62%.

Four severity-4 problems are open:

- The Label "+" offers "Show labels", which people pick and which changes nothing visible.
- No screen names a node's neighbors.
- The list's search box cannot find a node, an id or a value.
- "Show the 32 rows" lists only the 3 unmatched rows in the preview, and they cannot be exported.

Four tasks were decided by skeleton defects (among them a run that never finishes and a size choice never applied); those get wired first.

Round 9 changes all go to tier 1: skeleton wiring, then labels, search, neighbors, the bare sample and export. Round 9 uses fresh personas and reports every number with and without skeleton defects. The feedback log gets a round 8 section answering your 2026-10-02 priorities note and the ten unlogged items from your first refined B review.
