After one Undo

One press reverses one step: Filter out group 8 is unticked, not removed, so group 8 is back on the canvas and the chip reads 40 of 77 nodes · 2 of 3 steps. One line above the toolbar names what was reversed, "Undone: Filter out group 8", with Show in steps, which opens the list at that row. It shows on every Undo and Redo, with the left panel open or closed, is announced politely and never takes focus. Redo, or ticking the row again, puts the step back.

After a second Undo: nothing is lost

A second press reverses the step before it, Filter to degree >= 5, the wrong middle step. It is unticked too, and both rows stay in the steps list. The same line now names both, newest first, "Undone: Filter out group 8 and Filter to degree >= 5", so the first press is not lost under the second; Show in steps opens the list at group 8's row, where one Space ticks the good step back on. Redo does the same until the next change; after that the checkbox still does.
