The load step: one dialog for Open, Add data, Join, Update with new data and Re-map columns. Shell: Figma's large two-column modal (Manage libraries), no dimming behind it; wider than Figma's 760 because the sample needs four to five columns at a readable width. interface-templates.md 20, 20a. compact-mantine Modal.
Format first, detected by graphty-element and changeable. Ends are type slots: pick a column or type its name. They and Direction are the only place the ends are set, so the endpoint columns' Role reads "source" and "target" as plain text. 20a. FieldRow, StyleSelect, ComboInput x2.
A numeric edge column loads as a plain attribute: its Role starts at None and the Role list offers no Weight. Nothing about weight is asked here and nothing about it can hold Load back. What a higher amount means is asked by the first run that uses it, or from the weight sentence in Statistics. framework-changes.md, "Load step: the weight's meaning is asked at the first weighted run". StyleSelect (role).
The weight sentence: one of three, the same words on every screen ("Weight: {column}, not used yet", "Weight: {column}, used as similarity", "Weight: no numeric edge column"; under a Weight label the label is the prefix). The state comes from graphty-element, not the app. Here without its Change... link, which lives in Statistics. framework-changes.md, "Load step: the weight sentence, the plain wording of pairs and unconnected nodes". DataRow.
No Issues section: a section with no command and nothing in it is left out of a dialog, as Figma leaves out property sections that do not apply. Whether an absent section reads as "nothing wrong" is a study question. content-design.md, Empty surface (proposed wording change).
timestamp is recognized as a date, not read as text: its kind is Date and time with the zone the file writes (Z, so UTC), and the sample header gives its range. A date type is what makes a range filter on it honest (a time window is an ordinary filter step) and lets a path say when its hops are not in time order. If the guess is wrong, Read as changes it here. framework-changes.md, "graphty-element: dates at load, and time order on paths" (waits on graphty-element). StyleSelect.
