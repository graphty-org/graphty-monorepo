Frame past the drawing limit

A patent citation sample, 124,318 patents and 1,480,221 citations, opens in graphty. That is more than this browser draws at once (50,000 nodes), so the canvas draws nothing and says so in its middle, with the one way forward. The graph's Statistics, with the in-degree distribution a first look at a drawing would give, and the table stand in for the picture. The isolate and component counts open their rows in the table. The table lists every patent, read a page at a time in the order it is sorted by, and Keep top rows... on the table keeps its first rows as a plain filter step: the analyst types 200, sees where the cut falls and that the 200 will draw, and presses Keep these 200 rows (states 2b and 2c). Narrow the graph... opens the filter chip's list of steps, which offers the same step and the neighbors of one node. The analyst can write a rule instead, see whether its result will draw before committing it, and the canvas draws the 612 patents that are left. The last state keeps the 3 most cited and then adds their neighbors, as two separate steps. The unsupported-browser case (no WebGPU) is the Layout row under state 4: the work runs on the CPU and the row says so plainly, not as an error. Each state is the whole app at 1440 by 900; every number comes from the kit's fixtures. The magenta markers and the notes under each frame cite the design framework and name the compact-mantine component; they are not product UI.
