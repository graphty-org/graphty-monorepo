# Session: leave yourself a reason for the accounts you kept -- Sarah, fraud investigator

Participant: Sarah, level-2 financial crime investigator at a mid-size bank (simulated; composite
persona in study/personas/fraud-analyst.md). Mode: not mandated -- she is judging whether this
beats her current habit.

Task as given by the moderator, and nothing more: "Leave yourself what you would need next week
to remember why you kept these accounts."

Material: the Notes panel mock (nine states: empty, hover, chosen, row menu, find, no hits, out
of date, filtered, view only) and the Inspector mock, including its money-transfer state
("Transfers, March 2026", 3,000 accounts, a kept group called "Mule ring"). Read from the
rendered pictures; the page source was read only to see what a control does when pressed.

Result: finished with difficulty. SEQ 4 of 7.

## Transcript (think-aloud)

**1. First screen -- the Notes panel, empty.**

> "Okay. 'Human protein interactions.' That's not my data. TP53, MYC... I'll pretend these are
> accounts. Left side says 'No notes yet. Add a note about the selection.' Fine. Where's the
> button? There's no button. It's a sentence. I'd click on the sentence and nothing happens,
> I'd bet."

She scans the left panel for a plus sign or an "Add" button. There is none in the empty state.
Her eye drifts to the right column and finds "Notes" with a "+" next to it.

> "There. Right side, 'Notes' with a plus. So I add a note on the right and read it on the left?
> Okay. Two places for one thing, but okay."

She skims the grey line "Notes are saved in the project and travel in project files and
findings reports" and does not read it to the end.

> "Saved where? 'The project.' Is that on my machine or somewhere else? Doesn't say. If this
> goes to a server I need to know before I type a customer name into it."

**2. Finding "these accounts".**

The moderator lets her move to the transfers picture in the Inspector mock. She sees "Sets and
paths -- Mule ring, fixed, 14" on the left.

> "Mule ring, 14. That's the thing I kept. 'Fixed' -- I'm guessing that means I picked them by
> hand, not some rule. Good, that's what I'd want. I click it."

The render of that exact state for the transfer data is not in the mock; the protein version
("DNA repair, 30 nodes, Rule set") stands in. She reads its right column top to bottom.

> "Rule, From, Layout, Statistics, Members by degree, Appearance, Used by... Where's Notes? In
> this picture there's no Notes on the set at all. I'd be scrolling looking for it."

In the newer picture of the 7,495-selection state she does find "Notes -- + Add a note" near
the bottom of the right column, above Export.

> "Okay, so it's at the bottom, after the colours. That's the last thing I'd look at and the
> first thing I need. Why is 'why I kept these' below 'what colour are they'?"

**3. Writing the note -- what the note carries.**

She turns to the "Chosen" state of the Notes panel, where a note is open on a kept group.
It reads "About TP53 neighborhood, 33 proteins", "2 hours ago", the text, then "CITES
Betweenness, full graph" and "QUOTES TP53 betweenness 0.114".

> "Now this I like. It says what it's about, how many, and it quoted the number at the time.
> If it said 'About Mule ring, 14 accounts' and quoted 'ACC-393859 received 9,800 on the 12th'
> -- that's my note. That's exactly the bit I lose by next week: what the number WAS when I
> made the call."

> "But 'Cites betweenness'? I don't know what betweenness is. If mine said 'Cites: transfers
> over 9,000, March' fine. If it says an algorithm name I'll ignore it."

> "What I'd actually write: 'Kept 14. Shared device ID with the escalated account, funds in
> and out same day, 3 hops to the cash-out at ACC-whatever. Excluded the 312 at Elm Street --
> apartment building.' Does the note let me say which ones I THREW OUT and why? Because that's
> half of it. The examiner asks why you didn't include the others."

Nothing on screen answers that. A note is about one thing: the group, one account, or the
selection.

**4. One reason per account?**

> "Fourteen accounts. They're not all in for the same reason. Three are the device, the rest
> are money flow. Do I write fourteen notes? One note for the ring and cram it all in? The
> picture has a little '1' bubble on the account with a note -- fine, that's how I'd spot
> them on the chart next week. But fourteen bubbles, no."

She settles on one note on the ring, with the per-account reasons typed as lines in the body.

**5. Next week -- will it still be right?**

She looks at the "Out of date" state: "Earlier run -- Use current" and "Detached -- Restore
set".

> "'Earlier run, use current.' So if the data changed under me it tells me. Good, that matters.
> But 'Use current' -- does that rewrite my note's numbers? Because I don't want my note
> changed. My note is what I saw on the day. I'd leave that alone and be nervous about it."

> "'Detached, Restore set.' Somebody deleted my ring? Restore -- okay. Who deleted it? It
> doesn't say."

**6. Dates and who.**

> "'2h', '1d', 'Sep 24'. For the case file I want the date and time, and who wrote it. It says
> 'Dr. Chen' in one picture, so there's an author. And if I edit it, is the old version kept?
> 'Edit note', 'Delete note' -- delete with no confirm and no history? My QA reviewer will ask
> what the note said before."

**7. Getting it out.**

> "And the note has to end up in my case file. Is there 'copy note' or 'export notes'? I see
> Export at the bottom of the right side, but that looks like pictures. If I have to retype
> this into the case system, I wrote it twice."

**8. Search.** She looks at "Find in notes" with "Betweenness" highlighted in two rows.

> "Search is good. Next week I'd type the account number. As long as it finds the account
> number inside the note text, fine."

## After the task

**Would she use it instead of her current tool?**

> "Right now I keep this in a Word doc next to the case and a comment column in the Excel. If
> this note travelled into whatever I export for the reviewer, with real dates and who wrote

> record, not this."

## Problems observed

1. Empty Notes panel tells her to "Add a note about the selection" but gives no control to do
   it; the add control is in the other column. (severity 2)
2. In the kept-group inspector the Notes section is last, below colours and statistics; in the
   older picture of a kept group it does not appear at all. (severity 2)
3. No way to record what was excluded and why, or to give different reasons for different
   members of one kept group. (severity 3)
4. "Cites" shows an algorithm name she cannot read; she wants the evidence named in case
   terms (transfers, amounts, dates). (severity 2)
5. Relative times ("2h", "1d") instead of a date and time; no visible edit history; delete has
   no confirmation. Audit trail concerns. (severity 3)
6. "Use current" on an out-of-date note: unclear whether it rewrites the note's quoted numbers.
   She wants the original kept. (severity 2)
7. Where notes are stored is unclear ("saved in the project"); she hesitates to type customer
   details until she knows nothing leaves the machine. (severity 2)
8. No visible way to copy or export notes into the case file. (severity 3)
9. The mocks use protein data; she had to translate every label to accounts. (severity 1)

## What worked

- A note is tied to the kept group and names it with its size ("About ..., 33 proteins").
- The note quotes the value as it was when written.
- Out-of-date and detached warnings on the note itself.
- The numbered bubble on the chart marks which account carries a note.
- Search inside notes.
