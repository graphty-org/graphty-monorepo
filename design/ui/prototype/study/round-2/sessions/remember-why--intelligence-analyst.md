# Session: "Leave yourself what you would need next week to remember why you kept these accounts"

Participant: Marcus, criminal intelligence analyst at a state fusion center (i2 Analyst's Notebook
and Excel every day). Simulated participant, round 2.

Screens used: the Notes panel mock (its nine states) and the Inspector mock (its eleven states and
the right-column crops), as static renders at 1440 by 900. Where a render shows a control, the
page source was read only to learn what clicking it would open, as a clickable prototype would.

Moderator's task, word for word: "Leave yourself what you would need next week to remember why you
kept these accounts."

## Think-aloud transcript

**1. First screen: the Notes panel, empty.**

> "Okay. 'Human protein interactions.' That's not my data. Proteins. Fine, pretend. Left side says
> 'No notes yet. Add a note about the selection.' So I have to select something first. Got it.
> 'Notes are saved in the project and travel in project files and findings reports.' Travel. Travel
> where? That's the first word I don't like. Over on the far left it says 'Assistant -- Off.
> Nothing is sent.' Good, that's the thing I'd want IT to see. But 'travel' and 'nothing is sent'
> are sitting ten inches apart and they don't obviously agree. If I write 'kept these because the
> CI says so' in a note, is that in a file somebody can email? I need that answered in one line."

He looks for accounts. There are none on this screen. He looks at the right column: Graph,
Statistics, Notes with a plus, Export.

> "There's a plus next to Notes over here too. So two places to start a note. Which one? I don't
> have anything selected, so if I press that plus, it's a note about... the whole chart? Probably.
> Not what I want. I want it on the accounts."

**2. Looking for accounts.** He flips through the Inspector states. Everything is proteins until
the last full screen: "Transfers, March 2026", 3,000 accounts, a set on the left called "Mule
ring, fixed, 14".

> "There. Mule ring, fourteen. That's my 'these accounts'. 'Fixed' -- I'm guessing that means I
> picked them by hand and it won't reshuffle on me. That's what I'd want. If 'fixed' means
> something else nobody told me."

The render he is looking at has 7,495 things selected -- a big outline around a grey blob.

> "Seven thousand four hundred ninety-five selected. That's not fourteen. That's somebody's
> two-hop sweep off a merchant. Right side: Statistics, Attributes, Memberships 'Mule ring, holds
> 13 of 7,495', Appearance, Export. Where's Notes? It's not there. On every protein screen there's
> a Notes section with 'Add a note'. Here, with accounts, I don't see it between Appearance and
> Export. Maybe it's below. Maybe it isn't. On this screen I'd have gone looking for a right-click."

He decides the sensible move is to click the Mule ring row itself, since that is the thing he
kept. There is no render of the Mule ring selected, so he uses the nearest one: a set selected in
the protein data ("DNA repair, 30 nodes, Rule set").

> "Okay, so if I click the set on the left, the right side becomes the set. Name, '30 nodes, Rule
> set', Rule, From, Layout, Statistics, Members by degree, Appearance, Used by... then it runs off
> the bottom. I have to scroll to find Notes. On a 1080 monitor at 125 percent that's going to be
> even further down. Notes is the thing I actually care about for this job and it's under
> 'Appearance'. Colors come before why I kept them. Backwards for me."

(From the page source: the set's Notes section with "Add a note" sits below Appearance and above
"Used by"; it is not in the visible 900 pixels.)

**3. Starting the note.**

> "Scroll down, 'Notes -- Add a note.' Click."

(The source says this opens a Note editor beside the selection. Neither mock draws that editor,
so from here he is reading the finished notes, not writing one.)

> "And now I don't get to see what I'm typing into. So I'll tell you what I need in it, and then
> I'll look at what a finished note looks like over in the panel and see if it could hold that.
> I need: why each one's in -- 'received from the two flagged payroll accounts inside 48 hours,
> moved it out same day'. Where that came from -- the bank return, subpoena number, page. How
> good it is -- source reliability, information reliability, the grade. Date. And my initials so
> when Tanya picks this up she knows it was me."

**4. Reading the filled Notes panel.**

> "Okay, these are real notes. 'About TP53 neighborhood, 33 proteins, 2h.' The note says who it's
> about at the top. Good -- that's like i2 where the note hangs off the entity. 'Cites
> Betweenness, full graph.' So it can cite an analysis run. Can it cite a record? A bank
> statement? I don't see anything like that. 'Cites' is pointing at the tool's own math, not at my
> evidence."
>
> "Times say '2h', '1d', 'Sep 24'. For a case file I want the actual date and time on every one,
> not '2h'. Two hours from when? And no name on any of them. There's an 'A' in a circle up top --
> that's me I guess. When it's handed off, whose note is whose?"
>
> "The little '1' flags on the chart next to CDK1 and TP53 -- that's a note pinned on the thing.
> I like that. That's the closest this gets to what I do in i2 with a label on the chart. If I
> print this, do those flags print? Do the notes print? 'Findings reports' -- maybe."

**5. The chosen note.** In the "Chosen" state a card opens over the chart: "Note. About TP53
neighborhood, 33 proteins. 2 hours ago. [text]. CITES Betweenness full graph. QUOTES TP53
betweenness 0.114."

> "Quotes. So it grabbed the number and put it in a box. That's actually good -- if the number
> changes later I'd want to know it was 0.114 when I wrote it. The right side lit up the note
> under the set too, 'Notes 1'. So the note lives on the group. Fine. That's where I'd put 'why I
> kept these'."

**6. The row menu.** Hover a note, three dots: "Edit note", "Delete note".

> "Edit and delete. That's it. If I edit it, is the old version gone? In my world you don't
> quietly change the reason you gave last week. I'd want the old wording kept, or at least 'edited
> Sep 30 by MR'. And delete with no 'are you sure' shown -- I'd want a way back."

**7. The out-of-date state.** Notes now carry yellow marks: "Earlier run -- Use current", and one
"Detached -- Restore set" on "About DNA repair, 30 proteins".

> "Now this scares me. 'Detached.' Detached from what? The note's about a set of thirty. So the set
> changed, or went away, and my note is floating. 'Restore set' -- does that put back the thirty
> people I had when I wrote it, or does it re-run whatever rule made it? Those are two very
> different things in court. And 'Use current' on the other one -- if I press that, does my note
> now quote a different number than the one I wrote down? I would not press either of these
> buttons without somebody telling me what happens. I'd leave it yellow and write the old numbers
> in the text myself."
>
> "Upside: it told me something changed. i2 wouldn't have. I'll give it that."
>
> "This is exactly why I want 'fixed' on my Mule ring. If fourteen accounts is a rule, next week
> it could be twelve and my note is about accounts that aren't in it anymore."

**8. Find in notes.**

> "'Find in notes' box at the top. Good. Next week I type the account number and it finds the note.
> Does it search every case, or just this one file? Because the real problem is next year, a
> different case, same account shows up. If it only searches this project, that's the old i2
> folder problem again."

**9. Wrapping up.**

> "So could I do the job? Yeah, I think so: click the Mule ring, scroll down past the colors,
> 'Add a note', type the reason, and it hangs a little flag on the group and shows up in the list.
> What I can't tell is whether the note keeps the actual fourteen account numbers with it, and
> there's nowhere I can see for source and grade. So I'd end up typing 'Source: BoA return
> 24-117, p. 3, B2' into free text, which is what I do in Word now."

## After the task

**Single Ease Question: 4 of 7.**

> "Middle. Finding where to put the note wasn't bad once I clicked the group. Scrolling past the
> colors to get to it was annoying, and on the big selection I couldn't find Notes at all. What
> dropped it was not knowing what the note actually holds -- the fourteen accounts, the source,
> the grade -- and those yellow 'Detached' and 'Use current' buttons that could change my record."

**Would you use this instead of your current tool?**

> "For keeping notes on a case, not yet. Right now I keep the 'why' in a Word doc next to the i2
> chart, with the source and grade in a column. This is better in one way -- the note sits on the
> group and flags it on the chart, and it tells me when the numbers under it changed. That's real.
> But I can't hand a prosecutor a note that says '2h', has no author, no source field, no grade,
> and can be edited with no history. Give me a date, my initials, a source and grade line, a
> history on edits, and a promise that the fourteen account numbers are frozen in the note, and
> I'd put my 'why' here instead of in Word. And show me a screen with accounts and phones on it,
> not proteins, before you ask me again."

## Problems observed

1. **No place for source and grade** (severity 3). Notes are free text; "Cites" only points at
   analysis runs, never at a record. He would type source and grade as prose.
2. **Relative times and no author on notes** (severity 3). "2h", "1d" and no name make the note
   unusable in a case file or a hand-off.
3. **"Detached -- Restore set" and "Earlier run -- Use current" are not explained** (severity 3).
   He would not press either; he cannot tell whether they restore the original members or re-run
   a rule, or whether they change what the note quotes.
4. **Unclear whether a note on a set keeps the members it was written about** (severity 3).
   Nothing shows the fourteen account ids frozen into the note.
5. **Notes sits below Appearance and off the bottom of the set inspector** (severity 2). He had to
   scroll past colors and sizes to reach the one section this task needed.
6. **Notes section not visible on the large account selection** (severity 2). On "7,495 selected"
   the right column showed no Notes between Appearance and Export.
7. **"Travel in project files and findings reports" next to "Nothing is sent"** (severity 2). He
   read "travel" as possibly leaving the building and wanted a one-line answer.
8. **Edit and Delete with no history shown** (severity 2). Changing the recorded reason with no
   trace is not acceptable for evidence.
9. **Find in notes appears to search this project only** (severity 2). The cross-case "I've seen
   this account before" need is not visibly met.
10. **Mocks are protein data** (severity 1). Only one screen had accounts; he had to translate.

## What worked for him

- The note says what it is about in its first line, and a numbered flag marks the item on the chart.
- "Quotes" freezes the number he wrote down.
- The panel warns when something under a note changed.
- "Assistant -- Off. Nothing is sent." visible on every screen.
