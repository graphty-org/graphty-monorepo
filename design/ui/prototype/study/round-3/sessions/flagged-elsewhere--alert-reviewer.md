## What happened (observer notes)

- She reached the found row in the March project (task completed as far as the mock goes) with
  one paste and one button, but only after an unexplained zero she first read as her own typing
  error.
- The first screen showed a different project (Les Miserables); she noticed at once and it cost
  her trust in "what am I looking at".
- She did not know what a "project" was in her world, or whether an old case would be among the
  "7 recent".
- The found row carried no account facts and no reason it was missing from April; she named the
  missing status as the first thing QA asks.
- She hesitated at Open, unsure whether it replaces the April project she has open. The mock ends
  there, so what she lands on is untested.
- "0 results in all 3,093 nodes" and "(3,093 accounts)" on consecutive lines: two nouns, one
  count, both saying zero.
