# Get back to where you were -- Explorer Elena

**Participant:** Explorer Elena, a product manager with no graph training (simulated; see
`study/personas/explorer-elena.md`). Trackpad, company laptop, Mac keys. Her reflexes come from
Google Sheets and her product-analytics dashboard.

**Task as read by the moderator:** "After your last few actions the numbers changed in a way you
did not expect. Get back to where you were, without losing work you meant to keep."

**What the moderator planted (Elena was not told):** Les Miserables, 77 characters, narrowed in
three filter steps. The middle step (keep characters with at least 5 connections) is the mistake;
the first and last are work to keep. A stray click has also just cleared a hand-built selection
of Valjean and the 17 characters beside him.

**Screens, in order:** the undo screen in its participant view, starting after the wrong step;
the same screen after one and after two presses of Cmd+Z; the steps list opened from the undo
line; the steps list after she ticked and unticked rows; the same screen with the selection
brought back. The filter chip screen was shown at the end for comparison of the steps list. The
pink dashed outlines around some buttons were explained by the moderator as "not built yet".

**Outcome:** finished with difficulty. She ended at 47 of 77 with the wrong step off, the group 8
step on and her selection back. The first undo scared her: it took away the step she wanted, and
she did not know that until she opened the list. She found the wrong step by its size ("took out
20"), not by understanding what it did. She nearly left the selection behind.

## Problems noted by the moderator

1. **The first Cmd+Z reversed the step she wanted to keep.** The undo line named it clearly, but
   she read it as a loss, not a reversible untick, until the list was open. Frustration 2.
2. **The steps list on the undo screen has no plain-words explanation of a step.** The filter chip
   screen has one ("keeps only nodes with at least 5 neighbors among the 60 it reads"); the undo
   screen shows only "Filter to degree >= 5". She identified the wrong step by the size of its
   cut, not its meaning. Frustration 3.
3. **"Previous selection" reads as a label, not an action.** She found it only because the task
   said "do not lose work". A selection lost to a stray click would otherwise stay lost.
   Frustration 2.
4. **Turning a step off created three floating dots and "components 3" with no explanation.** A
   new unexpected number in a task about unexpected numbers. Frustration 2.
5. **The chip says "2 of 3 steps", not which step is off.** She had to reopen the list to check.
   Frustration 1.
6. **"Create rule set" and "density" are unexplained jargon.** Ignored. Frustration 1.

## What worked

- The undo line ("Undone: Filter out group 8 -- Show in steps") told her what the press reversed,
  and "Show in steps" took her to the exact row.
- The row stayed in the list, unticked, so the step she wanted was one tick away.
- "Took out 20 -- 40 left" on each row let her spot the step that did the damage without knowing
  what degree is.
