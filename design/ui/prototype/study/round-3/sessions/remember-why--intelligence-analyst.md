# Session: remember why you kept these accounts -- Marcus, criminal intelligence analyst

**Task, as the moderator gave it:** "Leave yourself what you would need next week to remember why
you kept these accounts."

**Screens seen, in order:** the Notes panel (empty, then with notes, a chosen note, the row menu,
notes marked out of date, view-only), then the right-hand column for a set of proteins and for a
7,495-item selection of March 2026 transfers. Renders: `shots/r3-rw-ia-notes-s3.png`,
`shots/r3-rw-ia-notes-s4.png`, `shots/r3-rw-ia-notes-s7.png`, `shots/r3-rw-ia-notes-s9.png`,
`shots/screens__notes-panel.png`, `shots/r3-rw-ia-inspector-set.png`,
`shots/r3-rw-ia-inspector-cap.png`.

**Outcome:** partial. He found where a note would go and what a finished note looks like, but
never saw the moment of writing one, and nothing on these screens let him record a source or a
grade. He would not trust it to carry a case over a weekend.

## After the task

**Single Ease Question:** 4 of 7. "I could tell where the note goes. I couldn't tell how you
write one, and the thing I actually need to write down -- the source and the grade -- has no
place."

**Would he use this instead of his current tool?** "Not for this. For keeping a case, i2 already
lets me hang a note and a source reference on an entity, and it prints the grade. What this has
that i2 doesn't is the 'the set changed, your note's out of date' warning, and the note keeping
the number it quoted. That's good -- that's the kind of thing a defense attorney asks about.
But if I have to type 'grade B2' into a comment box, and there's no author on the note, it's a
sticky note, not a case record. And I still need to hear where 'travels in findings reports'
goes before any real account number goes in."

## Problems observed

1. **No moment of writing a note on these screens.** Both "Add note" and "Add a note" are shown,
   but neither screen shows the box that opens, what it asks for, or what it is attached to by
   default. He had to guess. Severity 3.
2. **No field for a source record and a reliability grade.** "Cites" and "Quotes" point at the
   tool's own runs and numbers, not at the evidence record. For an analyst the "why" of a kept
   entity is a source reference plus a grade; free text is where it gets lost. Severity 3.
3. **Two counts for the same set.** "Mule ring, fixed, 14" in the left panel and "Mule ring holds
   13 of 1,863 nodes" on the right, with nothing saying why they differ. Severity 3.
4. **The "why" of a set is cut off.** "module = DNA re..." and "Same value as TP..." -- the rule
   and origin, which answer half the task, are truncated at 1440 wide. Severity 2.
5. **Notes carry a date but no author.** A note handed to a colleague does not say who wrote it;
   "Owner notes" (a checkbox) was not understood. Severity 2.
6. **"Notes ... travel in project files and findings reports"** reads as "this may leave with
   anything I share" -- he wanted to know who sees a note before typing a name into it.
   Severity 2.
7. **"Detached" and "Restore set"** -- he could not tell what restoring would undo, so he would
   not click it. Severity 2.
8. **No place to record why something was dropped.** A note is about what is kept; excluded
   accounts are no longer in any set to hang a note on. Severity 2.
9. **The screens were about proteins, the task was about accounts.** Only the last screen showed
   transfers. He had to "pretend", which cost him confidence early. Severity 2.
10. **"fixed"** as a set label was a guess ("hand-picked"?). Severity 1.

## What worked

- A note is visibly "about" one thing (a set, a person, a run), dated, and pinned on the chart
  with a badge -- the i2 attached-note idea he already knows.
- "Quotes TP53 betweenness 0.114" freezes the number he relied on; "Earlier run -- Add current
  value" adds rather than overwrites. He said that is what a defense attorney would ask about.
- A set remembers how it was made ("Created from Neighbors of TP53").
- "View only" is what he would hand a sergeant.
