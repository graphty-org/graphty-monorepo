# First-click test: Sarah, fraud detection analyst

Participant: the fraud-analyst persona (level-2 financial crime investigator, Excel and i2 user,
reads numbers and IDs closely, skims legends and help text, expects Ctrl+Z to work). Each answer
was given looking only at the named screen, before the correct targets were shown. Confidence is
1 (guess) to 7 (certain). Answers were not changed after scoring.

## Les Miserables network, nothing selected (round-5-fc-lesmis-rest.png)

### Picture of the network for the paper
- First click: the three-line menu button at the top left.
- Confidence: 4
- In her words: "There's no Export button anywhere. In every Windows program the export is under
  the menu, so that's where I go. If it's not there I'm screenshotting it and cropping in Paint."
- Scored: correct.

### Repeat the earlier bridges calculation exactly
- First click: the Bridges row under Results in the right panel (the one that says "done").
- Confidence: 4
- In her words: "There's Bridges twice -- one in the Style stack that says 'off', one under

  settings. If it only shows me colours I'm annoyed."

### Rank the characters a second way
- First click: the "..." at the right of the table header (next to the search icon).
- Confidence: 3
- In her words: "The ranking I have is the degree column in the table. A second ranking is

  'run a new ranking'."
- Scored: incorrect. The target was the + on Results, the lightning (Quick actions) button, or

## Score

8 of 14 correct.

Misses, in her terms:
- Anything computed and new (a second ranking, in versus out) she looks for in the table, not
  under Results or the lightning button. Neither control said to her "work something out".
- Replacing the data file: she clicks the file chip, because that is the file.
- Undo of a lost selection: the keyboard, always, before any menu.
- "Amount not used yet. Change..." pulled her away from the Path tool: the sentence answered
  half the question, so she took it.
- Bringing in a style file: she went to the Style stack +, not the menu or Data.
