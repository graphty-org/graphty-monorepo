# First-click test -- Marcus, criminal intelligence analyst

Participant: Marcus, 44, criminal intelligence analyst at a state fusion center. Lives in i2
Analyst's Notebook and Excel. Looked at each screen once and said the one thing he would click
first, with how sure he was (1 = pure guess, 7 = certain). Answers were given before the
intended targets were revealed and were not changed afterwards.

## What he said, prompt by prompt

**Picture for the paper.** "I don't see Export anywhere. No printer, no camera. That three-line
thing top left is going to be the File menu, it always is. That's where Save As lives." (4)

**Repeat the bridges calculation.** "There's 'Bridges, done' under Results. That's the run.
Click it, it had better tell me what it was run on and with what settings, because that's the
first thing defense asks." (5) He also noticed "Bridges off" in the style stack and asked why the
same thing is on in one place and off in another; he decided the style one is just the paint.

**Rank a second way.** "It's sorted by degree down there. Second way is betweenness -- the
middleman number. Where do I run it? There's a plus next to Results. Guess that adds a result."
Hesitated over the lightning bolt: "Lightning is 'quick something'. I don't click things I can't
name." (3)

**Recolor group 2 or 3.** "Group 3 isn't even in the little key, it's hiding under '6 more'. So
I'll click the orange square for 2 and change that one." (5)

**Get the selection back.** "Control-Z. If you make me click, the menu top left, Edit, Undo.
Nothing on the screen says 'previous selection', so I'm hoping Undo covers it." (3)

**What is painting Valjean.** "Appearance, and the Group color line is lit up, and he's group 2
over in Attributes. That's the answer. I'd click Group color to be sure." (5) Asked what
"Top wins" means on the other screen: "top of the list beats the bottom, fine, like layers."

**Where the betweenness number comes from.** "0.57, highest. Point five seven of what -- that's
my question every time. The Results line is where I'd click. The column header down in the
table would just sort it." (4)

**Next month's file.** "Data on the side. That's where the file is. I half wanted to click that
little file chip under the name, 'transfers-2026...', to swap it, but I'd go Data first." (4)
Wants it said plainly that his styles and sets carry over; nothing on screen promises that.

**Money in far more than out.** "It says right there 'amount not used yet'. Then it can't answer
the question until it uses the amount. Change." (4) He treated this as the obvious first step,
not a detour.

**Cheapest route with bigger transfers costing more.** "Path is the thing I do most. The button
with the two circles and the line, that's a route. Whether it knows bigger money is 'more
expensive' -- no idea, and that's the part I'd be checking." (3) Said the grey honeycomb blob was
"not a chart, that's a weather map".

**Did anything leave the computer.** "'Nothing has been sent from this project.' With a lock.
That's the first line I'd read on any tool anyway. Click it and I want dates and a log I can
print for IT." (6) Also read the Assistant text on the rail: "Off, nothing is sent. Good. Keep it
off."

**Two blues.** "Ribosome, top of the key, light blue. Spliceosome, dark blue. Click the Ribosome
square." (5)

**Lab's color file.** "Not my world, but a file coming in goes through Data. I'd look there for
an import. If it's in the top-left menu instead, I'd find it the second time." (3)

**How Ribosome differs.** "Click Ribosome in the key and hope it picks all 56 of them. Then I'd
want the table for just those against everybody else. There's a Table strip at the bottom." (3)

## His summary

"The side panels read like a case file: what was run, what's painting what, and the line about
nothing being sent. That's the stuff I care about. What I couldn't find on the screen was plain
words: Export, Import, Undo. I had to guess they're behind the three lines. And the lightning
button I'd leave alone until somebody tells me what it does."
