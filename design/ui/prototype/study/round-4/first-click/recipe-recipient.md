# First-click test: the recipe recipient (Tom, lab manager)

Tom is a lab manager who opens files his postdoc sends and never builds a network himself. He
reads headings, numbers and legend labels, skips grey small text and long side panels, and his
habit with any new tool is: drag the file onto the window, then the biggest button, then File.
Each answer is the one thing he clicks first, with his confidence from 1 (guess) to 7 (certain).
Correct targets were read only after all answers were given; no answer was changed.

## Les Miserables, nothing selected

**fc-1. A picture of this network for the paper.**
Clicks: the three-line button at the very top left. "There's no File menu, so that's probably it.
I want a PNG, same as she'd send me." Confidence 4. Correct.

**fc-2. Repeat the earlier bridges calculation exactly.**
Clicks: "Bridges ... done" under Results on the right. "There's a Bridges under Style stack too,
with 'off' next to it, which worries me. But 'done' sounds like the calculation." Confidence 4.
Correct.

**fc-3. Rank the characters a second way.**
Clicks: the three dots above the table on the right. "The table says 'Sorted by degree'. I'd look
for a different sort. I don't know what else there is to rank by, that's her side." Confidence 2.
Incorrect (the + on Results, the lightning button, or the main menu).

**fc-4. Groups 2 and 3 are hard to tell apart; change one.**
Clicks: the orange square next to "2" in the box at the bottom left. "Group 3 isn't even in that
box, it's under '6 more'. So I'd change 2." Confidence 4. Correct.

**fc-5. A stray click cleared your selection; get it back.**
Clicks: the three-line button at top left, looking for Edit and Undo. "I don't do shortcuts. If
there's an Edit, that's where Undo is." Confidence 3. Correct.

## Les Miserables, Valjean selected

**fc-6. Valjean is not the color you expected; what is painting him?**
Clicks: "Group color" under Appearance. "Appearance, colour. He's orange, the box says orange is
group 2, and his group is 2 further down. That's the one." Confidence 4. Correct.

**fc-7. Where does his betweenness number come from?**
Clicks: the "betweenness 0.57, highest" line under Results. "I don't know what betweenness is. But
that's where the number is, so that's where I'd click to ask." Confidence 4. Correct.

## Transfers, March 2026

**fc-8. Next month's file has arrived; bring it in so everything carries over.**
First instinct: drag the file onto the window. As a click: the little file chip
"transfers-2026..." under the project name. "That's this month's file. I'd expect to swap it there.
I'd be nervous it replaces everything, though." Confidence 3. Incorrect (Data on the rail, the
project name, or the main menu).

**fc-9. Accounts that take in far more money than they send out.**
Clicks: "Change..." after "amount not used yet" on the right. "It says it isn't using the amount.
The question is about money, so the amount has to be switched on first." Confidence 3. Incorrect
(the + on Results, the lightning button, or the main menu).

**fc-10. Cheapest route between two accounts, bigger transfer costs more.**
Clicks: the second button on the bottom toolbar, the wiggly line between two dots. "It looks like
a route. Though again it's about amounts, so maybe that Change... first." Confidence 2. Correct.

**fc-11. IT asks whether anything has left the computer.**
Clicks: "Nothing has been sent from this project" with the lock, under the name. "That's the
first thing I read on this screen. If it's true, that's my answer to IT." Confidence 6. Correct.

## Human protein interactions

**fc-12. Ribosome and Spliceosome are two blues you cannot tell apart; change one.**
Clicks: the light blue square next to Ribosome in the box at the bottom left. "Those two blues
are the same to me too, but the names are next to them." Confidence 4. Correct.

**fc-13. The lab's file of standard colors and sizes; bring it in.**
First instinct: drag it onto the window. As a click: the three-line button at top left, looking
for File > Open. "I did notice the little paint palette on the right, but I'm not sure that opens
a file." Confidence 3. Correct.

**fc-14. How is the Ribosome module different from the rest?**
Clicks: "Ribosome" in the box at the bottom left. "I'd hope it shows me just those. Different
how, though? I'd ask her what she meant." Confidence 3. Correct.

## Tally

11 of 14 correct. The misses:

- Ranking a second way: nothing on the screen says "rank"; he went to the table.
- Next month's file: the file chip under the project name looked like the place to swap the file.
- Money in versus money out: "amount not used yet. Change..." caught him, because the task was
  about money and that is the only place the screen mentions money.
