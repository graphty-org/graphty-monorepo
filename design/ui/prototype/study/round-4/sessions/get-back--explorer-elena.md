# Get back to where you were -- Explorer Elena

**Participant:** Elena, a product manager with no graph training. Lives in Google Sheets, Slides
and the company's analytics dashboard. Company 14-inch laptop, trackpad, Windows, Chrome. Says
"dots" and "lines", not "nodes" and "edges". Not sure anything can be undone until she has seen it
undone.

**Task as given by the moderator:** "Something on the screen just changed that you did not expect.
Get back to where you were."

**Clock:** run as the long "curious afternoon" variant (no deadline, three or four dead ends
tolerated). Where the short "first contact" variant would have ended is marked below.

**Starting point:** the Les Miserables sample, 77 characters, narrowed by three filter steps to 27.
She had been looking at Valjean with the 17 characters around him selected (black rings on the
dots, the table rows tinted blue, "18 nodes" at the top of the right panel). A click on empty
canvas has just cleared that selection. She was not told any of this; she was shown the
selected screen for a few seconds before the task, as "what you were looking at".

## Think-aloud

**The starting screen.** "OK. Something changed. ...The right side is different. It had my, um,
it said 18 something, and now it's 'Co-appearances, Graph', edges, components, density. I don't
know what density is. And the dots -- they had those little black circles around them before. Now
they don't."

"So I lost my -- the ones I picked. The Valjean ones."

*(She looks at the canvas, not the panels. She does not look at the button at the top left, "27 of
77 nodes, 3 steps". She does not read the line above the table.)*

"Did I click something? I probably clicked something. I do that on the trackpad."

"Ctrl+Z. That's what I'd do in Sheets."

**Ctrl+Z, once.** *(A whole light-blue bunch of dots appears at the bottom -- Marius, Gavroche,
Enjolras. A dark bar above the toolbar says "Undone: Filter out group 8" with a "Show in steps"
button. The black rings do not come back.)*

"Oh! OK -- more dots. Wait. Are these the ones? The blue ones?" *(They are not; the blue group
was never part of her selection.)* "No... my ones were around Valjean, in the middle. These are
new."

"'Undone: Filter out group 8.' I don't know what group 8 is. Is that a thing I did? I don't think
I filtered anything. Maybe it was already like that when I opened it."

"The circles are still gone. So it undid something else. Not my thing."

"Maybe it's one more back. It says it undid one thing, maybe mine's before that."

**Ctrl+Z, twice.** *(More dots: some dark ones off to the right, a few blue ones near Myriel. The
bar now reads "Undone: Filter out group 8 and Filter to degree >= 5". Top left: "60 of 77 nodes,
1 of 3 steps".)*

"Oh no. No, that's worse. That's a lot more stuff. 'Degree greater than or equal to 5'?" *(Laughs.)*
"I have no idea. I'm making this worse, aren't I."

"Can I -- how do I un-undo? In Sheets it's Ctrl+Y. ...Actually I don't want to press anything else
yet. I'll just look."

*(First-contact variant: this is where she stops. Two presses of the key she trusts, each one
moved her further from what she had, and the message used two words she does not know. Her
answers in the short variant got shorter from here: "yeah", "not sure".)*

**Finding the line above the table.** *(In the long variant she keeps looking. Her eyes go to the
table, because Valjean is at the top of it.)* "Valjean's still there. 'Selected: none, showing the
previous selection.' ...Previous selection. That's the button, right? That's literally what I
want."

"It's grey text though. Is that a button or a label?" *(She hovers, it highlights.)* "OK it's a
button."

**Previous selection.** *(The black rings come back on the Valjean dots. The right panel says "18
nodes" again. The table rows tint blue again. The dark bar about undoing has gone.)*

"There! OK. There they are. That's mine."

"...But all the other stuff is still here. The blue ones and the black ones. It wasn't like this.
It was smaller before. There was a lot less."

"And the undo message is gone, so I can't even see what it said it undid."

*(She looks at the legend card.)* "Blue is 8. 'Group 8', that's what the message said. So group 8
is the blue ones, it put them back. OK. That makes sense now, sort of."

"The black ones in the corner -- those are, like, the dead ends? Nobody connects to them?" *(A
wrong reading: black is simply the colour of one small group, as the legend's "5 more" would show
if opened. She does not open it.)*

**Finding the filter button.** "Where did it say how many there were... Up here. '60 of 77 nodes.'
It was 27 before. I'm pretty sure it was 27, I remember thinking that's not many. '1 of 3 steps.'
So there are three steps and one is on?"

*(She clicks the button. A list opens: "Filter to degree >= 2, took out 17, 60 left", ticked;
"Filter to degree >= 5, off, takes nothing out", unticked; "Filter out group 8, off, takes nothing
out", unticked.)*

"Oh. Oh, these are checkboxes. OK, this I get. Two of them are off. The undo turned them off."

"I still don't know what degree is. But it was on before and now it's off, and I didn't turn it
off, the undo did. So I just put them back how they were?"

*(She hesitates over the tick box for a while.)* "If I tick it, does it lose my circles again?"

*(She ticks "Filter to degree >= 5". The top left reads 40 of 77, 2 of 3 steps. The black rings
stay. She ticks "Filter out group 8". The top left reads "27 of 77 nodes, 3 steps". The blue bunch
goes. The rings are still on her 18.)*

"27. There. That's it. That's what it was."

"And my Valjean ones are still circled. OK. Good."

*(Asked whether she is back where she was.)* "Yeah. I think so. It looks the same. I'd probably
take a screenshot now so I don't lose it again."

## Post-task questions

**Single Ease Question (1 = very difficult, 7 = very easy): 3.**

"The ending was easy -- the checkboxes, and the 'previous selection' button, once I saw them.
Getting there was not. The first thing I tried made it worse, and then I did it again. If I didn't
have all afternoon I'd have closed it after the second undo."

**Would you use this instead of what you use now?**

"I don't really use anything for this now. Sheets, and I tried Gephi once and gave up at the
install. So, compared to that, yes, it's in the browser and it didn't lose my stuff for real. But
the undo scared me. I pressed the button every app has and it took away things I didn't know I'd
done. I'd want it to just give me back my circles."

## What the session showed

Written for the design team, outside the think-aloud.

1. **Her "something changed" was the lost selection, and Ctrl+Z does not bring a selection back.**
   The first thing she reached for, twice, was the key every app she uses has. It undid two filter
   steps instead, moving her from 27 to 60 characters, and left the selection gone. The recovery
   that works, "Previous selection", sits in grey text above the table, and she found it only
   after the undo key had failed twice. Nothing at the moment of loss is drawn on screen (the
   message about the cleared selection is spoken to a screen reader only), so a sighted reader
   has nothing to tell her what happened.
2. **The undo message uses words she cannot map to the picture.** "Filter out group 8" and "Filter
   to degree >= 5" did not tell her what had changed. She connected "group 8" to the blue dots only
   later, from the legend, after the message was gone.
3. **The undo message disappears on the next action.** Clicking "Previous selection" ended it, so
   when she wanted to reread what her two undos had done, it was no longer anywhere she could see.
4. **The filter button's list is what she understood.** Checkboxes, "off", "60 left": this was the
   first control in the session that matched something she already knew, and it is what got her
   back. She did not notice the button until she went looking for a count she half remembered.
5. **She did not know Redo, and would not risk guessing it.** Redo would have been the direct way
   back after the two undos. The key is printed only inside the main menu's Edit submenu, which she
   never opened.
6. **The selection ring is easy to miss.** A thin black ring is the only sign on the canvas that a
   dot is selected; she noticed its absence mainly from the right panel changing.
7. **Her end state differs from the facilitator script's "done".** The screen's own facilitator
   notes expect the reader to switch off the middle filter step as a mistake (47 of 77). With only
   this moderator's wording, nothing told her any step was a mistake, so she restored exactly what
   she had seen: 27 of 77, three steps, her selection back. For her that was success.
8. **Word mismatch between screens.** The undo screen counts "nodes"; the filter-chip screen counts
   "characters". She used neither; "characters" would have been closer to how she talked about the
   dots ("the Valjean ones").
