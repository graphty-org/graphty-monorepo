## What her misses show

- She starts and reads calculations through the algorithm list (Gephi's Statistics panel), not
  through Results, in both arms. Where results live mattered to her only for finding how an
  earlier run was set up: with Results on the rail she went straight there; with Results in the
  right panel she first went back to the algorithm list, where she had started the run.
- "Recipes" does not read as a place for a palette file. To her a file of colors and sizes is
  Appearance, so she goes to the style stack and adds a layer.
- Her undo reflex beats the Previous selection item sitting one line below Undo. If Undo does not
  bring the selection back, she will call undo broken rather than look further.
- For "where the money went next after a date" she builds it the Gephi way, as an ego-network
  filter plus a time range, and never looks at the node's own Neighbors action. The filter chip
  offers no such step by name, so she would find out only when she opened Add a step.
- Choosing which way a weight runs on the cheapest route: she expects it in the algorithm's own
  settings and is not confident it is there, because the tree shows the algorithm names only.
