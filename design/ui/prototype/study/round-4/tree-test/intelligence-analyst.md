## What this participant's path says

- **"Recipes" does not read as colors and sizes.** He saw the word both times and skipped it as a
  cooking word or a vague template. A file of someone else's colors went to the style stack
  instead. The label, or a hint beside it, needs to say what a recipe carries.
- **Weight belongs where the shortest path is chosen.** Both times he went to Algorithms > Path
  first and found nothing about what a bigger amount means; he found it only on the canvas
  toolbar.
- **Selecting a group or a route raises expectations the panel does not meet.** He expected the
  panel for a selected route to total the money and the panel for a selected group to compare it
  with the rest. Both answers were elsewhere (the table footer, the table row menu or a result).
- **A rail place called Results pulled him in for a single person's score** and cost him a
  backtrack (top nodes only), but made the run record easy to find. For him the two arms came
  out even.
- **Money in versus money out** was an Excel pivot in his head first; "Weighted degree: in, out"
  was recognisable once he saw it, but not where he started.
