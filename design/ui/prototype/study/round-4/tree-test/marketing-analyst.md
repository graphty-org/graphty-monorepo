# Tree test transcript: Jordan, marketing network analyst

Jordan is a growth-marketing analyst who does "the network stuff" one or two days a week. She
knows degree from betweenness but calls every centrality an "influence score", says "cluster"
for community, and says "export" for both a picture and a table. She reads labels carefully and
skips anything that sounds like jargon or a code editor. She saw only the text outline of the
navigation, one level at a time, and took the 16 tasks twice, in two separate sessions with no
memory of each other: first with Results as a section of the right panel (arm A), then with
Results as its own place on the left rail (arm B). Task order was shuffled in each session.

For each task: the path she took, where she stopped, the first top-level place she opened, how
sure she was (1 = guessing, 7 = certain), and afterwards whether the stop was a correct place and
whether she got there without going back up the tree. Her answers were not changed after the
correct places were shown.
