# Notes with names -- Analyst Alex

**Participant:** Alex, operations data analyst at a logistics company. Computes metrics in NetworkX,
draws in Gephi, pastes into PowerPoint. Company Windows laptop, Chrome, no admin rights. Hands
work to colleagues as a .gephi file plus a Word doc of "what I did".

**Task as given by the moderator:** "A colleague opens this project next week and will read your
notes beside her own. Make sure she can tell which ones are yours."

**Starting point:** a project with notes already in it. Alex had not set a name anywhere. He was
not told where the name setting lives.

**Screens seen** (renders the participant looked at, in order):
- `../../../shots/r4-alex-names-tan-s3.png` -- a note just added: the Note popout and the Notes
  row in the inspector (one-author project, date only)
- `../../../shots/r4-alex-names-tan-s5.png` -- the Notes panel, next session, one author
- `../../../shots/r4-alex-names-tan-s2.png` -- writing a new note
- `../../../shots/r4-alex-names-preferences.png` -- the main menu open on Preferences, with
  "Your name on notes and recipes..." and, further down the page, its small dialog
- `../../../shots/r4-alex-names-notes-s2.png` and `../../../shots/r4-alex-names-notes-s3.png` --
  the Notes panel of a project with notes by two people (Adam Powers, Lin Chen)
- `../../../shots/screens__export-dialog-evidence--study.png` -- the Export dialog, findings

## Think-aloud

**The note just added.** "OK so here's a note I wrote. 'About Mule ring, 14 accounts.' Sep 28
2026, 10:42. Then the text, cites PageRank, quotes one account. ...Where's my name? There's a
date. There's no name. Down in the right panel it just says 'just now'."

"So right now this note is... nobody's. If she opens this next week she sees a date. That's the
problem I've got with my Word doc, basically -- I have to write 'AR:' in front of everything."

**The Notes panel.** "Left side, Notes. List of them, newest first. Dates, text, 'About' line.
Sep 21, Sep 20, Sep 19. Still no names. Nothing says 'you'. There's a find box -- 'Find in notes'.
And a little filter icon next to it." *(Hovers the funnel icon; nothing about people.)* "I was
hoping that's 'by me'. I don't know what it is."

**Looking for where the name goes.** "Right. So I need to tell it who I am. First place I'd look
is -- top right, there's normally a little circle with your initials. There's '100%'. That's zoom.
No profile thing."

"Second guess, the note itself. The three dots on the note." *(Reads the design notes of the
note's menu: Edit note, Delete note.)* "No. Not there."

"New note editor -- 'New note', the text box, Cites, Quotes, 'Enter adds'. Nothing about who's
writing it. OK."

"Hamburger, then. File, Edit, View, Selection, Algorithms, Recipes, Preferences, Help. ...I'd go
Preferences. That's where Chrome keeps that kind of thing." *(Opens it.)* "Scroll wheel, WebGPU,
Theme, Reduced motion, Default overview -- and 'Your name on notes and recipes...'. There it is.
That's a clear label, I'll give it that. It literally says what it's for."

"About forty seconds, three wrong places first. Not terrible. I wouldn't have found it in the
first place I looked, though."

**The name dialog.** "Name field. I'd type 'Alex R.' -- or my full name, probably, because the
director reads these sometimes." *(Reads the help line.)* "'Saved with each note and recipe you
make from now on, exactly as typed.' ...From now on. Hang on."

"So the note I wrote five minutes ago -- the Mule ring one -- doesn't get my name? And all my
notes from last week? That's the whole task, those are exactly the ones she's going to read. If I
have to go back and prefix them by hand I'm back to typing 'AR:' again."

"'Shown only when a project holds work by more than one person.' OK... so I type my name, hit
Save, go back to my notes -- and they still don't show my name, because it's only me in the
project. So I can't check it worked. I'd click Save and then have nothing to look at. That makes
me nervous. I'd want to see it on my own note once, just to know it's there before I send the
file."

"'Leave blank to record no name.' Fine. And there's no sign-in, no account. Good. I don't want
another login, and I don't want my name going to some server."

*(Moderator: "Where is the name kept?")* "The top of the page says 'kept in this browser'. So if
I open this on another machine, or IT wipes my Chrome profile, it's gone and I'm nobody again.
And -- also -- anyone could type 'Alex' in theirs. For a colleague that's fine. For anything
that's an audit trail I wouldn't lean on it."

**What she sees: two people in one project.** *(Shown the Notes panel with Adam Powers' and Lin
Chen's notes.)* "OK, now that's what I wanted. 'Adam Powers, Sep 28 2026, 10:14.' 'Lin Chen, Sep
27 2026, 16:42.' The name's right under the 'About' line, before the text. I can scan down and
see who's who. Same grey as the date, so it doesn't jump out -- but it's there on every row."

"If I'm her, I'd want to go 'just show me Alex's'. Let me try the find box." *(Reads what Find
matches: a note's text, what it is about and the runs it cites.)* "It doesn't search names? So
I type 'Alex' and get nothing, or worse, get a note that mentions Alex. That's a miss. Twenty
notes, fine, I'll scroll. Sixty, no."

"And what does she see on my old notes, the ones from before I set the name? Blank? Just the
date, next to hers with a name on? That's actually worse -- then it looks like hers are 'real'
and mine are anonymous. I can't tell from this screen."

"Another thing -- 'Edit note'. If she edits one of mine, whose name is it then? Mine? Hers?
Both? I'd want to know, because 'Alex said X' and it was her edit is the kind of thing that ends
up in a deck wrong."

**What she actually gets.** *(Asked what he would send her; opens Export.)* "Project file,
'Everything, with the data' -- that's what I'd send. Or the findings report. The report's first
page says 'written 2026-09-28 by Sarah Okafor' on the filter step. OK, so names do travel into
the report. 'Other notes, 2 notes with their quotes and citations' -- I'd assume my name's on
those too, but it doesn't show me. I'd check the file before I sent it. I always do anyway."

"'Nothing is uploaded.' Good. Keep saying that."

## Single Ease Question

**4 out of 7.**

"Setting the name, once I found it, was easy -- one box, Save. That part's a 6. What drags it
down: I looked in three places first, I couldn't see my own name on anything afterwards so I
couldn't check it worked, and the notes I'd already written don't get it. The task was make sure
she can tell which ones are mine. I'm not sure I did. I think the new ones will be. The old ones,
I don't know."

## Would he use this instead of his current tool?

"For this? Yes, probably. What I do now is a Word doc next to the .gephi file with my initials in
front of every line, and Gephi itself has nowhere to put a note at all. Here the note sits on the
thing it's about, with the run it cites and a date, and her name and mine next to them. That's
better than a Word doc and I wouldn't argue with that."

"But I'd want two things before I'd trust it for handover. Let me put my name on the notes I
already wrote -- or at least ask me when I set it. And show me my own name on my own note, even
if I'm the only one, somewhere, once. Otherwise I'm sending it off hoping."

## Moderator notes

- Alex's first two guesses for the name setting were a profile avatar at the top right (there is
  none) and the note's own menu or editor. He found "Your name on notes and recipes..." under
  Preferences in the main menu on the third guess, about forty seconds in, and read the label as
  clear.
- The help line "from now on" was read at once as "my existing notes stay nameless". This is the
  heart of the task, since the colleague reads the notes already written. He did not find a way to
  name earlier notes, and the mocks do not show one.
- "Shown only when a project holds work by more than one person" meant he had no way to confirm
  the name had been recorded in his own one-author project. He said he would send the file
  "hoping".
- He could not tell from any screen what a note written before the name was set looks like in a
  two-author project (a blank author line beside named ones). The mocks do not show that row.
- Find in notes does not match author names; he expected typing a name to narrow the list to that
  person's notes.
- He raised, unprompted, whose name a note carries after a colleague edits it. The mocks do not
  say.
- The name is a per-browser preference; he noted it is lost with the browser profile and can be
  typed by anyone, and judged that acceptable for colleagues but not for an audit trail.
- Positive: no account and no sign-in; the full date and time on every note; names on every row in
  a two-author list; "Nothing is uploaded" on the Export footer; the report carrying a name on the
  filter step.
