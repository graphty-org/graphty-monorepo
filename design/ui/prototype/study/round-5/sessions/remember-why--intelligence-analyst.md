# Leave yourself a note on why you kept these accounts -- Marcus, criminal intelligence analyst

**Task, as the moderator gave it:** "You kept these accounts aside for a reason. Leave yourself a
note on why, so you remember next week."

**Participant:** Marcus, criminal intelligence analyst at a state fusion center. Works in i2
Analyst's Notebook and Excel; every fact he records needs a source, a grade and a date. Gives a
new tool about five minutes, reads labels and error text word for word, skims everything else.

**Screens used, as the participant saw them (design notes hidden):** the take-a-note screen (the
"March transfers" project with a 14-account set called "Mule ring"), the notes panel (shown on a
second project, "Human protein interactions", because the panel mock uses that data), and the
take-a-note flow page. Renders: shots/record/r4-marcus-remember-take-s1.png to -s8.png,
shots/record/r4-marcus-remember-notes-s1.png to -s10.png.

**Outcome:** done, with one wrong turn and one half-posted note. **Single Ease Question:** 4 of 7.
**Would he use it instead of his current tool:** no, not instead; maybe beside it.

---

## Think-aloud

**1. Which accounts?** "Kept aside." OK. Left side, "Sets and paths", one row: "Mule ring,
frozen, 14". Mule ring -- money mules, fine, that's bank-return work, I do that. But "frozen".
In my world a frozen account is one the bank froze. Did somebody freeze these fourteen? Or does
it mean I froze the list? I'm going to guess the list, because the other option means the tool
knows something about the bank it has no business knowing. Still -- that word is going to get
read wrong by every detective I hand this to.

And the moderator said "kept aside". The left side says "set". The row says "frozen". Later I
see a dropdown that says "Kept sets". Three words for one thing. Pick one.

**2. First move: the word "Notes".** I read labels, so the first thing I see that matches the
job is "Notes" on the far-left strip. I click that before I click anything on the set. (Notes
panel, empty state.) "No notes yet. A note can be about a selection, a set, a result or the
whole graph." Button: "Add note...". Under it, small: "Notes are saved in the project and travel
in project files and findings reports."

"Travel." Travel where? That's the sentence I care about most on this whole screen and it is the
vaguest one. If "the project" is a file on my machine, say "on this computer". If it's on your
server, I'm done before I type. The strip at the left does say "Assistant: Off. Nothing is
sent." Good. I like that line. Put the same kind of line next to the notes.

**3. Add note... asks what it's about.** (Notes panel, new note with nothing selected.) A box
opens: "About:" and a dropdown already set to "the graph Human protein interactions", with a
check mark on "The graph". Under that, "Kept sets", with the sets and their counts, and "Select
something first".

Hold on -- why am I in a protein thing now? (Moderator: this screen uses a different sample
project; pretend it's March transfers.) Fine. So on my project I'd see "Mule ring, 14 accounts"
under Kept sets. I pick it. Good that it asked. Bad that the default was the whole graph. If I
was in a hurry I'd have started typing and written a note about fourteen accounts onto the
entire case. It shows me what it's pinning to before I type, which I'll give it credit for --
i2 would've just dropped a sticky on the chart wherever I clicked.

**4. The other way in, which I'd have found second.** (Take-a-note, first state.) With Mule ring
clicked, the right side shows the set: 14 accounts, 7 countries, riskScore 88 to 98, members.
Four little icons at the top with no words on them -- a people icon, a funnel, a plus, three
dots. I don't click a plus on a list I'm keeping as evidence. The three dots: Compare with...,
Collapse, Hide on canvas, Run layout, Extract as graph, then a line, then "Add note...". It's
there, it's last. I'd never have gone looking under "Run layout" for it.

The graph's own panel has a "Notes" heading with a plus even when it's empty. The set's panel
doesn't, until there's a note on it. So the one place I'd look first -- on the thing I kept --
doesn't tell me notes exist.

And down on the bottom toolbar there's a little sticky-note icon. No label, no tooltip in this
picture. I'd have tried it if the rail hadn't said "Notes". (Moderator shows the state where
it's armed: "About ACC-233575. Shift+click adds. Esc: done with this note.") So if I'd clicked
that and then clicked on one of the orange dots, I'd be writing about one account, not the ring.
It does say which one at the bottom, in small type. I'd have missed it.

**5. Writing it.** (Take-a-note, writing.) Header "New note", then "About Mule ring, 14
accounts". Good, that's the first thing I read, before the box. I type the way I write in a
case file:

"Kept aside 9/28. 14 accts flagged by bank monitoring, riskScore 88-98. Money comes in from
dozens of accts and goes out through ACC-753261 -- looks like the collector."

and I hit Enter to start the next line, "Source:" -- and it posts. "Enter adds. Shift+Enter, new
line." It says so, grey, under the box. I didn't read it because nobody reads that. Every
report form I've used, Enter is a new line. Now I have half a note. There's "Edit note" in the
note's three dots, so I can fix it, but that's the first thing I did in this tool and it went
wrong.

**6. "Cite a run..."** There's "Cites" and "Quotes" under the text. I see "Cite" and I think:
great, this is where the source goes. The subpoena return. Wells Fargo, statement period,
return number. No. "Cite a run" means one of your calculations -- PageRank, full graph.
That's not a source to me, that's your math. I'll take it, because if I put a number from it in
a report I do need to say where it came from -- but I need the other kind of cite more, and
there's nowhere for it.

"Quote a value..." searches the fourteen accounts' own values. I type "753" and get
ACC-753261, PageRank 0.000551, riskScore 95, country BR. That's decent -- the note keeps the
number as it was the day I wrote it. But "riskScore 95" -- whose score? The bank's monitoring
system's, I assume, not yours. The quote should say which.

**7. What isn't here.** No source field. No grade. In my shop every entry carries source
reliability and information reliability -- B2, C3, whatever. I can type "B2" in the text, but
then it's just letters in a paragraph; it won't print as a grade and I can't find "everything
graded below C". For an intelligence product that's not a nice-to-have, it's the field.

**8. Added.** (Take-a-note, added.) The note opens beside the ring: About Mule ring, 14
accounts; Sep 28 2026, 10:42; my text; cites PageRank; quotes ACC-753261. A little "1" marker
on the chart where the ring is. And the set's panel now has a "Notes 1" section with the note
in it. That's what I wanted.

Two things. The panel row says "just now" and the note itself says "Sep 28 2026, 10:42". Same
note, two answers. Put the real time everywhere; "just now" is useless on Monday. And there's no
name on it. (Moderator: the name shows only when a project has notes by more than one person.)
The day a second analyst touches this case, every old note has to say it was me. And on a
printout it has to say it was me whether or not anybody else ever opened the file.

**9. Next week.** (Take-a-note, next session; notes panel, list.) Notes panel, newest first,
each one saying what it's about and when. Clicking the Mule ring note selects the fourteen,
frames them and opens the note next to them. That's the "I left it Friday, it's there Monday"
thing I actually want. Better than i2, where I'd be hunting for a text box somewhere on a
six-foot chart.

"Find in notes" at the top. I search for a name that isn't in any note (notes panel, no match)
and the list just goes blank. Nothing. No "no notes match". Is it broken or is there nothing?
Tell me.

One note says "Detached -- The set this note pointed to was changed." with "Bring back Suspects,
first pass (as kept Sep 19)". I like that a lot. The note didn't silently start pointing at
something else. That's exactly the kind of thing I get cross-examined on.

**10. After new data.** (Take-a-note, after April's data.) The note now says "Earlier data" with
"Add current value", and the quote says "now 0.000428 in April's data" next to the old number.
Good: it kept what I wrote and tells me what changed. "Earlier data" is a bit vague -- earlier
than what? Say "March data".

But -- the dates. Last screen, this same note said "Sep 21 2026, 10:14". Now it says "Apr 3
2026, 10:14". Same text, same note. If the date on a note changes when the data changes, I can't
use this. A note's date is when I wrote it. Full stop. (Moderator: the two screens were made
separately.) Then fix the screens, because that's the one thing I'd have walked out on.

**11. Getting it out.** The flow page says notes go in a "findings report", one HTML file, each
note as text with what it's about, who, when, citations and quotes, opens offline, prints to
PDF. Offline and one file -- that's the right answer for my building. I'd want the source and
grade in there too, printed next to the note, the way our reports do it.

---

## Single Ease Question

**4 of 7.** The note ended up on the right fourteen accounts and it will be waiting next week,
which is the job. I lose points for: the About box defaulting to the whole graph; Enter posting
half my note; "Cite" not meaning a source; no grade; and a note date that changed between two
screens.

## Would he use this instead of his current tool?

"Not instead. Right now 'why I kept these' goes in a text box on the i2 chart and in my case
notes in Word, with the source and grade. This is better than the i2 text box at one thing:
it's stuck to the accounts, not to a spot on the chart, and it tells me when the numbers under
it moved. But until it has a source and a grade, and I know the project file stays on our
machines, it's a scratch pad next to my real notes, not the record."

## In his own words, afterwards

"Put a Notes box on the set I kept, not three menus away. Make Enter a new line. Give me a
source field and a grade, and put my name on every note. And never, ever change the date on a
note."
