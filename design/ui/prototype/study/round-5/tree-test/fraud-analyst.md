  is the only thing that will catch her.

  task. She reaches the task only through Quick actions, and would still rather pivot it in Excel.
- In arm A she found Results only by reading every top-level line; "with nothing selected" is not
  where she expects a record of past runs to live. In arm B the rail word "Results" took her there
  first time for starting a run and for its set-up.

- Comparing a group with the rest is not where she looks: selecting the group shows Appearance,
  Members and Notes, and nothing that compares.
- On IT's question she wants a list, not a sentence: the line under the project name reassured her,
  but she went on to Data > Sent and saved for evidence.
