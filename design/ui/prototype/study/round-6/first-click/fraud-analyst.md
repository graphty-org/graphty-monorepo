## What she said, prompt by prompt

**1. Picture for the paper.** "Nothing on this screen says export. Three lines in the corner is
where every program hides Save As and Export, so that's where I go. The little camera icons next
to 'The whole novel' made me look twice, but those look like saved bookmarks, not a picture I can
paste in a file." (4)

**2. Repeat the bridges calculation.** "The only place I see the word 'bridges' is the 'Bridges
off' line on the right. So I click that and hope it tells me how it was set up. 'Results' on the
left might have it too, but that is a flask icon, and I'd get to it second." (4)

**3. Rank a second way.** "In Excel I'd add a column and sort by it. There's a table, and a '...'
next to it, so I'd look there for 'add column'. I would not guess a flask called 'Results' is where
you start a new calculation -- results is what you get after, not where you begin." (3)

**4. Get the picked-out characters back.** "Ctrl+Z. Before I've even read anything. I saw the black
box that says 'Bring it back' once I looked -- that's fine, that's the same thing -- but my hand is
already on the keyboard. If Ctrl+Z doesn't do it, I'd be annoyed, then I'd notice the button." (5)

**5. Where Valjean's number comes from.** "Right panel, under Results: 'betweenness 0.57, highest'.
That's the number. I'd click it and expect it to tell me how it was worked out. If it just shows me
the number again, it's a score without a reason and I can't use it." (5)

**6. Next month's file.** "It says 'Loaded: transfers-2026-03.csv' with 'Change...' right after
it. That's the file, that's the change button. The little grey file tab at the top with the name
cut off -- I saw it, but I read the full sentence first. What I actually worry about is whether
'Change' throws away everything I set up. It doesn't say." (4)

**7. In far more than out.** "That's a pivot: total in, total out, by account. The Table at the
bottom is the only thing that looks like rows. I open that. 'Quick actions' sounds like a sales
demo button." (5)

**8. Cheapest route, bigger transfer costs more.** "It literally says 'amount not used yet.
Change...'. So first I have to make it use the amount, and that's the link. The route part comes
after. The icon with two circles and a line on the toolbar might be a path thing but it has no
label and I don't click unlabelled icons on a case." (4)

**9. Has anything left the computer?** "'Nothing has been sent from this project', underlined, under
the name. Good. I'd click it to get something I can screenshot for IT. The left rail also says
'Nothing is sent' under Assistant -- that's twice, which I like." (6)

**10. Two blues.** "The legend. Ribosome, light blue, 56. Click the colour square. That's where I'd
change it in Excel too, click the series in the legend." (5)

**11. Lab's standard colours file.** "It's a file, so 'Data' on the left. Maybe the three-line
menu. I wouldn't think of the plus next to 'Style stack' -- I don't know what a style stack is. Low
confidence." (3)

**12. How Ribosome differs.** "I'd want the numbers side by side: Ribosome versus everyone else. Table
at the bottom, filter to Ribosome, compare. Clicking the word in the legend might do something, but
the legend is a key, not a button, as far as I know." (3)

**13. One protein's name always shows.** "First I have to find the protein. The first magnifier I
see is next to 'Graphs' on the left, so I type the name in there. I did read '7 more hidden where
they overlap' after, but it didn't say which seven or that I could click them." (3)

**14. Run again, keep this one.** "'Re-run'. What I'm not sure of is whether Re-run overwrites this
one. 'Compare with...' is sitting right beside it and pulls at you, because the task says compare.
But you can't compare with something you haven't run yet, so Re-run first. If it overwrites my
first run without asking, that's my audit trail gone." (4)

## Her overall comment

"The words that are on the screen, I follow. 'Nothing has been sent', 'Loaded', 'Change...', the
legend with counts -- I read those and click them. The icons with no words -- the flask, the two
circles, the lightning -- I skip. So when the right answer is an icon, I miss it, and when a text
link is sitting nearby that sounds close enough, I take it. Twice I clicked 'Change...' on the
loaded line because it was the only thing that mentioned the file or the amount. If that link can't
do the job, it should at least tell me where to go."
