## What the misses say

- **"Change..." on the Loaded line drew two different tasks** (a new month's file, and a weighted route). It sits beside the file name, so she reads it as "swap the file"; and it sits beside "amount not used yet", so she reads it as the place to make the amount count. Both readings are reasonable from the wording. For the route task she was not wrong that the amount has to become a weight first -- she just started there instead of at a path tool.
- **"Bridges off" was the only place the word "bridges" appeared.** With nothing on the rail or in Results naming the bridges run, the style layer was the only matching word on screen, so she clicked it expecting the run's settings behind it.
- **Low confidence on the transfers and module tasks** (3 each) comes from vocabulary: nothing on the transfers screen says "in" or "out", and nothing on the protein screen says "compare" or "enrichment". She found a correct target by elimination, not recognition.
- **Unlabelled toolbar icons were never her first click.** The lightning and path icons on the Les Miserables screen were skipped every time; "Quick actions" with its word label on the other screens was clicked.

Her standing doubts, unprompted: whether the exported picture carries the legend (prompt 1), what "betweenness" and "highest" mean (prompt 5), whether swapping a file keeps her style (prompt 6), whether IT would accept a sentence instead of a list (prompt 9), and whether Re-run overwrites the earlier result (prompt 14).
