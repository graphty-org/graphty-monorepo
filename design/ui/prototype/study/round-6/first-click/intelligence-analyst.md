# First-click test -- Marcus, criminal intelligence analyst

Marcus is a state fusion center analyst who builds link charts in i2 Analyst's Notebook and
lives in Excel. For each prompt he looked at one still screen and said the one thing he would
click first, with how sure he was (1 = guessing, 7 = certain). His answers were scored against
the correct targets afterwards and were not changed.

## Answers

### 1. A picture of the Les Miserables network for a co-author's paper
- Click: the three-line menu button at the top left.
- Sure: 4
- Said: "In every program I use, export lives under File. That button is the only thing that
  looks like a File menu. The little camera icons next to the saved views had me for a second --
  a camera means a picture -- but those look like bookmarks, not an export."
- Score: correct.

### 2. Repeat the earlier bridges calculation exactly -- how was it set up?
- Click: "Bridges off" in the Style stack on the right.
- Sure: 4
- Said: "That's the only place on the screen that says Bridges. I'd expect clicking it to open
  whatever made it. 'Results' on the left is a beaker; I didn't connect a beaker with a record of
  how something was run."
- Score: wrong (that is a style layer, not the run's record).

### 3. Rank the characters a second way, to check against the first
- Click: Results on the left rail.
- Sure: 4
- Said: "Degree is one way. For another measure I'd want a list of calculations, and Results is
  the nearest word to that. The lightning bolt on the toolbar has no label, I wouldn't try it
  first."
- Score: correct.

### 4. A stray click cleared the characters you picked out -- get them back
- Click: "Bring it back" on the dark notice above the toolbar.
- Sure: 7
- Said: "It's sitting right there and it says exactly what I want. I wouldn't even think about
  Ctrl+Z with that in front of me."
- Score: correct under both undo designs (the notice-only design and the Ctrl+Z restore design).

### 5. Where does Valjean's betweenness number come from, and how was it calculated?
- Click: the "betweenness 0.57, highest" row under Results in the right panel.
- Sure: 5
- Said: "Valjean is selected and that's his number. If I'm going to be asked on the stand how
  it was computed, I'd click the number itself and expect it to tell me. If it doesn't, I'd try
  the column header in the table."
- Score: correct.

### 6. Next month's transfers file has arrived -- bring it in so the setup carries over
- Click: "Change..." at the end of the "Loaded: transfers-2026-03.csv" line.
- Sure: 4
- Said: "It names the file that's loaded now and says Change right after it. Changing the file is
  what I'm doing. I'd worry it throws away my setup, but that's where I'd start."
- Score: wrong (that link changes how the current file is read, not which file).

### 7. The accounts that take in far more money than they send out
- Click: the Table strip at the bottom of the screen.
- Sure: 4
- Said: "This is a pivot table job. Money in, money out, subtract, sort. The picture is a grey
  blob anyway -- I'd go to the rows."
- Score: correct.

### 8. Cheapest route between two accounts, where a bigger transfer costs more
- Click: "Change..." on the "Loaded" line.
- Sure: 4
- Said: "It says 'amount not used yet'. The route has to use the amount, so first I have to tell
  it to use the amount. The icon next to the arrow on the toolbar might be a path tool, but it
  has no label and I'd be guessing."
- Score: wrong (counted separately as the "Change..." lure).

### 9. IT asks whether anything from this project has left your computer
- Click: the underlined line under the project name, "Nothing has been sent from this project".
- Sure: 6
- Said: "That's the first thing I'd want to see on any web tool, and it's already there. I'd click
  it to get something I can screenshot for IT."
- Score: correct.

### 10. Ribosome and Spliceosome are two blues you can't tell apart -- change one
- Click: the Spliceosome color square in the legend.
- Sure: 6
- Said: "That's where the colors are listed. On a black-and-white printer those two would be the
  same anyway."
- Score: correct.

### 11. The lab's file of standard colors and sizes -- bring it into this project
- Click: the three-line menu button at the top left.
- Sure: 3
- Said: "Bringing a file in is an Import, and Import is under File. The paint palette icon next
  to Graph was tempting since it's about colors, but it looks like it would just change a
  background."
- Score: correct.

### 12. How does the Ribosome module differ from the rest of the network?
- Click: "Ribosome" in the legend.
- Sure: 3
- Said: "I'd pick out the Ribosome group first and hope it tells me something about it. I don't
  really know what 'differ' would look like here -- I'm not sure I'd find it."
- Score: correct.

### 13. One protein you care about has no name showing -- make its name always show
- Click: the Table strip at the bottom, to find the protein by name.
- Sure: 3
- Said: "I know its name, I don't know which dot it is. So I'd find it in the rows first. I saw
  '7 more hidden where they overlap' in the legend but didn't take it as something to click."
- Score: correct.

### 14. Run the betweenness calculation again with one setting changed, and keep this one to compare
- Click: "Compare with..." under the run's settings.
- Sure: 3
- Said: "Re-run sounds like it overwrites what I have. The word compare is in what I'm trying to
  do, so I'd start there and expect it to let me set up the second one."
- Score: wrong (counted separately: it compares runs that already exist).

## Tally

- Correct: 10 of 14 (prompts 1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13).
- Wrong: 4 (prompts 2, 6, 8, 14).
- Prompt 4 scores correct under both undo designs, because he clicked the notice.
- Two of the four misses went to the same link: "Change..." on the "Loaded" line on the transfers
  screen (prompts 6 and 8). The words "Loaded: <filename> ... Change" read to him as "swap the
  file", and "amount not used yet" read as a step he must do before any money calculation.
- The other two misses followed a matching word: "Bridges" in the Style stack for the bridges
  run, and "Compare with..." for a task that said "compare".
- Unlabeled icons (the lightning bolt, the path icon) were never his first choice; he picked the
  labeled "Quick actions" nowhere either, preferring the table or the main menu.
