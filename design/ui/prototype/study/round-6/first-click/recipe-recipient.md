himself. He looked at one still screen per prompt and named the one thing he would click first,
with how sure he was (1 = guessing, 7 = certain). Answers were given before seeing the correct
targets, and were not changed afterwards.

## In his words

**1. Picture for the paper.** "Honestly I'd ask her for a PNG. If I have to do it myself, File.
There's no File, so that three-line thing at the top, that's usually where File lives. The little
camera next to 'The whole novel' looks like a picture too, but I don't know what that saves."

**2. The bridges calculation.** "The only place I see the word 'bridges' is on the right, 'Bridges
off'. So that. 'Off' worries me a bit. Is the result wrong now, or just not showing? I wouldn't
have thought of the 'Results' thing on the left, that's just an icon with a flask."

**3. Rank a second way.** "The table's sorted by degree. I'd click the column heading and hope it
lets me pick a different number. I don't know what the other ways are called, that's her
department."

**4. Get them back.** "It says right there, 'Selection cleared, Bring it back'. I'd click that
before it goes away. I don't use keyboard shortcuts."

**5. Valjean's betweenness.** "There's the number, 0.57, highest, under Results. I'd click that
and hope it tells me in English what it is. How it was calculated, I'd probably ask her."

**6. Next month's file.** "First I'd just drag the file onto the window. If I have to click, the
little file name under the title, that's the file that's in here now. There's also 'Change...'
on the right. I'm nervous about that one, 'change' sounds like it would throw away what she set
up."

**7. Money in versus out.** "That's a manager question, not mine. 'Quick actions' is the only
button that says words, so there. Not sure at all."

**8. Cheapest route.** "It says 'amount not used yet' right there next to 'Change...'. The cost
depends on the amount, so I'd guess I have to switch the amount on first. So 'Change...'. The
curly line button on the toolbar, I don't know what that is."

**9. IT's question.** "That's the first thing I'd want to know, and it's right under the title:
'Nothing has been sent from this project'. It's underlined, so I'd click it to see the proof.
The grey bit on the left says 'Nothing is sent' too. That's what I'd show IT."

**10. Two blues.** "The legend. Click Spliceosome's little square and hope it lets me pick a
colour. To me both of those are just blue. At least the names are next to them."

**11. The lab's colour file.** "She'd tell me 'drop it on', so first I'd drag it onto the window.
Clicking, I'd go to the menu at the top left, File, Open or Import. The paint palette icon on
the right crossed my mind, but that looks like it's for the background."

**12. Ribosome versus the rest.** "Click 'Ribosome' in the legend and see what happens. I don't
know what 'differ' would even show me. A number, I hope."

**13. A protein's name.** "The legend says '7 more hidden where they overlap'. That's probably
mine, so I'd click that line. I couldn't find the one dot out of 300 anyway."

**14. Run again, keep this one.** "'Re-run' sounds like it would write over this one, and I want
to keep it. 'Compare with...' says compare, and that's what I want at the end. So that one."

## What tripped him up

- Where the screen names a thing in plain words that match his question, he found it fast and
  was sure: the "Nothing has been sent" line (7 of 7), "Bring it back" (6), the betweenness row
  and the legend (5).
- Where the words on screen pointed somewhere else, he followed the words, not the structure.
  "Bridges off" in the style list read as "the bridges thing"; "amount not used yet" beside
  "Change..." read as the switch for cost by amount; "Compare with..." read as "keep this one
  and compare". He never considered the Results icon on the left rail for a calculation he had
  not run himself.
- He is afraid of words that sound like they discard the sender's setup: "Change..." for the new
  file, "Re-run" for a run he wants to keep.
- For bringing a file in, his first move is always dragging it onto the window, before any
  click.
