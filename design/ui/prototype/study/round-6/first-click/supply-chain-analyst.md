## What she said, prompt by prompt

1. "A picture for a paper is an export. I don't see an Export button anywhere, so it's
   the menu at top left, like File in Excel. The little camera icons next to the saved views
   made me look twice, but they seem to be saved screens, not a picture."

2. "The only place I see the word 'bridges' is that 'Bridges off' line on the right. So I'd
   click that. I wouldn't think to go to 'Results' on the left -- nothing there says bridges."

3. "Second ranking means another column to sort by. Tables are where I'd do it, so the '...'
   on the table. I don't know what 'Results' is supposed to hold until I've got some."

4. "That's easy: it says 'Selection cleared' and there's 'Bring it back' right there. In
   Excel I'd hit Ctrl+Z, but the button is in front of me, so I'd use it."

5. "It says 0.57, highest, under Results. I'd click that and hope it tells me how. I still
   want the reason, not just a number -- a score with no explanation doesn't go on a slide."

6. "The file name is on the Loaded line and right next to it is 'Change...', in blue. New
   month's file, change the file. That's what I'd click. The grey chip at top left is cut
   off, I didn't read it as something to click."

7. "Money in versus money out is a table job. I'd open that Table strip at the bottom and
   look for in and out amounts to subtract. If I can't, I'm exporting to Excel."

8. "It says 'amount not used yet'. If bigger transfers cost more, the amount has to be
   used, so I'd click 'Change...' first to switch the amount on. The icons on the toolbar
   don't have names; I wouldn't guess which is a route."

9. "That's the first thing I'd look for, and it's right under the name: 'Nothing has been
   sent from this project'. I'd click it to get something I can show IT."

10. "Click the colour square next to Ribosome in the key, and pick another colour. I'd
    expect that to just work."

11. "Our standard colours file -- that's an import, so the main menu. I don't know what the
    little palette icon on the right does and I wouldn't click a paint icon to import."

12. "I'd click 'Ribosome' in the key to pick those out, and then look for something that
    compares. I'm not sure what 'module' means here, but the name is the handle I have."

13. "First I'd find the protein. The only search box I see is that magnifier at top left, so
    I'd type its name there. I read the legend's first line about labels and skipped the
    rest."

14. "There's a 'Re-run' button. My worry is it overwrites the one I have, which is exactly
    what I don't want. 'Compare with...' sounds tempting, but I don't have a second run yet,
    so Re-run first and then check the old one is still there."

## Her overall reaction

- The line "Nothing has been sent from this project" is the most useful thing on these
  screens for her. It is the first answer she needs before IT will approve anything.
- She follows words, not icons. Where the right target is an unlabelled toolbar icon (the
  route tool, the lightning button on the Les Miserables screen) or a rail section whose name
  does not match her task ("Results" for "bridges"), she went to whatever visible text matched
  the words in the task instead: "Bridges off", "Change...", "amount not used yet".
- A blue "Change..." link beside the file name reads to her as "replace this file". She took
  it twice, once for a new month's file and once to switch on the amount.
