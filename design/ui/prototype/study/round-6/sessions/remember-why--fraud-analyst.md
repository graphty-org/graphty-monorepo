# Remember why -- Sarah, fraud investigator

Participant: Sarah, level-2 financial crime investigator at a mid-size bank, eight years in. Works
four to six escalated cases at a time. Usual stack: the bank's case-management system (its case
notes are the audited record of what she did), Excel for the statement pivot, Word for the SAR
narrative and for a per-case "scratch sheet" of notes to herself, i2 Analyst's Notebook a few
times a year.

Mode: not mandated. She is trying the tool on her own initiative, with her usual patience of about
five minutes for a first real task.

Task as given by the moderator: "You kept these accounts aside for a reason. Leave yourself a note
on why, so you remember next week."

Screens seen, all in the participant view (design notes hidden):

- the take-a-note screen in eight states (the March transfers project, a kept set of 14 accounts
  called "Mule ring");
- the notes panel screen in eleven states (a protein project, a different dataset, used here only
  for how the notes list, its menu, find, stale citations and the read-only view behave);
- the sets-and-paths screen, one state (the same March transfers project with three kept sets).

Renders: shots/r6-sarah-remember-tan-s1.png to -s8.png (take a note),
shots/r6-sarah-remember-np-s1.png to -s11.png (notes panel), shots/r6-sarah-remember-sp.png
(sets and paths).

Outcome: success. She wrote the note in the place offered and found it again next week from the
notes list; the Enter key that cut her note in half last time now makes a new line. What still
stopped her short of trusting it: the editor says it is saving under someone else's name, the
note she is handed already carries text and a citation she did not write, she still cannot quote
the transfers her reason rests on, and nothing on any screen gets a note out into her case file.

## Observations for the studio

1. "Saving as: Marcus. Change..." appears in the note editor while she writes. She is not
   Marcus. She read it as her note being filed under someone else's name -- an audit-trail
   problem on a SAR case -- and would stop to fix it before typing, without knowing where
   "Change..." leads. The saved note then shows no name, so she cannot confirm the change took.
   She expects the author to come from who is signed in, or to be asked once. Severity: serious.
2. Quote a value still offers only values of the note's accounts (riskScore, country, PageRank).
   Her reason for keeping the accounts is the transfers between them -- amounts, dates, the
   shared device, the common beneficiary -- and none of that can be quoted, so the figures an
   examiner will ask her to trace are typed by hand. Unchanged from last round. Severity:
   serious.
3. Getting notes out: a note's menu holds only Edit note and Delete note; nothing copies, prints
   or exports a note, and "findings reports" still means nothing to her. She would try Ctrl+C on
   the note text first. Severity: moderate.
4. The composer opens with text and a PageRank citation already in it. With "Saving as: Marcus"
   below, she now reads it as a colleague's draft rather than the tool's own words, which
   alarmed her less, but she still clears both before writing, because she cannot explain the
   citation. Severity: moderate.
5. "Add current value" on a stale citation: she still cannot tell whether it adds the April
   value beside March's or replaces it, and would not press it on a note tied to a filed report.
   She asks for a way to mark a note closed so the link goes away. Severity: moderate.
6. Find in notes with no match still shows an empty list with no message; she could not tell
   "nothing found" from "broken". For her the find box is where an account number goes.
   Severity: minor.
7. The stale-citation mark reads "Earlier data" on the transactions screen and "Earlier run" on
   the protein screen; she wondered whether they were two different problems. Severity: minor.
8. The notes list puts the date first on the transactions screen and "About ..." first on the
   protein screen; she skims by date. Severity: minor.
9. Half her notes are to-dos; there is still no way to mark one done, so she would edit "DONE"
   into the text. Severity: minor.
10. Vocabulary: "degree 907" in a note, "damping 0.85, unweighted" in a citation, "Detached" for
    a changed set. She would write "907 counterparties" and understood "Detached" only from the
    sentence under it. Severity: minor.

What worked, in her words:

- "Enter, new line. Ctrl+Enter adds the note. That fixes the thing I was angriest about."
- "Nothing has been sent from this project. That's the first sentence I'd want my IT person to
  read."

- A view-only copy with the same picture and her notes is what she would send her reviewer.
