# Leave yourself a note on why you kept these accounts -- Marcus, criminal intelligence analyst

**Task, as the moderator gave it:** "You kept these accounts aside for a reason. Leave yourself a
note on why, so you remember next week." (The same task and wording as the previous round.)

**Participant:** Marcus, criminal intelligence analyst at a state fusion center. Works in i2
Analyst's Notebook and Excel; every fact he records needs a source, a grade and a date. Gives a
new tool about five minutes, reads labels and error text word for word, skims everything else.
He did this task on the previous version of these screens and remembers what went wrong then.

**Screens used, as the participant saw them (design notes hidden):** the take-a-note screen
("March transfers", a 14-account set called "Mule ring"), all eight states; the notes panel
(shown on a second project, "Human protein interactions", because that mock uses that data);
the sets-and-paths screen, first state. Renders: shots/r6-marcus-remember-take-s1.png to -s8.png,
shots/r6-marcus-remember-notes-s1.png, -s2, -s3, -s5, -s6, -s7, -s10, -s11.png,
shots/r6-marcus-remember-sets-s1.png.

**Outcome:** done, first try, no wrong turns. **Single Ease Question:** 5 of 7.
**Would he use it instead of his current tool:** beside it, for the working notes; not as the
record, until it carries a source and a grade.

## Single Ease Question

**5 of 7.** Up from 4. The note landed on the right fourteen accounts first try, Enter made a
new line, the dates held still, and it knew who I was. It loses points for: "Add note" buried
last under three dots with no Notes section on the set until a note exists; no source or grade
field; my name hidden on the note itself; a blank list when the search finds nothing; "frozen"
and "Cite" meaning something other than what they mean in my shop.

## Would he use this instead of his current tool?

"Beside it, and closer to instead than last time. For 'why did I keep these', it beats a text
box on an i2 chart: it's stuck to the accounts, it keeps the number I was looking at, and it
tells me when the data under it moved. My case notes in Word still carry the source and the
grade, so Word stays the record. Give me a grade field and my name on every printed note and
I'd stop keeping the Word file for this."

## In his own words, afterwards

"They fixed the Enter key and the dates. That's the two I'd have quit over. Now put Notes on the
set's panel where I can see it, give me a source and a grade that print, and put my name on
what I wrote whether or not anybody else ever opens the file."
