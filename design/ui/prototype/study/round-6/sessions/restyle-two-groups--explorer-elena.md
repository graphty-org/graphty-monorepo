## Observations for the studio (moderator notes, not the participant)

1. **The swatch route works once found, and the picker's "Not used in this layer" row is the
   strongest thing on these screens for this persona.** She named it as the whole answer ("a color
   nobody else has") and saw the toast's Undo without prompting. This is the improvement over the
   previous round's route through the Style stack row and the layer editor.
2. **She still found the swatch third.** Order of attempts: a canvas dot (selects one node), the
   legend name (selects the group, per the decided behaviour), the "color" tag on the inspector's
   Appearance row (inert), then the swatch. Nothing on the at-rest legend distinguishes the swatch
   as a separate, clickable target from the name; on this persona the name's larger target wins
   first. No hint was needed this time, but two dead ends preceded it.
3. **The "color" tag on an Appearance row still reads as a button** (inspector, group selected).
   Same finding as the previous round; it drew a click and did nothing.
4. **No flag on the problem she was asked to fix.** The at-rest Les Miserables legend shows two
   oranges (groups 2 and 3) and two blues (1 and 8) with no too-close mark, because the flag covers
   only colors the reader picked. She read the flag on the legend page as "the tool checks for
   close colors", then asked why it had not flagged her pair. The design's rule is invisible and
   she inferred the opposite.
5. **The after-state contradicts the flag she just learned.** On the run-colors page, the picked
   dark gold for Community 8 sits beside Community 1's orange and Community 5's red-orange, with
   no flag, while the legend page flags a picked light blue as too close to sky blue. Both are
   reader picks. Either the dark gold clears the shared distance test (then say nothing, but the
   mock invites the doubt) or the mock is missing its flag. She blamed herself first ("I probably
   picked the wrong kind of gold"), then the tool ("when does it warn?").
6. **"Fix..." is unwired.** She clicked it expecting the tool to choose a replacement color by
   itself; she said she would not follow it into another settings panel.
7. **The value-row menu has no visible opener** (categories editor). "Change color..." lives in a
   menu she could not see how to open; on a trackpad she would not right-click.
8. **Mixed samples again.** The task starts on Les Miserables and every recolor state is drawn on
   the protein sample. She had to "pretend" three times and asked to see her own picture after the
   change.
9. **Misreading, not corrected by the screens:** she read the black group 0 as "the loners, people
   who only show up once". Nothing on the legend contradicts it.
10. **"Other" understood this time.** The line under Other ("Groups 6, 7 and 10") and the
    categories editor's "Other: Unassigned moved" together told her Other is a pile, not a group.
11. **Mock defect:** the inspector page at rest overflows at 1440 wide; the right panel is cut off
    ("Change..", "0.028", "undirecte", "1 to 3"). Present in the previous round too.
