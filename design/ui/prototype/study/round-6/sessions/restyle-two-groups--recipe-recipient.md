Task as given: "Two of the groups are drawn in colors you cannot tell apart. Fix that so a
colleague can read the picture."

Participant: Tom, lab manager, 52, mild red-green color weakness. He opens files the postdoc
sends; he does not build them. Laptop at 1440 by 900.

## Observer notes (what he did and where it went wrong)

- First try succeeded: he clicked the swatch in the legend because it is the only place the color
  is written. The picker opening beside the legend with the clicked swatch ringed matched his
  expectation. No failure counted.
- The legend swatch at rest (Les Miserables screen) has no visible affordance; he said it "doesn't
  look like a button" and tried it only for lack of an alternative. He also said he might click the
  group name first, which opens the group's inspector, not a color control.
- The picker is shown only on the protein network; the Les Miserables screen does not draw it. He
  noticed the switch ("that's not my picture") and needed the moderator to bridge it.
- The picker gives no help choosing a color that stays apart from the others for a red-green
  reader. The "Used in this layer" row again shows the two oranges side by side.
- The default palette's own orange pair (groups 2 and 3) carried no warning when he opened the
  file; he read that as the tool not knowing about his color weakness. He could not tell whether
  "Too close" means close for anyone or close for him.
- "Fix..." on the too-close flag: he would press it, but did not know what it would change (his
  pick, or the other group's color).
- The notice "Proteasome is now light blue, was orange ... Undo" was the strongest moment of the
  session: old color, new color and Undo in words.
- Whether the change is his copy or the sender's file stayed unanswered. "Nothing has been sent
  from this project" answered a different question (sending, not changing).
- The Look menu's Print look names color-blind readers but promises gray; High contrast promises
  contrast against the canvas, not between two colors. "For the whole project" made him avoid it.
- "Okabe-Ito" in the Libraries tab meant nothing to him; he said he would press a palette labelled
  as safe for red-green.
