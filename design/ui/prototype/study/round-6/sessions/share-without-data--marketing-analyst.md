# Share your setup with a partner, without your data -- Jordan, marketing network analyst

Participant: Jordan, a growth-marketing analyst who does "the network stuff" one or two days a
week (see study/personas/marketing-analyst.md). Moderator's task, word for word: "Share your
setup with a partner, without your data."

The mock's project is a lab's protein study (300 proteins, colored by fold change). Jordan was
asked to pretend it was her creator map.

## Setting it up in my head

"OK. Real version: the agency running our creator outreach wants to run the same thing I ran --
bridges, cluster colours -- on their own creator list. I cannot send them our CRM export, legal
would end me. So I send them the settings. The how, not the who."

"And this is a protein thing. Fine. Genes are creators, I'll squint."

## Step 1 -- where do I start

"Top right. Share is always top right. Google, Figma, Canva, Notion -- top right. Here it's...
a zoom percent. 100%. That's it. OK, so no Share button. Again."

"Left, the Data section has an Export... button. And the project name has a little arrow. Let me
try the arrow first, because Export to me means a picture."

(Opens the project-name menu.)

"Open, Update with new data, Export..., Download project file, Version history, Project info,
Rename, Duplicate, Close. Still no Share."

"Hm. 'Download project file.' Is that it? The project file is... my project. The setup. That's
kind of what I want? But 'project' in every tool I use means everything, data included. I'm not
clicking something that might dump the customer list into a file I then email to an agency. I'll
take Export and see what it offers."

Moderator note: about 20 seconds in the top right and 10 seconds hovering over "Download project
file" before choosing Export... She did not click Download project file, but she said she would
have if she had been in a hurry and the partner were internal.

## Step 2 -- the Export dialog

"Big one. Figures, Rows, Report, Graph data... 'Share the setup, without data.' There it is.
That's the sentence. Why is that sentence in here and not on a button outside? Anyway."

"Recipe, .graphty. 'Readable text (JSON) with styles, steps and layout. It never holds your
data.' Recipe. Weird word, but it's the instructions not the ingredients, I get it. JSON -- I
don't care if it's readable, I'm not reading it. I care it's not our customers."

(Ticks Recipe.)

"And everything else goes grey and there's a padlock bar: figures, images, tables, the report,
graph data and the project file are off because they would carry my data. Good. So the project
file DOES carry data. Glad I didn't click that menu thing. That bar is the most useful thing on
this screen -- I don't have to think about whether a PNG counts as data. It just won't let me."

"Scope, greyed out, 'A recipe takes definitions, not a scope of data.' I don't know what that
means. It's grey, I'm not fighting it."

## Step 3 -- reading what goes out

"'No data inside. No genes, interactions, fold-change values, positions or notes on genes. Only
how to build the analysis again on someone else's data.' OK. In my world: no handles, no emails,
no follower counts. That box is the screenshot I send legal. Honestly that alone saves me a
meeting."

"Travels: 3 runs. PageRank, damping 0.85, weighted by confidence. Louvain, resolution 1, seed 7.
Degree, exact. So the agency gets the same influence score and the same clusters method, with
the seed. Good -- so when their clusters don't match mine it's their data, not a coin flip."

"Style layers 2, a filter, the layout with its settings and seed, the view. Fine."

"'1 note, on the confidence filter' -- and it shows me the whole note. Wait, notes go? My notes
say stuff like 'this creator is under contract with a competitor, do NOT contact'. OK, it shows me
the full text of the one that goes, and '4 notes on genes' are under Not included. So a note on a
person stays home, a note on a step travels. I think that's the rule. I'd still rather have a
little checkbox next to the note than work out the rule. If I'd written something rude on the
filter, it'd be going to the agency."

"You supply: gene symbols to join a table, a fold change above and below 0, a confidence per
interaction. 'You.' Me? I already have all that. Oh -- it means the agency. It's telling me what
THEY need to bring. So I'd have to write that into the email myself. 'Dear agency, you need a
column of IDs and a column of numbers.' Which is exactly the email I wanted this to write for me."

"Not included: 300 proteins, 1,262 interactions, fold-change values, knockdown hits, positions,
4 notes. Positions -- so their map won't look like mine. Right, it's their network, it shouldn't."

## Step 4 -- name and export

"Name: 'Expression overlay'. I'd call it 'Bridge creators Q3'. File underneath says
expression-overlay.graphty -- I hope that changes when I rename it, or the agency gets a file
called expression overlay and thinks I sent the wrong thing."

"Footer: '1 file goes to your Downloads folder. Nothing is uploaded.' Good. That's my laptop
question answered before I asked. And the rail on the left says 'Assistant Off. Nothing is
sent.' Fine, same message, twice. I'll take it."

"Export 1 file. And then... it's in Downloads. I attach it to an email. For an outside agency,
honestly, that's what I want -- I'm not giving them a login to our stuff. But no link, no 'send
to', no 'copy a note for them'."

(Moderator shows the project after an export.) "The toast says it exported... a figure SVG. That's
somebody else's export, not mine, but OK -- I assume my recipe shows up in 'Sent and saved' on
the left the same way. On the other project's Data panel there's a line up top, 'Nothing has been
sent from this project', and 'Every file an export writes is listed here.' Good, so I can prove
later what I sent. That's actually a thing legal asks: what did you send them and when."

"What I still don't know: what does the agency do with a dot graphty file? Do they need an
account? Does it cost them? Do they need to install something, get procurement to sign off? The
dialog says 'Opens in graphty or any app with graphty-element.' Graphty-element. I don't know
what that is. My agency won't either. That's the first question their account manager asks."

## Step 5 -- what the partner sees (shown by the moderator, after the task)

"OK, so they open it and it's a card: 'Recipe waiting for data. Saved by Maren on Sep 26.
Expects: a protein network... your own table of genes, a gene id per row and a fold change.'
And a padlock: 'Data stays on this computer. This recipe names no server.' Big Add data button.
That's good. Their junior could do that."

"Then Apply, and it says '84 of 96 genes matched', and it lists the 12 that didn't, with a
'use MDM2' fix for the capital-letter one. That's the thing I check first in any tool -- did my
known account come through. I like that more than I expected."

"Hold on, though. Up top of this board, the sender's preview is different from what I just
saw. Here there's a panel, 'The person who opens it sees', with an 'Expects' list and 'Where
each comes from', and a dropdown per input -- Their table, The network. That's the email I was
going to write! Why wasn't that in my dialog? Mine said 'You supply'. And in this one the
confidence comes from 'The network', but in mine confidence was under 'You supply'. So which is
it? Does the agency bring confidence or not? If I tell them the wrong thing they'll waste a day."

"Also the 'Travels' list here says overview readings 6, notes on definitions 1, and nothing about
the three runs, and mine said 3 runs with PageRank and Louvain. Same recipe? Two different
summaries? This is my whole thing with Talkwalker -- the dashboard says 4,000 and the download
says 3,100. If the tool can't describe the same file the same way twice, I start wondering what's
actually in it."

Moderator note: the Export dialog she used and the sender preview drawn in the recipe
storyboard describe the same recipe differently -- different Travels contents, "You supply"
versus "The person who opens it sees", and confidence as supplied versus taken from the network.
She raised this without prompting, the same disagreement she raised last round.

Off-topic drift: "The agency's list won't even be handles anyway. It'll be whatever internal ID
their listening tool spits out. Brandwatch does that to us too since they lost half of
Instagram. So first time, I promise you, it's 'nothing matched'. At least this one would tell
them why."

## Single Ease Question

5 out of 7. "Once I was in Export it was easy: the heading said my task in my words and it locked
out everything with data in it, so I couldn't mess up. But I burned time looking for Share, I
nearly clicked 'Download project file' thinking that was the setup, and I still have to write
the partner an email about what columns to bring -- and after seeing the other version I'm not
even sure the columns list I'd copy is right."

## Would you use this instead of your current tool?

"For this job, yes. My current tool for this is a screenshot of Gephi's settings panel and a Word
doc of what I clicked, and the agency never gets the same clusters. A file that carries the
method and the seed, that says 'no data inside' in a box I can show legal, and that tells me
afterwards what I sent -- that beats what I have. But I'm not switching my whole workflow for
it, and I wouldn't send it to an agency until I could tell them in one line what they need to
open it and whether it costs anything. Right now the answer on screen is 'graphty-element', which
means nothing to them."

## What worked for her

- The dialog heading "Share the setup, without data" matched the task in her own words.
- Ticking Recipe turned off every output that would carry data and said why in one bar, which
  also told her that the project file carries data -- after she had nearly used it to share.
- "No data inside" first in the preview, "Nothing is uploaded" in the footer: the
  leave-my-laptop question answered before she asked.
- The runs listed with their settings and seed, so she could tell the partner's clusters would
  differ only because of the data.
- The Data panel's record of what was sent and saved, which she read as proof for legal.
- On the partner's side, the "Data stays on this computer" line and the match report that names
  the rows that did not match.

## Problems observed

1. No Share entry point. She looked top right first, where every web tool she uses puts Share,
   then in the project-name menu, and reached the recipe only through Export..., a word she
   associates with pictures and tables. Severity 2.
2. "Download project file" in the project-name menu looks like the way to share the setup. The
   Export dialog says the project file carries all the data, but the menu entry does not; she
   nearly used it and said she would have for an internal partner in a hurry. Severity 3.
3. The dialog's "You supply" column is addressed to the sender, but lists what the recipient
   must bring. She read "You" as herself, then planned to write the partner an email with that
   list. The recipe storyboard draws a sender preview with "The person who opens it sees" and
   "Expects", which is the text she wanted; the dialog she used does not have it. Severity 3.
4. The same recipe is summarized two ways: the dialog lists 3 runs and says confidence is
   supplied; the storyboard's sender preview lists overview readings and a note on definitions,
   no runs, and says confidence comes from the network. She named this as the thing that makes
   her distrust a tool. Severity 3.
5. Nothing tells her what the partner needs to open a .graphty file: "Opens in graphty or any app
   with graphty-element" means nothing to a marketer or an agency, and there is no word on
   account, cost or install. Severity 2.
6. Which notes travel is a rule she had to infer (notes on steps go, notes on nodes stay); she
   wanted a per-note choice before a note leaves. Severity 2.
7. The Scope line, disabled, reads "A recipe takes definitions, not a scope of data"; she did not
   understand it and ignored it. Severity 1.
8. The Name field shows "Expression overlay" while the file line shows
   expression-overlay.graphty; she could not tell whether renaming changes the file name.
   Severity 1.
