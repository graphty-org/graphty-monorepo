## What the misses say

- **What a bigger amount means (tt-5).** She put it on the column, as a property of the data,
  because that is where she fixes meaning in her own master file. The path tool's options were
  never opened. A route setting that lives only inside the run is invisible to someone who
  thinks of units as belonging to the data.
- **A team's colors file (tt-7).** "Recipes" meant nothing to her and she skipped it on sight;
  a file goes in where files go in. The Style stack's "From a recipe or file..." sits two levels
  under a state of the right panel she never opened. If a colors file dropped on Data > Sources
  > Add a source were recognised and routed to styles, she would have succeeded.
- **Where money went next (tt-16).** "Neighbors" is not a money word. She rebuilt the trace by
  hand with a date filter in the table, one hop at a time. The date-and-direction option exists
  but is named in network terms she does not read.
