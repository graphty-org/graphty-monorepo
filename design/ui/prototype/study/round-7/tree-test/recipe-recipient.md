# Tree test, round 7 -- the recipe recipient (Tom, lab manager)

Tom gets network files from the lab's postdoc and reads them. He does not build them. In this
test he saw only the text outline of graphty's menus and panels. For each task he says, step by
step, where he would look, where he would stop, and how sure he is (1 = guessing, 7 = certain).
He reads headings and skips long lists. He avoids anything called Settings, Delete, Replace or
an algorithm name, and he does not use right-click menus.

---

## tree01 -- find the go-betweens between groups

Path: Toolbar > Analyze > "Find paths and edge sets".
Started at the rail's Graph list because it said "Rows added by Analyze", which told me analysis
lives under Analyze. Went to the toolbar's Analyze. Of the five choices, "Find groups" is about
groups, not the people between them. "Find paths" sounds like getting from one group to another,
so I picked that. "Rank nodes and edges" crossed my mind, but nothing says ranking gives you
go-betweens.
Ended at: Toolbar > Analyze > Find paths and edge sets.
Confidence: 3. "I'd probably ask her which one does go-betweens."
