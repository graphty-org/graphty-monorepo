# First-click test -- Dev, the student with a class project

Participant: a third-year history student in a digital-methods course, no programming, has
followed one Gephi tutorial video (import, layout, statistics, ranking, labels, preview, export).
Each answer is the one thing he would click first on a still picture, with his confidence from
1 (pure guess) to 7 (certain), in his own words.

## r8-fc01 -- see it working before my data is ready
Click: "Les Miserables" in the Samples column (the title or its little picture). Confidence 7.
"It literally says good for a first look and it has worked examples already added. That's what
I want -- see what done looks like. I'd also hit No thanks on the box at the bottom first, but
that's not really part of it."

## r8-fc02 -- bring in my spreadsheet from Downloads
Click: "Open project or file..." at the top of Start. Confidence 5.
"I want to open a file, so Open. But there's also New from data... and I honestly don't know
which one is for a spreadsheet. Project sounds like the program's own save file. If Open
didn't let me pick a CSV I'd try New from data next."

on most' -- I'd have to ask what PageRank means."
