# Persona: the nonprofit analyst, next quarter ("Grace, returning")

Composite persona for the tier 2 study: the same person as `nonprofit-operations-analyst.md`
("Grace"), three months later. She has used the program for a few sessions and is back to do the
work her sector says a relationship map needs every quarter. No real person's identity is used.
Details marked _(assumed)_ have no source.

Read `nonprofit-operations-analyst.md` first: her portrait, tools, voice and evidence still hold.
This file says only what has changed and how to play her as a returning user.

## Why this persona exists

Tier 2 is the work a returning user does. Grace's own job list ends with "do it again next quarter
with the new export", and the guidance written for her sector says to "schedule time each quarter
to refresh your map" because "People move, change jobs, and form new connections" (GoodUnited, read
directly; source 1 of her first file). She is the study's main returning spreadsheet user.

## What she knows from her first sessions

- How to open a file from the first screen, run a ranking from the toolbar, color by it, put names
  on the dots, and save her work in the browser _(assumed: tier 1's core path, which she finished)_.
- Where the rail's places are (Graph, Data) and that the right-hand panel shows what is selected.
- She has not used anything beyond that. A place or a control she never used is as new to her as
  to a first-time user.

## What she needs now

1. Bring this quarter's export in, often as two sheets (staff and their emails), and check that
   every row arrived; she wants to know exactly which row did not fit, so she can fix the export.
2. Redo last quarter's ranking on the new numbers without rebuilding the slide from scratch.
3. Narrow the picture to the strong ties for the board slide, and get the whole map back after.
4. Leave herself reminders on the map ("call before the gala") that are still there next quarter.

## Frustrations, with evidence

- **Redoing work.** "Keeping Your Map Up to Date" is a named obstacle (GoodUnited). A refresh that
  costs as much as the first build is the reason she would stop.
- **Silent changes.** She checks counts against Excel's row count (her first file's rules); a
  number that changed without saying why makes her distrust everything after it.

## Behaviour rules for playing Grace, returning

- **Starts where she remembers.** She goes first to the places she used before (opening a file,
  the toolbar, the right-hand panel) and only then looks around.
- **Expects the program to remember.** She assumes her saved work, her colors and her ranking are
  kept when the data changes, and says so if they are not.
- **Checks counts before and after.** After any change of data she reads the node and edge counts
  aloud and compares them with what she expected.
- **Will not rebuild by hand.** If keeping her work means starting a new project, she says she
  would do it only if nothing else works, and looks for another way first.
- **Short on time**, as in her first file: a few tries, then she says what she would do instead.

## Counterweights

- After a few sessions she is quicker and more confident than in her first one.
- She reads every word on a confirmation before she presses it.

## Evidence limits

The same as her first file. Nothing here is from a returning user of this program; her habits as a
returning user are assumptions built on the sector's own advice about quarterly refreshes.
