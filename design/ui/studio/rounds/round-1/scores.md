## What worked

- **No false "done" in 43 sessions.** Every claim matched the screen; participants read "N hidden
  to avoid overlap" and said names were missing. Round 8 had 10 of 21 false "done" on names.
- **The whole first session passes its bar:** 8 of 8 reached all five parts, on a sample and on an
  own file, with the legend in the exported picture.
- **Ranking, groups, loading and exporting are quick:** running-club ranking 3 of 3 at the path's
  length (ease 5.3); circles of characters 3 of 3 (ease 5.7); a CSV drawn on open and checked
  against "41 rows, 41 edges" 3 of 3; a picture and a CSV with the computed column in 5 steps, no
  wrong turn (ease 6).
- **Save and reopen** passed in 6 steps (ease 6).
- **Every run repainted the drawing and the legend at once**; no silent commit on any run, label
  or binding.
