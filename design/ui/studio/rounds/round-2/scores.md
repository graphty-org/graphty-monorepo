# Round 2 scores: the tier 1 study on the real graphty app

Scored against `criteria.md` from the graders' `grade.md` files in `sessions/` and their scripted
reproductions in `repro/`. Ease is each participant's own rating as asked (1 = very difficult,
7 = very easy), read from the transcript; it never decided a grade. Every row is in
`tmp/researcher/r2-score.py`, which prints the numbers below.

All participants are simulated: one model playing composite personas. A failure is strong evidence;
a pass is weak until real people confirm it. Screen-reader speech (what a real screen reader
announces, in what order, how verbosely) is not tested by this study; the screen-reader persona
is graded from the focused element and live-region text the tool prints, and the tool has no
browse (reading) mode.

**All 56 planned sessions ran and are graded; 0 are void.** Every session ran on commit 4a7a1a7fb
with no uncommitted changes (`session.json`), and every `session.log` is empty (no script errors,
no failed requests). 54 are tier 1 tasks; 2 are the first look (T16), measured, not graded.
**Build-decided:** 2, both the screen-reader persona's (r2-s07, r2-s47).

## What worked

- **The neighbors task passes on both halves** (8 of 8): the chevron on the Degree row drew 8 of 8
  to the list, including 17 names on Les Miserables, where round 1 had 1 of 3.
- **First-time personas 34 of 34**; core four success 33 of 34; tier 1 ease up from 4.50 to 4.98.
- **Loading, ranking, groups and the numbers file are quick:** running-club ranking 2 of 2 in 5-6
  steps (ease 6); circles of characters 2 of 2 in 8 (ease 6); a CSV checked against "41 rows, 41
  edges" 2 of 2; the picture and a CSV with the group column 2 of 2, no wrong turn.
- **The refusal of a broken file** (from "Open project or file...") gave cause, line and remedy in
  one step; all 3 succeeded, including the screen-reader persona, ease 6.
- **Save and reopen** 3 of 3; Control+S, a plain name dialog and a recent list with name, place,
  node count and time.
- **The keyboard-only persona passed all three** core tasks; the new focus ring was visible on
  most controls.
- **No false "done" in 56 sessions**; every participant who saw "N hidden to avoid overlap" said
  names were missing.
