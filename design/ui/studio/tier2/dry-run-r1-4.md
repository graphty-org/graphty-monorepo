# Tier 2 fourth dry run before round 1: what to fix and what to leave

Every tier 2 task was walked again, each prompt on both datasets, on the build frozen for round 1
(8c4eef4722ac, graphty@0.8.56, commit 8c4eef472), after the third dry run's fixes were built
(`dry-run-r1-3.md`). This page lists every problem the walk found, sorts it, and gives the reason.

The sessions are for learning what a returning user needs. A session spent on a tooltip over the
names being counted, an Escape that throws away a whole import, or an "Edit source" that doubles
every tie teaches nothing about that, so every such problem is fixed before round 1. A real design
question -- where something lives, what a reader expects, what a new concept is called -- is left
in, because answering it is what the sessions are for.

Classes:

- **(a) fix now.** An implementation or polish defect a participant could hit. Each names its fix
  group (below).
- **(b) leave for the sessions.** An open design question the study exists to answer.
- **(n) no change.** Expected behavior, a property of the drawing that is not a build defect, a
  tool limit, or a pilot note that is not a problem; the reason says which.

Screenshots are under `rounds/r1d3/pilot/<task>/` (for example `T4A/11.png`). The two halves of a
task are its two datasets: T4A people and messages, T4B players and passes; T17A, T18A, T19A, T21A,
T23A and T24A the friends network; T17B and T22B the novel's characters; T18B, T19B, T23B and T12RB
the Florentine families; T20A the bus stops, T20B the hiking trails; T21B a team list and its
second version; T22A the bus stops; T24B the city stops; T12RA the novel's characters.

Fixing the (a) list changes the build again, so round 1 runs on a new frozen build, with every task
re-piloted on it and every "known on this build" note in the answer key re-recorded.
