# Tier 2 first dry run before round 4

Every tier 2 task half (A and B of T4, T17 to T24 and T12R) was piloted from its start on build
38e180aac230 (`criteria.md`, first line). The walks are in `rounds/r4/pilot/<task><half>/`. The
pilots reported 71 items, one row each below, with the class and the reason. Each item was checked
against the answer key and the round 3 dry runs (`dry-run-r3-{1,2,3}.md`) first; the three that
were new, or newly caused by a round 3 change, were checked on their screenshot and in source.

The question is the same as in every dry run: would a participant spend session time on a flaw in
the build instead of on what the task asks? Yes, in two places. Both come from round 3 changes
that landed on this build:

- **Filter counts lost the people.** A filter step over an edge column now counts only edges on the
  header chip ("12 of 41 edges") and on its Filters row ("41 to 12 edges"). T17 asks how many
  people (B: characters) are still in the drawing, and the only node count left on screen is the
  Overview's "Nodes showing" in the right-hand panel (T17A/07, T17B/07). Before round 3 the chip
  and row counted only nodes, which made a step that hid 21 edges read "20 to 20 nodes". Each
  version counts half of what the step changed. A step over an edge column changes both, so both
  counts are shown.
- **The new Filters tool opens an editor with a grayed "Add step" and no reason** (T17B/16). The
  same editor opens from the Filters "+". Every other disabled command in the app says why (the
  "Filter to neighbors" tooltip since the round 3 dry runs), so this is the same pattern missing in
  one place.

One older polish item is fixed with them: a run's Values tab opens on a section also headed
"Values" (T21A/02, 07).

T12R B never reached the app: its start waited for a browser slot while four other pilots held
them, and its folder holds only an empty `session.log`. It is piloted in the next dry run, on the
build that carries these fixes. T12R A's folder is complete (7 screenshots, `work.json`). The T22
and T23 pilots reported nothing new.

Classes, as in the earlier dry runs:

- **fix: <unit>** -- an implementation or polish defect a participant could hit. Fixed before the
  first session in the package that owns it; the units are listed after the tables.
- **design** -- a question the sessions exist to answer (where something lives, what a returning
  user expects, what to call it). Left alone.
- **no change** -- the behavior is right or deliberate, or the claim did not hold.
- **owner** -- decided by the owner, or waiting on the owner, in `../owner-decisions.md`.
- **process** -- about how the pilot ran or what the answer key tells graders, not the app.

Reasons that recur are named in `dry-run-r3-1.md` and used here by the same names: **3D overlap**,
**names by default**, **focus on a heading**, **covers until closed**, **All N rows**, **hover
under the pointer**, **tooltip on entry**.

## Units

- **Filter counts** (graphty app, `graphty/src/workspace/data-place/filterWords.ts`,
  `graphty/src/workspace/data-place/Filters.tsx`): a step over an edge column names both what it
  leaves of the nodes and of the edges, on its Filters row ("20 to 19 nodes, 41 to 12 edges") and
  on the header chip ("19 of 20 nodes, 12 of 41 edges"); a step over nodes keeps its node count.
  Both counts come from the element (`plan.effect.steps`, `visibility.summary`). The whole row text
  is visible on Les Miserables, the longest case, without an ellipsis (5, 9).
- **Step editor reason** (graphty app, `graphty/src/workspace/data-place/Filters.tsx`, with its
  words in `filterWords.ts`): while "Add step" (or the save button of an open step) cannot be
  pressed, it is drawn disabled with a tooltip that says what is missing (no attribute chosen, no
  value typed, nothing changed), the pattern "Filter to neighbors" uses (`aria-disabled`,
  `data-disabled`, a `Tooltip`), and a press or Enter still does nothing (10).
- **Run values heading** (graphty app, `graphty/src/workspace/inspector/RunValues.tsx`): the run's
  first section is headed "Distribution", not "Values" (51).
