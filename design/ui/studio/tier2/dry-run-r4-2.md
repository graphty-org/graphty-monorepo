# Tier 2 second dry run before round 4

Every tier 2 task half (A and B of T4, T12R and T17 to T24) was piloted from its start on build
5277e2cbe (`criteria.md`, first line), the build that carries the first dry run's three fixes. The
walks are in `rounds/r4d1/pilot/<task><half>/`; all twenty halves reached the app and finished,
T12R B included, which the first dry run never reached. The pilots reported 91 items, one row each
below, with the class and the reason. Each item was checked against the answer key and the earlier
triages (`dry-run-r3-{1,2,3}.md`, `dry-run-r4-1.md`) first; the four that read as new were checked
on their screenshot, in source, or on the build.

The question is the same as in every dry run: would a participant spend session time on a flaw in
the build instead of on what the task asks? No. Not one of the 91 items is a new implementation
defect, and none needs a fix before the sessions. The four that looked new did not hold:

- **"Add step" grayed with no hint** (T17A/13, T17B/15). The pilots photographed the button without
  pointing at it. On the build, pointing at the grayed "Add step" in the editor the Filters tool
  opens shows the tooltip "Choose an attribute first", as "Filter to neighbors" does. That is the
  first dry run's fix, present and working.
- **The neighborhood's group named by its whole content** (T12RA). The group is named by its
  heading, "Javert and 17 connections" (compact-mantine's `ControlSection`: `role="group"` with
  `aria-labelledby` on the heading; the app's story test finds it by that name). The run-together
  text is how the study tool describes a match it cannot tell apart (`aria-label`, else the
  element's whole text), not what a screen reader reads.
- **The tie inspector's gray From and To values** (T12RB/11). Measured at 7.3:1 (#b6bdb6 on
  #2c2c2c), above the 4.5:1 text needs. They are list-row values, drawn like the node's Degree row
  (`dry-run-r3-1.md`, row 100). The gray swatch is the edge's own color; the blue double line on
  the drawing is the selection mark over it.
- **The focus ring left on the note card after Escape Escape** (T19A/09). Classed in the first round
  3 dry run (row 40): focus is on the note where saving put it, and clearing the selection rightly
  does not move it.

The first dry run's three fixes hold on their screenshots: the filter row and chip name both
counts ("20 to 19 nodes, 41 to 12 edges", "19 of 20 nodes, 12 of 41 edges", T17A/07); a run's
first section is headed "Distribution" (T21A/07); the step editor's grayed button says what is
missing (walked above). The T21A, T22 and T23 pilots reported nothing new and no script, console or
request errors.

So the build stays frozen: 5277e2cbe is the round 4 build.

Classes, as in the earlier dry runs:

- **fix** -- an implementation or polish defect a participant could hit. None this time.
- **design** -- a question the sessions exist to answer (where something lives, what a returning
  user expects, what to call it). Left alone.
- **no change** -- the behavior is right or deliberate, or the claim did not hold.
- **owner** -- decided by the owner, or waiting on the owner, in `../owner-decisions.md`.
- **process** -- about how the pilot ran or what the answer key tells graders, not the app.

Reasons that recur are named in `dry-run-r3-1.md` and used here by the same names: **3D overlap**,
**names by default**, **focus on a heading**, **covers until closed**, **All N rows**, **hover
under the pointer**, **tooltip on entry**. One more, from `dry-run-r4-1.md`: **chosen face** -- the
owner chose Figma's face for a segmented control's chosen side on 2026-10-10 (a darker face with a
1px edge and a bolder label on the lighter track), so the chosen side does not stand out by a fill.
