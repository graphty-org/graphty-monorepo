# Tier 2 participants: returning users and their earlier sessions

Simulated participants: each session is a fresh agent playing one persona, with no memory of any
other session. What makes a persona "returning" is the history below, which the participant is
given word for word with its persona file (`tasks.md`, "Rules"). No real person is portrayed; the
persona files are composites built from public sources.

Paths: `S/` = `design/ui/studio/personas/` (this worktree); `P/` =
`/home/apowers/Projects/graphty-monorepo/.worktrees/ux-storyboards-mocks-and-study/design/ui/prototype/study/personas/`;
project records (designloom): `/home/apowers/Projects/graphty-monorepo/design/designloom/personas/`.
Every session record gives the persona file's full path.

## Who returns, and why these nine

- **Six who did tier 1 as first-time users** (Grace, Ruth, Dev, Elena, Nadia, Tom). The owner's
  priority is the average user, and tier 1's newcomers are exactly who tier 2 should serve next.
  Grace, Ruth and Dev have returning files (`S/returning-*.md`); for Elena, Nadia and Tom the
  history below carries what changed.
- **Three regular analysts** (Alex, Jordan, Dana), the designloom records whose stated rhythm is
  repeat work: Alex is weekly, "reproducible analyses", "use specific workflows repeatedly"
  (`analyst-alex.yaml`); Jordan analyzes ego networks and tracks change over time
  (`marketing-analyst.yaml`); Dana re-runs supplier risk as data changes
  (`supply-chain-analyst.yaml`).
- **Not in tier 2:** Morgan (screen reader) and Sam (keyboard only), because the owner ruled out a
  keyboard-only study and a touch profile for this work (2026-10-07); Mara (an expert Gephi user
  weighing a switch), whose questions are expert workflows, not common repeat work.

## How the histories are written

- Each history says when the earlier sessions were, what the person did and saw, what they
  remember in their own words, what annoyed them, and what they have never used.
- **A history names only places the persona's tier 1 sessions reached** (the start screen and its
  samples, "Open project or file...", the left rail's Graph and Data, the find box, the toolbar's
  analysis button and its list, a run's Values, the Style tab of Everything, the Export dialog,
  Save as and Recent projects, a person's list of connections). **It never names a tier 2
  control**: no filter, notes, path, hops, follow, replace, "Higher means", endpoints, or a rule
  typed in the find box. The preflight's history check enforces this (`criteria.md`, item 7).
- Every history ends with the same sentence: "Anything you have not done before is as new to you
  as it was the first day." A returning user knows the shell, not the features added since.
