## What holds

- **Equal path for picks.** Path From and To are typed comboboxes ("From node", "To node") beside
  the canvas pick buttons; Enter moves From to To to Find path.
- **Focus after most commits:** Find path and Rerun land on the run's heading; saving a note
  lands on its card; Select endpoints on "Selection actions"; Filter to neighbors keeps focus on
  its toggle (`pressed`); the step checkbox keeps focus; Escape from the Path popover returns
  to where focus was; both Loads land on the Graph rail button.
- **Announcements present:** filter on/off with counts, path found ("Shortest path added: Chloe
  to Milo, 4 hops"), note added with its target, selection counts, hops changes ("Ava's 14
  connections within 2 hops"), replacement loaded.
- **States in names, not color only:** "PageRank, out of date" in the tree, "Filter: 19 of 20
  nodes" on the header chip, the step row's word "off", `invalid` on a refused rule.
- **Contrast of text:** every small or dimmed string measured passes 4.5:1 (lowest: 4.73:1, the
  import page's "whole number" caption; the inspector's gray captions about 5.3:1). Focus rings
  3.32:1 (on #383838) to 3.95:1 (on #2c2c2c).
- The left-out row is fully readable as text in its inspector.

  read does not list controls inside a tree row; the rail's current place and the step checkbox
  were checked in source and by Tab instead.
