## What holds

- **Round 3's focus fixes, verified by keys on this build:**
  - Two Escapes keep focus in the find box (`replace-sr/95.png`-`97.png`).
  - Changing Follow by arrows keeps focus on Follow, and Hops stays at 1 (`path-sr/140.png`-`143.png`).
  - Tab out of the step editor commits the edit and says "Saved ...". Escape cancels it, says
    "Not saved: ... is as it was", and returns focus to the step row (`filter-sr/116.png`, `100.png`).
- **A repeated message is spoken again:** "Filter off" twice and "Note added about Shortest path"
  twice were each announced.
- **The runnable rule option** is the combobox's active option (`aria-activedescendant`), drawn
  highlighted, and Enter runs it. The refusal is polite red text at 7.20:1.
- **The repeated-ties line and "Replace friends.csv"** are reachable, named and in body text
  (13.97:1). Only the announcement is missing (finding 2).
- **Rule-named layers** carry the whole rule in their accessible name. A run row's count is read
  as the row's description in compact-mantine's Tree, so "minutes 14" is not missing from the
  row.
- **Other holds:**
  - Picking an edge in the find box now moves focus to its inspector heading, like a node.
  - The two-table Load says "people and messages: 12 nodes, 22 edges, 1 row left out".
  - Replace says "friends.csv replaced: 20 nodes, 41 edges. 1 run out of date".
  - Rerun returns focus to the heading.
  - The "Higher means" group is named.
- **Contrast:** every new or changed text measured passes 4.5:1. The lowest are the rule option's
  "Select its matches" on the highlighted row (4.91:1), the selected run row's "4 hops" and
  "minutes 14" (5.53 and 5.61:1), and the note chip (5.18:1). The state bar reads 9.29:1. A rule
  layer at the highlight look draws black edges on the light canvas, about 19:1.
