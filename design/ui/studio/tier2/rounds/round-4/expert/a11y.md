# Tier 2 round 4 -- accessibility walkthrough

Build 5277e2cbe1bd (graphty@0.8.64), frozen copy `.study-builds/tier2-r4d1-5277e2cbe/`, driven with
`tool/real.mjs` in screen-reader mode (`--sr`: keys only; the focused control, its description and
every live region printed after each step), 1440 x 900. Walked 2026-10-11 by the accessibility
specialist, before reading any session. Standard: WCAG 2.2 AA, plus the studio's rules (focus never
falls to the page, one utterance per change, no two reachable controls share a name, a disabled
control says why).

Counts: one severity 3, eight severity 2 and twenty-two severity 1, 31 in all. Findings 1 to 5 and
10 to 16 are new this round. The rest were open in round 3 and are still open. Round 3 had 25, so
bar 10's count rises. Six round 3 findings are closed (below), and twelve new ones were found, most
of them on routes this walk took for the first time (cancelling a note, an off step's editor, the
"or more" row, the toolbar's Filters button, picking on the canvas).

Finding 1 is not only a keyboard problem. A pointer user who clicks an off step to read its rule,
then clicks Graph, gets the step back on with no notice (`stepon-pointer/10.png`). It is on the
route of any task that turns a step off to compare.

## What holds

- **Round 3's agreed fixes, verified by keys on this build:**
  - Enter on Shortest path from the Analyze list (reached by "shortest path" and "fewest") puts
    focus in From, and the From list stays closed. Escape from the form goes back to the list.
    The next Escape clears the filter text, and the one after that closes the popover and returns
    focus to Analyze (`path-sr/04.png`, `82.png`-`84.png`).
  - Load on the Add page over a newer copy is described by "41 edges in this file are already in
    friends. Load adds them a second time." The line wears the warning glyph (6.92:1)
    (`replace-sr/13.png`, `16.png`).
  - Color on a selection's new layer puts focus on "Color swatch" (`path-sr/53.png`).
  - The disabled "Filter to neighbors" is described as "The drawing already shows only this
    neighborhood. Its step in Filters turns it off" (`path-sr/74.png`).
- **Closed from round 3's list** (old numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, 12 and 14):
  - Filters "+" puts focus in the new editor's Keep field (`filter-sr/07.png`).
  - The disabled "Add step" is in the Tab order and described as "Choose an attribute first"
    (`filter-sr/10.png`, `12.png`).
- **The changes new in this build:**
  - The Analyze list puts Shortest path first, with the only "Start here" badge
    (`path-sr/03.png`).
  - The "or more" row's visible text ("weight is 4 or more (12)") is inside its accessible name.
    Arrow keys scroll the active option into view (`path-sr/45.png`).
  - A filter step over an edge column counts edges on its row and on the chip
    (`filter-sr/22.png`).
  - The toolbar's Filters button opens the editor with focus in Keep. Its focus ring measures
    3.95:1 on the toolbar (`filter-sr/64.png`, `65.png`).
- **Other holds:**
  - Escape from the step editor says "Not saved: ... is as it was." and returns focus to the row
    (`filter-sr/35.png`).
  - Notes save by Tab to "Save note" or by Control+Enter, focus lands on the new note, and
    "Note added about Shortest path" is spoken each time (`path-sr/34.png`, `37.png`).
  - Select endpoints moves focus to "Selection actions" and says "2 nodes selected".
  - Follow and Hops keep focus on the control used.
  - Two Escapes keep focus in the find box (`replace-sr/33.png`, `34.png`).
  - Rerun returns focus to the heading.
  - The two-table Load says "people and messages: 12 nodes, 22 edges, 1 row left out".
  - "Higher means" is a named radio group.
- **Contrast:** every new or changed text measured passes 4.5:1. The lowest are "Select its
  matches" on the highlighted rule option (4.91:1), the step row's "off" on a selected row
  (5.46:1) and the run's time link (6.94:1). The "or more" row reads 9.79:1 highlighted, the
  refusal 7.20:1, the state bar 9.29:1. The selection's blue edges measure 4.42:1 on the light
  canvas. The canvas focus mark is a 1 px near-black line with a white line inside it: visible,
  but thin.
