# Tier 3, iteration 2: accessibility walkthrough of the style panel -- Accessibility Specialist

Build under test: build stamp `2faecd941bc0 graphty@0.8.68`, the one served at
https://dev.ato.ms:9373 and frozen as `.study-builds/tier3-i2-2faecd941b/`, opened at `/?next`.

How it was checked (WCAG 2.2 AA):

- **Keyboard and screen reader, by keys only.** One screen-reader-mode session of the studio's
  `real.mjs` (Playwright, its own browser, through the browser gate) on that build, which prints the
  focused control's role, name, value and states after every key, every live-region change, and
  what browse mode reads. Les Miserables open with Degree ("Connections") and Louvain
  ("Communities") run. Walked: the tree and its row actions, every row kind (Hovered, Selected,
  Everything, a run's layer, a layer made from a category, a layer made from the draft, a folder),
  the Style and Values tabs, Which elements on a run's layer and on the draft, the vary picker
  (by its button and by "=" in a number field), both binding popovers, a category row's menu, Text
  and Label settings, the row menu and Move to folder, Alt+arrow moves, Lock, Hide, Graph settings,
  Undo. Session folder: `tier3/build/a11y-i2/sr1/` (about 660 screenshots).
- **axe-core** (tags wcag2a, wcag2aa, wcag21aa, wcag22aa) in eleven panel states, light and dark
  theme, in a Playwright context of its own, with the measurements axe does not make: focus after
  each popover closes, after Detach and after the draft's Escape; option descriptions and groups in
  the vary picker; tree levels; field text cut off by its box; the kind group's name; the Selected
  ring around a selected node. `a11y-i2/axe-walk.mjs`, results `axe/axe.json`, screenshots
  `axe/<theme>-<state>.png`.
- **A layer's kind changed in place**, four cases by pointer (a run's layer and a reader's layer,
  to Edges and to Both): `kind.mjs`, `kind-*.png`.
- **Computed contrast** of every text in the tree, the inspector and open popovers, every
  placeholder, and the not-set look of each field kind, both themes: `contrast.mjs`.
- **200% zoom** (720 x 450 CSS pixels): `zoom.mjs`, `zoom-*.png`.

Every screenshot cited was looked at. `build/` is not committed; the paths below are under
`design/ui/studio/tier3/build/a11y-i2/`. "Owner" is the package where the fix belongs.

Severity: 4 blocks a keyboard or screen-reader user from a task; 3 loses their place or hides
information a task needs on a common route; 2 slows or misleads; 1 polish; 0 not a defect.

## What the iteration fixed

Each was checked by keys on this build. Of the previous walk's twenty-five findings, seventeen are
fixed, five in part (findings 3, 12, 17 and 18 below carry the rest), two remain (findings 9 and 20) and one, the shape list, was not walked again.

- **A layer goes into a folder by keyboard.** The row menu (Shift+F10) has "Move to folder" with a
  submenu of folders; Alt+ArrowUp moves a row into the folder above and Alt+ArrowLeft moves it out.
  Each move is announced: "Nodes where name contains Val moved into People, 1 of 1", "... moved to
  the top level, 4 of 8" (`sr1/377.png`).
- **Pop-outs fit the window and scroll the focused control into view** (Label settings,
  `sr1/208.png`, `sr1/238.png`), also at 200% zoom (`zoom-3-ramp.png`).
- **Closing a popover gives focus back** to the button that opened it: the vary picker by Escape,
  from its button or from "=" in the number field; Color from data by Escape; Label settings. Both
  themes (`axe/axe.json`: `focusAfterPickerEscape`, `focusAfterRampEscape`).
- **"New layer for Group 1"** focuses the new layer's color field and says "Layer Louvain Group 1
  added above" (`sr1/194.png`). Escape on a draft returns focus to "Add a layer or a folder".
- **Category names are drawn beside their colors** and each color says it is not set: "Louvain
  Group 1 color, not set, #E69F00"; rows are "Hide Louvain Group 1", "Louvain Group 1 actions"
  (`sr1/182.png`).
- **Space on a tree row selects it**; hiding is on the eye button, a toggle that says "pressed".
- **Color fields say "not set" and "Mixed"** on the Style tab ("Node color, not set, Mixed").
- **One Tab stop per list, and no one-item menu**: Text's "+" offers Label and Tooltip; once a
  label exists it becomes "Add tooltip"; Effects' last choice becomes "Add outline".
- **The color ramp's fields are labeled**: "Values from", "Values to" (`sr1/128.png`).
- **A bound value names its property**: "Node color: Degree", "Detach node color".
- **A locked layer's controls stay focusable**, read as unavailable, each described "This layer is
  locked", which is also drawn at the top of the panel (`sr1/416.png`).
- **A number field refuses letters**; ArrowDown steps from the current value.
- **Which elements**: Edges and Both are offered on every layer; Find names each node ("This node:
  Valjean, name contains Val") and says "No element matches Valzzz"; the kind group is a radio
  group named "Paints"; every layer, a run's included, shows its rule.
- **Graph settings** opens the Style tab, moves focus to its first section and says "Graph
  settings, Style" (`sr1/567.png`).
- **The ramp's sliders step by whole data values**, and Page Up and Page Down move by 3.
- **The vary picker** is titled and named alike ("Size by attribute"); its groups are named ("In
  use", "Degree", "Louvain", "Does not fit") and each option's range is its description ("Degree",
  described "1 to 36").
- **Tree row targets** pass 2.5.8 (axe finds nothing there now).
- **At 200% zoom every toolbar button is on screen**; the toolbar wraps (`zoom-2-layer.png`).
- **The Selected starting row draws a visible ring**: a near-black halo (`#111111`, measured
  17.3:1 against the canvas `#F5F5F5`, 8.8:1 against an orange node) around a selected node
  (`axe/ring-crop.png`). It was 1.13:1 before.

axe: no hit in the tree, the Style tab, the binding popovers or Graph settings in either theme; the
two rules it does hit are findings 5 and 6. Text contrast: every text in the tree, the inspector
and the popovers measures 4.5:1 or more in both themes; placeholders 4.66:1 (light) and 6.65:1
(dark). No step raised a script error, a `console.error` or a failed request.

## Severity 4

1. **Changing a layer's kind from Nodes to Edges destroys the layer, and a keyboard user meets it
   on the way to Both.** In Which elements the kinds are a radio group, and arrow keys select as
   they move, as radio groups do. From Nodes, ArrowRight selects Edges: the layer is renamed to an
   internal id ("connections_1", "nodes-where-name-contains-val_1"), its row reads "paints
   nothing", the Style tab empties (no rule, no sections), what it painted is gone (Valjean's size
   3 in the second case) and focus falls to the page, so the second ArrowRight toward Both does
   nothing (`sr1/96.png`, read at `sr1/98.png`). The pointer does the same: a click on Edges breaks
   a run's layer and a reader's layer alike, both themes (`kind-Connections-Edges.png`,
   `kind-Nodeswherename-Edges.png`, `axe/light-11-kind-to-edges.png`). Nodes to Both works
   (`kind-Connections-Both.png`). Undo (Control+Z) restores the layer in one step and says "Undid
   changing Degree.", which a reader would not connect to what just happened. Changing a layer's
   kind in place was this iteration's answer to question 8, and the natural keyboard route through
   it is blocked (2.1.1, 3.2.2). Mechanism not traced: the new id's shape suggests the row is
   re-created rather than its target changed. Owner: graphty-element (the kind change in place,
   `RowWrite.target`), with the graphty app's Which elements; the Design Engineer should trace it.
   Whatever the fix, a kind change that drops a part of the layer should keep focus on the radio
   and say what changed ("Connections now paints edges; node settings removed").

## Severity 3

2. **Detach drops focus to the page, and says nothing.** Enter on "Detach node color" replaces the
   binding with a fixed color, removes the Detach button and leaves focus on nothing; the next Tab
   starts again near the field (`sr1/84.png`; both themes, `axe/axe.json`: `focusAfterDetach` is
   "page"). The fixed color it chose (`#6366F1`, the color beneath) is not announced. Detach is
   on every bound value, the route of questions 5, 6 and 12 (2.4.3, 4.1.3). Fix: focus the color
   field that replaced the pill, and one polite line "Node color fixed at #6366F1". Owner: graphty
   app.

3. **Making a layer from the draft still drops focus and says nothing.** Typing the draft's first
   value (Node size 3, Enter) makes the layer "Nodes where name contains Val", redraws the panel
   and leaves focus on the page (`sr1/302.png`). The only live text is the count "1 of 77 nodes",
   which arrives already in its region. "New layer for" does this right now (focus to the new
   layer's first field, "Layer ... added above"), so the draft is the one route left. Fix: keep
   focus on the field just set and say "Layer Nodes where name contains Val added". Owner: graphty
   app (the same announcer "New layer for" uses).

## Severity 2

4. **Two Tab stops per row between the tree and the inspector, and growing.** Every row's Lock
   and Hide are in the Tab order, before and after the tree: with eight rows, 16 Tab stops lie
   between "Add a layer or a folder" and "Graph settings", plus four more (Graph settings, Resize,
   Canvas, Analyze) before the inspector's first control (the Tab walk in `sr1/`, after the folder
   was made). The tree itself is one Tab stop with arrow keys, as it should be. In a treegrid or
   tree with row actions, the actions belong inside the row's arrow navigation (ArrowRight into
   the row's buttons) or behind the row menu, not in the page's Tab sequence. Owner:
   compact-mantine `Tree` row actions.

5. **Effect rows cut their color to two characters, and their targets are too small.** On the
   Selected row, the Glow, Halo and Band colors show "FF" and "11": each hex box is 11 px wide for
   30-36 px of text, and the swatch button is 14 x 14 px (`sr1/448.png`,
   `axe/light-6-selected-row.png`). axe fails target-size on all six, both themes (2.5.8); the cut
   value fails 1.4.4 in spirit, since the value cannot be read at any size. Owner: graphty app (the
   one-line effect row gives the color no width), or compact-mantine `CompactColorInput` if it
   should refuse to render below a minimum width.

6. **The key scrolls but cannot be reached by keyboard.** With the Selected starting row, the key
   holds Glow, Halo, Edge color and six groups, more than its capped height; it scrolls, but
   nothing in it takes focus, so a sighted keyboard user cannot see the groups below the fold
   (`axe/light-7-label-entry.png`, axe `scrollable-region-focusable`, four states, both themes)
   (2.1.1). A screen reader reads it in browse mode. Fix: make the key card a named, focusable
   region (`tabindex="0"`, "Key"), or let it grow when focused. Owner: graphty app (the key card).

7. **Not set looks disabled for numbers, and "Mixed" shows one color.** A not-set number (Node
   size "1", Edge width "8") is upright on a gray fill, the look of a disabled field, while a
   not-set select or color is italic on the field's normal background (`axe/notset-crop.png`;
   measured on the Everything row: shape, colors, head and tail italic, size and width normal).
   And a not-set color reads "Mixed" beside a swatch of one specific color: on the layer for
   Valjean, "Mixed" sits beside an indigo swatch while Valjean is drawn orange (`sr1/302.png`); one
   node cannot be mixed. Sighted users are told something the drawing contradicts (1.3.1). Owner:
   compact-mantine (one not-set look for every field kind; a mixed swatch, not a color), and the
   graphty app or graphty-element for what "beneath" resolves to on a one-node layer.

8. **"Node defaults" is named everywhere and shown nowhere.** Not-set fields and Clear say "Node
   defaults gives Icosphere", "Clear: back to #6366F1, from Node defaults", "Edge defaults gives 8"
   (`sr1/57.png`), but no row, section or setting in the app is called that: the bottom row is
   "Everything", which itself sets nothing (`sr1/592.png`), and Graph settings has no node
   defaults. A reader told where a value comes from cannot go there (3.2.4, 2.4.6). Fix: name the
   source the reader can open ("from graphty's defaults", or make Everything carry the element's
   defaults and say "from Everything"). Owner: graphty app (words), graphty-element if the
   defaults should be a readable row.

9. **One value, four names.** Louvain's values are "Group 1" in the key and the category rows,
   "Louvain" on the pill and in the key's title, "Community is one of 1" in the rule of the layer
   made from it, and that layer is "Louvain Group 1" (`sr1/182.png`, `sr1/194.png`). The run's
   layer is "Connections" in the tree but "Degree" in Undo: "Undid changing Color on Degree."
   (`axe/light-5-edges-layer.png`, its toast). (3.2.4.) Fix: one display name per value and per
   layer, from the readable-values list, used by the rule, the key, Undo and the rows. Owner:
   graphty app (Undo's words, the rule's display); graphty-element if the rule cannot carry a
   value's display name.

10. **Label settings shows a full gradient editor for an unset gradient, and its Escape loses
    focus.** With Gradient "Default", the pop-out still shows the stops, a white-to-black bar, a
    color area, hue, format and two stop rows: about fourteen controls, read with values ("Color
    value 000000", "Edit stop 1 color, pressed") that look set (`sr1/238.png`). Escape in the
    editor's hex field blurs it and leaves focus on the page with the pop-out open; a second
    Escape closes it (`sr1/239.png`) (2.4.3). Fix: show the gradient editor only when a gradient
    is set; Escape in a field inside a pop-out closes the pop-out. Owner: graphty app (when to show
    it), compact-mantine (the gradient editor's Escape).

11. **At 200% zoom the key runs under the inspector.** The Degree ramp's maximum and the key's
    right half are hidden beneath the inspector, and the wrapped toolbar covers the lower canvas
    (`zoom-2-layer.png`) (1.4.10). Owner: graphty app (the key card's width against the canvas).

12. **Repeated names remain in label rows.** A label row's controls are "Look", "Size",
    "Position", "Label settings", "Hide label", "Remove label" (`sr1/206.png`): read after "Node
    size", "Size, not set, 48" is ambiguous, and a layer with node and edge labels has two of each.
    The add buttons already carry the kind ("Add to node text", "Add to edge text"). Fix: "Node
    label size", "Node label settings". Owner: graphty app.

## Severity 1

13. **Ramp sliders ignore Home and End** (`sr1/`, the slider walk); APG sliders go to the minimum
    and maximum. Owner: compact-mantine `RampEditor`.
14. **Menu items put the shortcut in the name**: "Rename F2", "Duplicate Ctrl+D", "Delete Delete",
    "Unpin Not pinned". Use `aria-keyshortcuts` and a description for the disabled reason. Owner:
    compact-mantine (the menu item), the app for the words.
15. **A set color does not say "set here"**: "Node color" value "E69F00", "Halo color" value
    "111111", where numbers and selects say "Node size, set here, 3". Owner: compact-mantine
    `CompactColorInput`.
16. **Clear has two glyphs**: a minus on the Style tab, an "x" in the binding popovers ("Reset
    Scale to default") and in Graph settings (`sr1/128.png`, `sr1/567.png`). Owner: graphty app.
17. **Label settings numbers** are named "Line height, not set, Default" but report a value of 0,
    so a screen reader says both. Owner: compact-mantine (no value when not set).
18. **Category counts are bare numbers**: "Group 1 20". Fix: "20 nodes". Owner: graphty app.
19. **Headings**: Label settings' groups (Type, Box, Letters, Placement) are level 4 under the
    dialog's level 2; the Values tab's Summary, Members and Painted are buttons, not headings, so
    they cannot be reached by heading. Owner: graphty app.
20. **Colors are spoken as hex**: "Fix at #B84203", "Clear: back to #6366F1". Owner: graphty app.
21. **In the key, the white Glow swatch looks like an empty checkbox** beside "Selected"
    (`axe/light-7-label-entry.png`). Owner: graphty app (the key's swatch for a white effect).
