# Tier 3, iteration 3: accessibility walkthrough of the style panel -- Accessibility Specialist

Build under test: build stamp `617b43b270eb graphty@0.8.68`, the one served at
https://dev.ato.ms:9373 and frozen as `.study-builds/tier3-i3-617b43b270/`, opened at `/?next`.

How it was checked (WCAG 2.2 AA):

- **Keyboard and screen reader, by keys only.** One screen-reader-mode session of the studio's
  `real.mjs` (Playwright, its own browser, through the browser gate) on that build. It prints the
  focused control's role, name, value and states after every key, every live-region change, and
  what browse mode reads. Les Miserables open with Degree ("Connections") and Louvain
  ("Communities") run. Walked: the tree and its row menu, Connections' Style tab (fields, Clear,
  Detach, Undo), the color ramp popover and its sliders, the vary picker (button and "="), a label
  and Label settings, Shape settings, an effect (add, Escape, set, hide, show, remove, Undo),
  Which elements and the kind radios (Nodes, Edges, Both, Undo), the category popover and "New layer
  for Group 3", a new layer from the draft, the Selected starting row, the Values tab, Graph
  settings. Session folder: `tier3/build/a11y-i3/sr1/` (441 screenshots).
- **axe-core** (tags wcag2a, wcag2aa, wcag21aa, wcag22aa) in ten panel states, light and dark
  theme, in a Playwright context of its own, plus what axe does not measure: focus after Tab leaves
  a popover, after Clear, Remove and Undo; the ramp slider's range after one step; an effect's color
  after hide and show; node settings after a kind change by pointer; text cut by its box; text
  contrast in the tree, inspector, popovers and key. `a11y-i3/walk.mjs`, results
  `axe/report.json`, screenshots `axe/<theme>-<state>.png`.
- **Row labels and effect chips**, both themes: `probe2.mjs`. **200% zoom** (720 x 450 CSS pixels):
  in `walk.mjs`, `zoom-*.png`.

Every screenshot cited was looked at. `build/` is not committed; paths below are under
`design/ui/studio/tier3/build/a11y-i3/`. "Owner" is the package where the fix belongs.

Severity: 4 blocks a keyboard or screen-reader user from a task; 3 loses their place, loses their
work or hides information a task needs on a common route; 2 slows or misleads; 1 polish; 0 not a
defect.

## What the iteration fixed

Each was checked by keys on this build. Of the previous walk's twenty-one findings, twelve are
fixed, seven in part (findings 2, 3, 13, 15 and 18 below carry the rest), one remains (colors
spoken as hex, finding 14), and one (the key's white Glow swatch) was not seen because nothing was
selected.

- **A kind change keeps the layer.** Nodes to Edges no longer renames the layer to an internal
  id or empties it. Focus stays on the radio and the change is said: "Connections now paints
  edges", then "Connections now paints nodes and edges" (`sr1/266.png`, `sr1/267.png`). What is
  still lost is finding 2.
- **Detach keeps focus and says what it did**: focus goes to the color button that replaced the
  pill, and "Node color detached, now blue" is spoken (`sr1/77.png`).
- **The draft's first value keeps focus**: typing 4 in Node size makes the layer, focus stays in
  the field ("Node size, set here, 4"), and "Layer Name is Valjean added" is spoken (`sr1/346.png`).
- **The tree is one Tab stop.** Lock and Hide left the Tab order; they are in the row menu
  (Shift+F10: Rename, Duplicate, Move to folder, Pin, Select what it paints, Hide, Lock, Delete).
  From "Add a layer or a folder" one Tab reaches the tree and the next reaches Graph settings
  (`sr1/24.png`). Rows say their kind and count ("Connections, layer, 77 nodes") and pinned rows
  are described "pinned to the top" or "pinned to the bottom".
- **Effect rows use a 24 x 24 px chip** with a name that says its state: "Glow color, set here,
  #111111" (`sr1/392.png`). axe finds no target-size failure anywhere in either theme.
- **"Node defaults" is gone.** Not-set fields say where their value comes from in words the reader
  can find: "Node size, not set, 1 by default", "Node size, not set, Connections gives 3", and Clear
  is described "Clear: back to 1, the default".
- **Undo right after Clear works** and names the row as the tree does: "Undid clearing node size
  on Connections." (`sr1/148.png`).
- **One name per value**, mostly: "Community Group 3" is the layer, its rule "Community is one of
  Group 3", its announcement "Layer Community Group 3 added above" (`sr1/314.png`); category counts
  say "20 nodes".
- **Label settings**: the gradient editor shows only when a gradient is set; Escape in any field
  closes the pop-out and gives focus to "Node label settings" (`sr1/188.png`); its groups are level 3
  headings under the level 2 title.
- **The Values tab** has real headings: Summary, Values read, Now, Painted (`sr1/417.png`).
- **Menu items** no longer carry their shortcut in the name ("Rename", "Duplicate").
- **A set color says "set here"** ("Node glow color, set here").
- **Clear is one minus** in the ramp's Scale row too.
- **At 200% zoom the key fits** beside the inspector (`zoom-1-layer.png`); the page never scrolls
  sideways (720 of 720).

axe: no violation in any of the ten states in either theme. Text contrast: every text measured in
the tree, inspector, popovers and key is 4.5:1 or more in both themes. No step raised a script
error, a `console.error` or a failed request.

## Severity 3

1. **Removing a value's last entry, or Clear on a bound value, loses focus.** Three routes, all by
   Enter on the focused button:
    - Clear on a bound color ("Clear Node color" while the color varies by Connections) removes
      the binding and leaves focus on the page; nothing is spoken; the next Tab lands on "Pick
      Node color" (`sr1/150.png`, `sr1/152.png`).
    - "Remove glow" leaves focus on the page, with no word (`sr1/222.png`; both themes,
      `axe/report.json` `focusAfterRemoveEffect: "page"`). Undo then restores the glow and also
      leaves focus on the page (`sr1/223.png`).
    - "Remove label" sends focus to the Text title, an `aria-hidden` button with `tabIndex=-1`, so
      a screen reader says nothing (both themes, `focusAfterRemoveLabel: button "Text"`). Traced:
      `focusSectionNext` asks for the first control in the section, and in an empty list that is
      the pointer-only title button (`graphty/src/workspace/style/useFocusLine.ts:63-65`,
      `Effects.tsx:169`), while the comment beside the call says focus goes to the list's "+".

    Clear is the owner's first question and Remove is on every text and effect entry (2.4.3, 4.1.3).
    Fix: Clear on a bound value focuses the field that replaces the pill (as Detach now does) and
    says "Node color cleared, back to blue"; Remove focuses the next entry, else the list's "+", and
    says "Glow removed"; Undo puts focus back on the control it restored. Owner: graphty app.

2. **Arrowing from Nodes to Both throws away the layer's node settings.** The kinds are a radio
   group, so ArrowRight from Nodes selects Edges on the way to Both. On Connections that silently
   removed the node size (3), the color ramp bound to Connections, the name label and the glow;
   Both then shows every node field unset (`sr1/266.png`, `sr1/267.png`). The pointer does the
   same: Edges then Both leaves "Node color, not set, blue by default" where "Node color:
   Connections" was (both themes, `kindBefore` / `kindAfterEdgesBoth` in `axe/report.json`,
   `axe/light-6-kind-both.png`). The announcement says only "Connections now paints edges".
   Two Undos restore everything, but only the first says so ("Undid changing Connections."); the
   second is silent (`sr1/268.png` to `sr1/271.png`). A reader who wants Both loses a run's whole
   look on the keyboard's natural path (3.2.2, 4.1.3). ArrowLeft from Nodes wraps to Both directly,
   which nobody would guess. Fix, either: the node part survives a pass through Edges (kept until
   the reader leaves the editor, or kept dormant on the row), or the kind control commits only on
   Space or Enter, as a toolbar of toggle buttons does; and an announcement that drops settings
   names them ("Connections now paints edges; its node size, color, label and glow are removed").
   Owner: graphty-element (what `styles.writeRow(row, { target })` keeps), graphty app (the control
   and the words).

3. **One step of the ramp's start handle cannot be stepped back.** In "Color from data", ArrowRight
   on "Ramp start" moves it from 1 to 2, and the histogram redraws over 2 to 36: the 27 nodes with
   one connection leave the picture and the handle's minimum becomes 2, so ArrowLeft, Home and
   PageDown do nothing (`sr1/119.png`, `sr1/120.png`, `sr1/126.png`; both themes,
   `rampStartAfterRightThenHome: "2"`). End on the start handle does the same at 35 (`sr1/116.png`).
   Control+Z inside the popover does nothing, on the slider and in "Values from"
   (`rampStartAfterUndoInPopover: "2"`, `sr1/121.png` to `sr1/123.png`); only Escape and then
   Control+Z, or the "Fit to the data" button that appears below, brings it back. A slider whose
   range follows its own value is a trap for keys and pointer alike (2.1.1 in spirit, 3.2.2).
   Traced: the handles' range is the histogram's bins (`compact-mantine/src/components/RampEditor.tsx:473-475`),
   and the app draws the bins graphty-element's preview returns over the binding's domain
   (`graphty/src/workspace/style/BindingPopover.tsx:431-440`). Fix: bins and handles span the
   data's full extent while the domain moves inside it, and Control+Z in a popover reaches the
   document when the field has no pending edit. Owner: graphty-element (preview bins over the
   data's extent, an additive option if the current shape must stay), compact-mantine (RampEditor),
   graphty app (Undo inside popovers).

## Severity 2

4. **Tab leaves an open popover for an unrelated place.** From the last control of "Color from
   data" ("Use one color: orange") or of "Glow settings", Tab goes to "Resize inspector", then the
   page, while the popover stays open over the canvas (both themes, `tabPastRampEnd: separator
"Resize inspector"; popover open: true`; `sr1/96.png` to `sr1/100.png`). Shift+Tab from the
   popover's first control skips back to the control before the opener. A keyboard user loses the
   popover and the row it belongs to (2.4.3). Fix: Tab past the end closes the popover and goes to
   the control after its opener (Figma's panels close on focus out), or the popover keeps focus in a
   loop. Owner: compact-mantine (Popout).

5. **Row labels and picker options are cut at the default width.** At the inspector's default
   (and minimum) 240 px, the property labels read "Sh..." for Shape (26 of 33 px), "C." or "Co..."
   for Color (26 of 29 px; the painted-over glyph takes the rest), "He..." for Head
   (`sr1/76.png`, `probe2.mjs` output, both themes). In the vary picker, "Rank of Connectio...",
   "Percentile of ..." and "2 that do not fit size" are cut (`sr1/136.png`, `cut` in
   `axe/report.json`). The accessible names are whole, so a screen reader is fine; a sighted
   keyboard user, a low-vision user and a voice user ("click Shape") are not (1.4.4, 2.5.3). Fix: a
   label column wide enough for the longest one-word label at 11 px, the painted-over mark outside
   it; picker options that wrap to two lines. Owner: graphty app (`style.css` label column, the
   picker's option layout).

6. **A black effect chip disappears in the dark theme.** The Selected row's Glow chip (#111111)
   measures 1.35:1 against the dark panel (#2C2C2C) and has no border; Halo and Band (#868686) are
   3.84:1 (`probe2-dark-selected.png`, `sr1/392.png`). In the light theme the Halo and Band chips
   are 3.64:1 and Glow 18.9:1. The chip is the value and the button, so it needs 3:1 to be seen
   (1.4.11). Fix: a hairline ring around every chip at 3:1 against the panel in both themes, as
   Figma draws its fill swatches. Owner: compact-mantine (`CompactColorInput` chip variant).

7. **Hiding an effect forgets its color.** A glow set to #FF0000, hidden and shown again with the
   eye, comes back as the default #00B4FF; the chip says "Glow color, set here, #00B4FF"
   (`sr1/230.png` to `sr1/233.png`; both themes, `glowAfterHideShow`). The code marks this as a
   known shortcut (`Effects.tsx`, "the eye forgets the color it turned off"). The reader loses a
   value without being told, and the eye means something else here than on a tree row, where
   showing again restores everything. Fix: hide keeps the value and only stops drawing it. Owner:
   graphty app; graphty-element if a channel cannot be switched off without losing its value.

8. **An effect added and then left with Escape vanishes silently.** Add to node effects > Glow
   opens Glow settings with focus in the color; Escape before typing removes the Glow entry and
   returns focus to "Add to node effects" with no word (`sr1/212.png`). A reader who opened the
   settings to look, not to type, loses the entry and does not know why. Fix: say "Glow not
   added", or keep the entry with its default value. Owner: graphty app.

9. **Undo after a Clear leaves focus on the page.** With the color cleared and focus on "Pick Node
   color", Control+Z restores the binding, removes that button, and focus falls to the page
   (`sr1/153.png`); the same after Undo of Remove glow (finding 1). Undo should put focus on the
   control it brought back. Owner: graphty app.

10. **Default node labels fall below 4.5:1 and cover each other.** A new name label draws black
    text with no background or outline over the nodes. On Louvain's blue (#0072B2) black text is
    4.0:1, and labels sit on top of neighboring nodes and of each other ("Blacheville Fameuil",
    "Valjean" under orange nodes) (`sr1/187.png`). Canvas text is the reader's data, and the text
    form is in the table, but the default look is what every reader meets first (1.4.3). Fix: the
    app adds a new label with a look that has a halo or a box (a choice, so graphty-element's
    default stays neutral), and "Move labels off nodes and edges" on by default for the app's new
    labels. Owner: graphty app.

## Severity 1

11. **The tree's Tab stop stays on the row last arrowed to, not the selected row.** After "New layer
    for Group 3" selected the new row, Tab into the tree landed on Communities (`sr1/329.png`).
    Owner: compact-mantine `Tree` (roving index follows selection).
12. **A row's description repeats itself**: Connections is described "Painted over by Communities
    Painted over by Communities" (`sr1/29.png`). Owner: graphty app (StyleTree).
13. **Not-set numbers in Label settings and Graph settings also report a value**: "Line height, not
    set, 1.2" with value "1.2", "Spring length, not set, 10" with value "10", so a screen reader says
    the number twice; the Style tab's not-set numbers report none. Owner: compact-mantine (the
    number field's not-set state).
14. **Colors are spoken as hex**: "Community Group 1 color, not set, #E69F00", swatch buttons
    "#6366F1", "Node color, not set, #6366F1" beside "Node color, not set, blue by default" for the
    same field (`sr1/235.png`). Owner: graphty app (color words), compact-mantine (swatch names).
15. **Names**: "Hide label", "Remove label", "Hide glow", "Remove glow" lack the kind word that
    their neighbors have ("Node label look"); the vary picker groups values under "Degree" and
    "Louvain" while the values are "Connections" and "Community"; the tree says "Communities", the
    pill "Community"; Graph settings and the ramp say "Reset Scale to default" where the Style tab
    says "Clear". Owner: graphty app.
16. **Two live regions with different politeness**: Undo speaks through an assertive alert ("Undid
    changing Color on Connections."), everything else through a polite status; the count line ("77
    of 77 nodes", "Nothing is added until a property is set") is often drawn already filled, which
    many screen readers do not read. Owner: graphty app (one announcer).
17. **Clear's description is also read as page text** in browse mode ("Clear: back to blue, the
    default" after the button), so browse mode says it twice (`sr1/76.png` read). Owner:
    compact-mantine (description text hidden from browse mode, kept as the description).
18. **"Varies" sits beside one orange swatch** in a new layer's not-set node color
    (`axe/light-9-draft-both.png`): the swatch shows a color nothing paints. Owner: compact-mantine.
19. **The rule editor's "Value" box opens its list when Tab reaches it**; Tab still passes on.
    Owner: graphty app (`openOnFocus` on that combobox).
20. **A disabled menu item puts its reason in the name**: "Pin to bottom Already pinned there".
    Use a description. Owner: compact-mantine (menu item).
21. **At 200% zoom the toolbar wraps to three rows** and covers half of the canvas that is left
    (`zoom-1-layer.png`). Owner: graphty app (toolbar at narrow widths).
22. **Escape after "=" in a number field returns focus to the vary button**, not to the field it
    was typed in (`sr1/143.png`). Owner: graphty app.

## Not checked, and why

- **What a real screen reader says.** The session read Chromium's accessibility tree; phrasing and
  interruption in NVDA or VoiceOver still need a person.
- **Folders, Move to folder and Alt+arrow moves.** Verified last iteration and not changed in the
  tree's keyboard code this iteration beyond the row menu; not walked again.
- **The edge selection band, badge presets, the key's white Glow swatch.** Nothing was selected and
  no badge added.
- **The shape list's names and the 320 px reflow.** Not walked.
- **Reduced motion** (label animation, edge Motion): graphty-element does not honor it yet; not
  drawn, not judged.
