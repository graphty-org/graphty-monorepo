# Tier 3 participants: returning users and their earlier sessions

Simulated participants: each session is a fresh agent playing one persona, with no memory of any
other session. What makes a persona "returning" is the history below, given word for word with its
persona file (`tasks.md`, "Rules"). No real person is portrayed; the persona files are composites
built from public sources.

Paths: `S/` = `design/ui/studio/personas/` (this worktree); `P/` =
`/home/apowers/Projects/graphty-monorepo/.worktrees/ux-storyboards-mocks-and-study/design/ui/prototype/study/personas/`;
project records (designloom): `design/designloom/personas/`.

## Who returns, and why these eleven

- **The nine tier 2 participants** (Grace, Ruth, Dev, Elena, Nadia, Tom; Alex, Jordan, Dana). Every
  one of them colored or sized dots in tier 1 or 2, so every one is a returning styler, and six of
  them came to the program as first-time users: the owner's priority is the average user.
- **Maren, the genomics Cytoscape user** (`P/genomics-cytoscape-user.md`, record
  `genomics-cytoscape-user.yaml`), brought in by the owner's brief: a researcher who has styled
  networks in Cytoscape for years and has used this program only a few times. Her expectations
  (a style that maps a column through a gradient, a "bypass" for one node) are exactly what unified
  style layers replace, so her first moves test whether the panel explains itself to a migrant.
- **Dr. Chen, the computational biologist** (`P/bioinformatics-researcher.md`, record
  `bioinformatics-researcher.yaml`), only for T36 and T29 B, so the genomics tasks have two
  personas.
- **Not in tier 3:** Morgan (screen reader) and Sam (keyboard only), as in tier 2 (owner,
  2026-10-07); the accessibility expert walks every screen instead.

## How the histories are written

- Each history says what the person did and saw, in their own words, and what they have never
  done. It names **what** they did, never **where**: tier 2's round 5 showed that a history naming
  the place a feature lives points at the route.
- A history names no control of the unified style panel: no layer, folder, Everything, Selected,
  Hovered, which elements, vary, rule, entry, Clear, Text or Effects list. The history check
  enforces this.
- Every history ends with: "Anything you have not done before is as new to you as it was the first
  day."
