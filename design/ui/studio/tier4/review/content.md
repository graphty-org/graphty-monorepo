## What the team should decide first

1. C1 is the only finding that spoils grading (T65, T66): a participant who picks "Hide" sees the
   opposite and may report "done". Fix before the dry run.
2. C2, C3 and C14 are one decision: the Data place uses the glossary's words (attribute, data
   source) and the Style panel's picker, so the filter and the style card are the same card in
   words as well as in code.
3. C9 is a blocked route for T60 when the participant is already inside a project.
