# Tier 4 review: interaction consistency (Interaction designer)

Build: e52d7c4f5748 (graphty@0.8.92, branch design/studio-tier4, which includes the flat filter
cards of PR #4454), walked with `tool/real.mjs` on the GPU from a frozen copy of `graphty/dist`.
Walk screenshots are under the main checkout's
`tmp/design-studio/tier4/review/interaction/` (w1: pantries + deliveries joined, w2: pantries then
a second file, w3: volunteers.graphml). They are not in git.

The question for this lens: does the same act behave the same way in Data, Style, Layout and the
toolbar? Acts checked: add (the "+" menus), remove (minus and x), Enter, Escape and Tab in new
entries and cards, undo, and the condition cards. Every finding cites `tier4/decided.md` or my
design criteria in `notes/interaction.md`. Severity: 4 blocks a task, 3 misleads or loses work or
breaks a decided rule, 2 is friction or inconsistency, 1 is cosmetic.

## What already holds (keep)

- A new Fields entry follows the Style and Location pattern: "+" picks the kind, the column list
  opens, Escape with no pick drops the entry and focus returns to "+" (w1 04-05).
- Undo is one labeled step per act across Data and Style: Ctrl+Z undid the layer condition, the
  layer, the filter card and the Time role in turn, each with its notice (w1 22-28).
- The toolbar Filters button opens the same Add filter menu as the Data place's Filters "+"
  (w1 36): one command, two doors.
- The table's Export... opens Export on Data; Escape on the join page cancels it and returns focus
  to Sources "+" (w2 07).
- A value card (role: Is any of) writes on each tick, with its count live (w3 04).
