# Tier 4 participants: returning users managing their data

Simulated participants: each session is a fresh agent playing one persona, with no memory of any
other session. What makes a persona "returning" is the history below, which the participant is
given word for word with its persona file. No real person is portrayed; the persona files are
composites built from public sources.

Paths: `S/` = `design/ui/studio/personas/` (this worktree); `P/` =
`/home/apowers/Projects/graphty-monorepo/.worktrees/design-studio-tier4/design/ui/studio/personas/`
(the four files tiers 2 and 3 read from the storyboards worktree were copied here on 2026-10-29,
so this tier does not depend on that worktree); project records (designloom):
`design/designloom/personas/`.

## Who takes part, and why these seven

- **Grace** (nonprofit operations) and **Dana** (supply-chain risk): people whose data arrives as
  two spreadsheets and has to be put together, cleaned and sent on every month
  (`supply-chain-analyst.yaml`: "exports often come as two sheets"). They take the food bank half.
- **Ruth** (reporter): her sources network, a colleague's online copy.
- **Tom** (lab manager who keeps the river project's map, `recipe-recipient.md`): reads every
  confirmation, changes nothing he does not understand; a careful newcomer to each data feature.
  He takes the river half.
- **Maren** (genomics researcher who knows Cytoscape, `genomics-cytoscape-user.yaml`): network files
  from public addresses and column kinds are daily work in Cytoscape; she shows where that
  expectation meets graphty. Her own yeast network, and a colleague's river project she helps
  with. Her findings are reported with who she is.
- **Alex** (operations analyst who knows Gephi and NetworkX, `analyst-alex.yaml`): the weekly
  routine for two clients, the food bank and the river project; expects a data laboratory as in
  Gephi.
- **Sam** (keyboard only, `keyboard-only-sam.md`): two tasks with every pointer step refused
  (`--keys`), for bar 5: putting two files together (the file chooser and the join screen) and the
  filter cards.

## How the histories are written

Each history names only places earlier tiers reached (the start screen, "Open a file", the left
rail's Graph and Data, the find box, the table from the toolbar, the Style tab, Analyze, Save and
Recent projects, the Export dialog for a picture). It never names a tier 4 control: no web address
box, second file, join, column kind, Fields, time or place, label or weight designation, filter
card, data issue or data export. Each history accounts for every start the persona is given: a list
opened on its own is one they opened before; a project with two lists already together, tidied or
narrowed was set up by a colleague or a friend, so the person has still never done it themselves.
Every history ends: "Anything you have not done before is as new to you as it was the first day."
