# Calm-first check on the fix-pass build (22a87a0679)

Build: `.study-builds/tier5-fixpass-22a87a0679` (graphty@0.8.92, commit 22a87a0679), the build
made after round 1's fixes. Date: 2026-11-03. Run by the owner-lens expert, before any round 2
session was read, by the rules of bar 9's calm-first check (`criteria.md`, "The calm-first check").

## Result

**The fix-pass build does not pass bar 9's calm-first limits: 1 candidate finding of severity 3
and 12 of severity 2, where the bar allows 0 and at most 3.** None is confirmed: the screenshot
audit confirms counts, gaps and alignment, and has not read these screenshots.

The fixes the pass made hold where they were aimed: a new Or group whose step is a range shows no
count on its row; the add buttons say "And..." in a group that starts with everything, "Add..." in
one that starts with nothing, and "Or..."; By position draws its end, count and unit on one row;
the empty Fill color box carries a cue; Advanced is closed on every step pane until opened. The eye
lands on the graph, the rule's rows or the next control on every screen walked. What keeps the
build outside the limits is mostly carried over from the frozen build's check
(`rounds/round-1-void/calm-first-frozen.md`): the inspector and a blank column beside every
workspace, rows out of line at rest, a card named by its first step, and the screens at rest over
the words budget. Two are new: the calm first state of an Or group does not hold for a "Within
hops of" step, and the words the fix pass added are cut in their boxes ("Hig...", "Pick or type a
co").
