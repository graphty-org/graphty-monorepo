## Per-fix verdicts

1. **Only one count spoken while a layer is selected (filter host): fixed.** The accessibility walk's step 14,
   picking trained Yes, now speaks one line, "15 of 24 nodes" (`fa/14.png`); before, a second "4 of 24 nodes"
   followed. The same holds for the hours step ("16 of 24 nodes" alone, `fa/21.png`) and in the style host under
   a filter ("4 of 15 nodes" alone, `sb/15.png`). The Undo path outside the workspace still speaks another base
   (finding 1).
2. **A step picked from a run over the visible nodes: fixed.** After Connected components ran on the 112 nodes
   left by Degree min 10, the new card's picker offers "Connected components (112 visible), 3 values" and "Piece
   size (112 visible), 2 to 105" (`s23/08.png`). Picking the first gives Group 1 105, Group 2 5, Group 3 2, (no
   value) 437 (`s23/09.png`); ticking Group 1 gives 105 of 549 (`s23/10.png`). Piece size reads 2 to 105 with
   "Not measured: 437" (`s23/12.png`). The Results section still lists one Connected components run after both
   picks (`s23/11.png`): no second whole-graph run. The scope is not carried onto the picked value (finding 9).
3. **By position: fixed, with a fit problem.** The row is end, box, unit; "Highest | Lowest" and "nodes | % of
   nodes" both open and apply (`s13/08.png`, `s13/10.png`; Lowest 5 % of nodes gives 2 of 24, `s13/11.png`, and
   Done leaves "PageRank: Influence bottom 5%" in the inspector, `s13/12.png`). "top 5" now says "'top 5' is not a
   number. Type a number, like 10. Nothing is cut until a count is typed." with 24 of 24, which agrees with the
   screen (`s13/06.png`). The end and unit selects are cut (finding 4).
4. **Is exactly on an empty text step: fixed.** It stays "Is exactly" by pointer, is ticked when the list is
   reopened (`s18/04.png`, `s18/05.png`), and typed text matches exactly: "Fox" gives 0 of 11, "Passes none"
   (`s18/19.png`). The count lags while the suggestion list is open (finding 6).

   staff", saved with Done and reopened with Enter, stays in Filters at All nodes after Clear then Escape (three
