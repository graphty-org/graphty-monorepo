### Fix lines

1. `WhichPicker.tsx:435` speaks the inspector row's count with `useAnnounceChange` while a selection workspace
   is open; `SelectionEditor.tsx:368` already says "the selection's lines stay quiet while an editor is open".
   Pass null there while a workspace is open. Check: a `real.mjs --sr` repro of k2/28.png speaks one line,
   "11 of 19 nodes".
2. A card that holds steps is not "new" once a step was made: Escape on the picker that Clear opened closes the
   picker and keeps the card at All nodes (or Clear's undo step survives). Check: k2's Clear, Escape keeps the
   card, and Ctrl+Z after it restores the steps.
3. Put the workspace region in the Tab order where it is drawn (before the side panel it sits beside) and Tab
   from its last control into the uncovered panel, not the status bar. No trap.
4-5. Build the reference picker's search from the same searchable combobox the value picker uses (expanded
   state, result count, Tab closes and moves on).
6. Keep arrows in the radio group; move focus to the edge value only on Tab.
