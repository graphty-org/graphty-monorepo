fix. This walk has 8 that count for bar 7 (findings 1 to 6, 8 and 9). Finding 7 is the same
thing as calm-first finding 6, so it counts once, in bar 9, which has the smaller allowance.

listed here only as pointers.

hosts, in both pickers and in every task that types into a picker.

  (`w1/53.png`).

   its first row.

   placeholders.

7. Give the sidebar outline the workspace outline's 40 px verb column.
8. Keep a filter card's name on Clear.
