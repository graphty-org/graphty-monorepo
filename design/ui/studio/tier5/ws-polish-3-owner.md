# Selection workspace polish round 3: the owner's lens

The selection workspace is the non-modal editor that opens over the canvas column when the reader
presses Edit on a style layer's "Which nodes" or on a Data filter card. At rest, the sidebar shows a
read-only outline of the rule. This review judges the build of `18e34e7295` (HEAD of
design/studio-tier5, which contains the interrupted round's commit `4a3ed7cf1e` and the two polish
rounds built on it) against:

- every point of the owner's feedback of 2026-11-01 (`owner-feedback-2026-11-01.md`): calm,
  organized, progressive disclosure, words, the "+" signs, large graphs, and whether he would want
  to walk away;
- the six fixes from the placement study (`placement-study.md`);
- the findings of the previous owner-lens review (`ws-polish-2-owner.md`), to see which held.

`graphty/dist` was rebuilt from HEAD and copied before the review, so no other rebuild could change
it mid-session. The app was driven with real input through `real.mjs`. Screenshots are in the main
checkout's `tmp/design-studio/tier5/ws-polish-3/owner/`:

- `o1` (1440 x 900): Les Miserables, a new style layer: Edit, Fit, Degree min 10, Or..., name
  contains Val, Done, reopen, Clear, Cancel, then Advanced > Edges where shared_chapters min 2,
  Escape three times.
- `o2` (1440 x 900): Les Miserables, Data: filter cards Degree min 10, then Degree max 3.
- `o3` (1440 x 900): `dept-50k.graphml`, 50,000 nodes, 100,000 edges, a node value "dept" with 40
  categories (generated by `gen.py` in the same folder): a new layer, dept, four ticks, search
  "D1", Check 10, Done.
- `o4` (1280 x 800): Les Miserables, a new layer, Edit, Degree, the step's "..." menu.

Every finding below was seen on screen; the timings are the tool's own measure of how long each
click took to be accepted (`route.jsonl`, `ms.act`).

## Findings, most severe first

### 1. Major: the preview key sits on the graph and repeats the outline

- **Evidence:**
    - `o1/03.png` and `crop-o1-03.png`: on opening a new layer on Les Miserables, edges run up into
      the key "Preview: Which nodes" (x 310 to 548, y 92 to 152) and stop there. After Fit
      (`o1/04.png`) the same top cluster of about 8 nodes appears at y 175 to 240, so on open it was
      under the key.
    - `o1/07.png`, `o1/08.png`, `o1/12.png`: the key grows with each row (No match, then B), to y 223,
      and covers the top cluster even after Fit.
    - `o2/02.png` (filter card), `o4/02.png` (1280 x 800, where the preview is 423 px wide): the same.
    - `o1/10.png`: the key says "A 22", "B 1, under A", "No match 55"; the outline 250 px to its left
      says "A 22", "or B 1". The header says "22 of 77".
- **Why it matters:** the preview is why the workspace exists, and the first thing the reader sees
  in it is a card covering part of his graph. It is also the owner's first point in a new place:
  the same groups and counts are drawn in the outline and again in the key. Framing around the key
  is not the answer: the owner's rule is that the canvas is the frame and overlays are never view
  insets.
- **Fix:** drop the floating key inside the workspace. The outline's group rows already carry the
  chit, the letter and the count, and the header carries the result. Move "No match 55" to a dimmed
  last row of the outline column (no selection, no hover), and "under A" into group B's row tooltip.
  The header's "Group colors | Styled" segment already says the colors are a preview, which was the
  key's reason to exist (spec section 9). Spec change: section 9's canvas key bullet and 4.3's
  "Preview key and table" row.

### 2. Major: a filter card says "33 of 77" while the cards together leave nothing

- **Evidence:** `o2/06.png`: editing the second card, Degree max 3, the header reads "33 of 77" and
  33 nodes are drawn green. The first card keeps Degree min 10, so together the cards pass none.
  The workspace never says so: the Filters header drops its total while the workspace is open. The
  only sign is the app's top bar, "0 of 77 nodes, 0 of 254 edges", far from the editor. After Done
  (`o2/07.png`) the canvas is empty and the Filters header reads "Passes none".
- **Why it matters:** he builds a card, sees 33 green nodes, presses Done and gets an empty canvas.
  The editor showed him a number that was never going to be the result.
- **Fix:** keep the Filters header's total ("Passes none", "41 of 77") drawn in the sidebar while a
  card is being edited. It is a different fact from the card's own count, so it does not repeat
  the header; it is the one place that says what the cards do together. Spec 3.0 ("the other cards
  keep their rows but drop their counts") keeps the header total.

### 3. Major: each tick on 50,000 nodes freezes the page for seconds

- **Evidence:** `o3/route.jsonl`, the time each click took to be accepted on `dept-50k.graphml`
  with the force layout running (it never settled in the session):
    - first tick (D05): 14.4 s (`o3/06.png`);
    - next ticks (D03, D04, D02): 3.4 s, 3.0 s, 3.6 s;
    - Check 10: 4.3 s; Done: 3.4 s; typing "D1" in the checklist search: 1.8 s.
- **Why it matters:** the owner's large-graph point was about whether the editor is viable at 50,000
  nodes. A list he can scroll is not viable if every box he ticks stalls the page for three
  seconds, and the first for fourteen. Whether the layout running alongside is the cause or only
  makes it worse, this is the state every reader meets right after opening a large file.
- **Fix:** profile the tick with the layout running, not only on a still graph (the round 2 profile
  of 4.9 s to 2.6 s was without it). Draw the tick and the outline row at once and run the count and
  the preview repaint after the frame, so the box never waits on the count. The evaluation and its
  scheduling belong in graphty-element (its styles evaluation, off the main thread where it can),
  not in the app.

### 4. Major: a new or cleared rule opens on an empty column, and typing does nothing

- **Evidence:**
    - `o1/03.png`, `o4/02.png`: a new layer's workspace. The properties column, 320 x 760 px at
      1440 x 900, holds one field, "Value...", and nothing else. The field has focus but shows no
      focus ring, and typing "Degree" types nothing (`o4`: "focus is on button Value; typed nothing").
    - `o1/13.png`: after Clear the column holds only "Condition..." and "Or...".
- **Why it matters:** this is the first screen of every new rule, and the screen after every Clear.
  It is the owner's "why are there empty spaces", and a reader who starts typing where the focus is
  gets nothing. The previous round opened the picker here, and the review then counted that as
  holding.
- **Fix:** on a selection with no step (new, or after Clear), open the value picker under the field
  at once, as round 2 did. At least make a printable key in the focused field open the picker with
  that text in its search.

### 5. Minor: the checklist footer reads as wrong arithmetic

- **Evidence:** `o3/05.png`: 8 rows drawn, footer "8 of 40". After two ticks (`o3/07.png`): 8 rows
  drawn, footer "6 of 38". After four (`crop-o3-09.png`): "4 of 36".
- **Fix:** say how many are not drawn: "32 more" (the spec's own "2,077 more checked" form). The
  footer then stays put while ticking. Spec 4.3's "6 of 4,999" changes with it.

### 6. Minor: an edge condition keeps focus on its field and is tagged before it scopes anything

- **Evidence:** `o1/18.png`: after picking shared_chapters, focus stays on the field, so typing "2"
  typed nothing; a step's value moves focus to its test. With the range untouched (1 to 31, every
  edge passes) the row already carries "Edges" and Advanced says "Edges: shared_chapters".
- **Fix:** after an edge value is picked, focus its Min box. Draw the row's Edges tag and
  Advanced's summary only once the condition leaves an edge out.

### 7. Minor: "No test" and "Condition..." are the words left to guess

- **Evidence:** `o1/06.png`, `o4/04.png`: a step just given Degree reads "Degree No test" in the
  outline. `o1/06.png`: the pane's first row is "Condition... Or...".
- **Why it matters:** the owner asked what "Select", "Filter to" and "Filter out" mean; "No test" and
  "Condition" are the same kind of word. "Condition... / Or..." also reads as two unlike things,
  where they are the pair "narrow" and "widen".
- **Fix:** a step with a value and no range reads just its value ("Degree"), no state word, since
  the header's "77 of 77" already shows it changes nothing. Ask the owner (`owner-decisions.md`)
  whether "And... / Or..." should replace "Condition... / Or..."; it is a word choice he owns.

### 8. Minor: the value picker changes width

- **Evidence:** `o1/05.png`: the picker is as wide as its field (287 px). `o1/08.png`, after Or...:
  220 px under the same field. `o2/02.png`: 240 px under a 286 px field.
- **Fix:** the picker always takes its field's width.

### 9. Minor: "Match case" and a lone "1 so far" on a text step

- **Evidence:** `o1/10.png`: the text step draws "Match case" below its box at all times, and its
  first row under "Condition... Or..." is empty but for "1 so far" at the right.
- **Fix:** move Match case into Advanced (and name it on Advanced's right when set). Draw "so far"
  on the row of the first control, not on a row of its own.

### 10. Minor: the sidebar outline at rest is indented into a narrow strip

- **Evidence:** `o1/11.png`: in the 225 px inspector, "or" sits at the left edge, the chits 45 px in
  and the steps 70 px in ("Degree min 10" starts at x 1287 of 1217 to 1417). `o1/01.png`: a lone
  "All nodes" floats 55 px right of its title, under nothing.
- **Fix:** in the sidebar's read-only outline, narrow the verb column to the width of "or" and
  drop the extra step indent; "All nodes" starts under the title's text.

### 11. Minor (still open from round 2): four "+" in the Data column

- **Evidence:** `o2/01.png`: "+" on Data sources, on the node count row "77", on the edge count row
  "254" and on Filters. The "Attributes" header has nothing under it; the count rows sit under a
  second "Les Miserables" heading.
- **Note:** recorded for the owner in `owner-decisions.md` ("the Data place's attribute '+' on the
  count rows"). It is the owner's "+" point one panel over from the editor, and it is what he will
  see when he opens Filters.

## What holds

- **The sidebar while editing:** only "Which nodes Editing" (`o1/03.png`); a filter card "Editing"
  in its box (`o2/02.png`). The rule is not drawn twice in the sidebar any more.
- **The canvas is the frame:** the preview is the canvas, sized to its cell; the 50,000-node graph
  is framed inside it (`o3/04.png`), and the camera is put back on close (`o1/14.png`, `o3/12.png`).
- **Reopening:** lands on the step last edited (`o1/12.png`).
- **Verbs:** no Keep word, one Except switch, "or B" between groups; Add instead in "..." only where
  it changes the group (`o4/04.png`).
- **Edges, not links:** "Edges", "All edges", "Edges where ...", "Edge value...", the tag "Edges"
  (`o1/15.png` to `o1/20.png`); the step's histogram redraws on the scoped edges (0 to 22).
- **Clear, Cancel, Escape:** Clear returns to All nodes, 77 of 77; Cancel restores; Escape closes
  Advanced, then the pane, then the workspace as Done, focus back on Edit (`o1-esc.png`).
- **Edit on the count:** replaces it whole ("Which nodes Edit", "Degree min 10 Edit").
- **The still checklist on 50,000 nodes:** ticking never moves a row; search, "Check 10", and "dept
  14 values" at rest.
- **Drawing:** the key did not stay on "Drawing" while the 50,000-node layout kept running.
- **The pickers:** open below their fields inside the properties column, never over the header.
