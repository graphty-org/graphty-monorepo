do in today's tools. Each document covers one population, cites its sources, and proposes the Designloom personas,
workflows and capabilities in `design/designloom/` that represent it.

  in-scope persona records: SEO crawl and site-architecture analysts (dependency document, persona 3.7 and Workflow
  5.11; seo-site-architecture-analyst, Tier B, W86), family historians and genetic genealogists working from DNA

  unknown-parentage-search-angel, professional-genetic-genealogist and endogamous-heritage-dna-researcher, all Tier B,
  W185, W186 and W2096-W2099), and
  central-bank systemic-risk analysts (supply-chain document, section 3.9; economic-network-analyst, Tier B). Section 9
  of [population-to-designloom-persona-index.md](population-to-designloom-persona-index.md) gives the evidence for each.
- **Populations deferred or out of scope.** Graph algorithm learners (step-by-step animation as a teaching product),
  fuzzy cognitive map modelers who need scenario simulation, cellular connectomics researchers, interpretability
  researchers exploring attribution graphs, telecom outside-plant planners, community managers and pharmacovigilance
  analysts. Trade economists and molecular networking chemists are in scope (economic-network-analyst and
  molecular-networking-chemist, both Tier B). Power-grid planners, control-room operators,
  landscape connectivity planners, whole-brain modelers, malware reverse engineers, model computation-graph
  inspectors and Boolean, ODE or flux-balance simulation are out of scope because a solver, simulator or disassembler
  owns the graph; signaling and metabolomics modelers who build, style and read signed causal networks or pathway
  overlays are in scope as genomics-cytoscape-user variants. Authors of hand-written text diagrams (Mermaid, PlantUML, D2, DOT in Markdown) are out of scope because
  the diagrams are small documentation rendered by the host where they are read; machine-generated DOT too large for
  a static image (terraform graph, bazel query) is in scope as the build-graph workflow W84
  ([developers-embedding-and-extending-graph-visualization.md](developers-embedding-and-extending-graph-visualization.md),
  section 2).

- **Proposed capabilities are unbuilt and unreviewed.** About 330 capabilities proposed by this research are recorded
  in `design/designloom/capabilities/` with status `planned` and no implementing component. Near-duplicates were merged
  by meaning; the merged descriptions are kept as requirements. None has yet been checked against graphty-element's
  shipped features, so some may already exist under another name.
- **No observation of real users.** Every workflow is reconstructed from manuals, tutorials, papers, protocols and
  practitioner self-reports, not from watching people work. Workflows carry `validated: false` until someone checks
  them with members of the population.
