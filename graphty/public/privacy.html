Privacy

graphty reads your files on your computer. They are never uploaded. This page lists every piece of usage data the app sends, who receives it, and how to turn it off.

There are two levels. A few anonymous counts are sent for every visitor, whatever you answer. Everything else is sent only after you choose Share usage data, and stops when you turn it off.

Always sent, whatever you answer

These counts go to Umami Cloud. They use no cookies, and Umami stores nothing in your browser. Each event below is sent at most once per page load.

Event What it carries
Page view The page path, with only the channel that sent you: the ref, utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign values when they are on a short fixed list (such as hn, reddit, github, newsletter); any other value is sent as other, and every other part of the address is dropped. The referring site's origin only (for example https://news.ycombinator.com). The page title is always sent as "graphty". Your language and screen size are not sent.
first_visit Nothing else.
return_visit days: how long ago your first visit was, as one of 1, 2-7, 8-30 or 30+. Sent at most once a day.
usage_data_shared Nothing else. Sent when you choose Share usage data. Nothing is sent for No thanks.
sample_loaded sample: the id of a built-in sample, or other.
own_file_opened format: the file format's id (such as graphml or csv, graphty for a project file), or other. via: file, url, server or join. Never the file's name, its address or its contents.
layout_changed layout: the layout's id, or other. outcome: applied or failed.
algorithm_run algorithm: the measure's id, or other. outcome: succeeded, failed or canceled.
export_done kind: graph, project, styles, recipe, image or video. format: the format chosen, for a graph, image or video. destination: download or clipboard, for an image.

Visits

To tell a first visit from a return, the app keeps two dates in this browser (graphty.visits.v1): the day of your first visit and of your latest one. Only the days bucket above leaves your computer, never the dates. A record older than about 13 months starts again. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, the app keeps no visit record and sends no first_visit or return_visit; the page view and the other counts are still sent.

What Umami receives with each count

Like any web request, each count reaches Umami Cloud with your IP address and your browser's User-Agent. Umami does not store the IP address. It does store what it derives from the request: an approximate location (country, region and city) from the IP address, and your browser, operating system and device type from the User-Agent. From the IP address, the User-Agent and a secret salt it derives a pseudonymous session id, so counts from one visitor can be grouped; the salt is replaced every month, so that id changes monthly. The app ignores and never stores any id Umami sends back.

Sent only with Share usage data

When you choose Share usage data, the app also sends the following to PostHog (EU) and Sentry. Before either starts recording, the app removes everything after the path from the address bar, so a replay never records it.

PostHog

  • Everything in the table above, every time it happens rather than once per page load, plus your channel, the days bucket and, once you have answered the survey, your answer to "What are you trying to do?".
  • A replay of some sessions. All text, typing and labels are hidden, every attribute that could hold a name is masked, and the graph area, images, video and file pickers are recorded as blank boxes. Console messages, network headers and request bodies are not recorded.
  • Clicks and control changes, with all text and every value masked: where on the page you click, repeated or ineffective clicks, that you changed a control (a menu, checkbox, slider or text box) or submitted a form, never what you chose or typed, and, for controls that run a command, the command's id (such as history.undo). Clicks on the graph itself are not recorded as clicks.
  • What you do, by id only: command_run and command_failed (the command's id and, when it failed, the error code); graph_action (the kind of change, such as a style, a filter or a note, never its contents or the note's text); canvas_selected (node, edge, both or none; how many, as 1, 2-10 or 11+; and whether by click, box or keys); camera_used (orbit, pan or zoom); nodes_dragged; source_expanded (expanded or failed); panel_opened (the panel's id); project_reopened; load_failed (the error code and whether it failed reading or preparing); the error code of a measure that failed.
  • Page speed (Web Vitals timings), and when you leave the page.
  • Survey answers, if you answer a short survey, including any text you type in its Other box. Do not paste data from your graph there: what you type is sent.
  • PostHog keeps a random id in this browser while sharing is on, so it can tell your later visits apart from other people's. The app never links it to a name or email. Turning sharing off deletes it, in every open tab.
  • The app keeps your survey answer and which surveys you have seen in this browser (graphty.surveys.v1). Turning sharing off deletes the answer.

Sentry

  • Error reports: the kind of error and where in the code it happened; for graphty's own errors their code, never their message; for any other error its message with everything in quotes removed. Also your browser and operating system, your browser's language and time zone, and the clicks and network requests just before the error, with any address on graphty's own site cut to the page path and any other address sent only as the word external.
  • A replay of the moments before an error, masked the same way: text, typing, names and media hidden. Only the moments before each error are sent: sessions are not recorded by Sentry.

Every address sent to PostHog or Sentry is cut the same way as the page view on graphty's own site, to the path and the channel. An address anywhere else, a file's or a server's, is sent only as the word external.

Problem reports

Help > Report a problem sends to Sentry your message, an optional name and email, and any attachments you add. Today it works only while Share usage data is on.

Never sent

  • The contents of your files.
  • File names, and the addresses of files or servers you open.
  • Node and edge labels, attribute values and column names.
  • Project names and note text.
  • A PostHog id, or anything at all to PostHog or Sentry, before you choose Share usage data.
  • A location from PostHog or Sentry: PostHog's IP location lookup is off, and Sentry is told never to record your IP address. (Umami's approximate location is listed above, under Always sent.)

If you turn on the assistant and add a key from an AI provider, what the assistant reads from your graph goes to that provider. That is separate from usage data and is shown in Settings > Privacy.

Where it goes

  • Umami Cloud (gateway.umami.is): the anonymous counts.
  • PostHog, EU region (eu.i.posthog.com, and eu-assets.i.posthog.com for its settings): only with Share usage data. PostHog's code is not even downloaded before then.
  • Sentry (sentry.io): only with Share usage data.

The data is used only by graphty's author, and his Claude Code sessions, to improve the app.

How to turn it off

  • Answer No thanks to the usage data question, or turn off Share usage data in Settings > Privacy (the privacy chip in the status bar opens it). PostHog and Sentry stop at once, in every open tab, and PostHog's id is deleted. Nothing that happens after that is sent; PostHog may still send what it recorded in the last few seconds before you turned it off, including the click that turned it off.
  • The anonymous counts have no switch in the app. Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control stops the visit record; a content blocker that blocks gateway.umami.is stops the counts.
