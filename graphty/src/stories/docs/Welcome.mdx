# graphty

graphty is a graph visualization app that runs in your browser at [graphty.app](https://graphty.app/).
You open a network -- people and who knows whom, proteins and how they bind, pages and their links
-- and it draws the nodes and edges, finds what matters in them and lets you style, explore and export
the result. Files you open are read on this computer and never uploaded.

This Storybook shows every screen of the app in each of its states, in light and dark. The drawing
itself comes from [graphty-element](https://graphty.app/docs/graphty-element/), the web component the
app is built on; its own Storybook is at
[graphty.app/storybook/graphty-element](https://graphty.app/storybook/graphty-element/).

## What you can do

- **Open a project, a file or a sample graph** on the [Start screen](?path=/docs/workspace-start-screen--docs).
- **Bring in your own tables**, and check them before they load, on the [Data page](?path=/docs/workspace-data-page--docs).
- **See what the data holds** in the [Data place](?path=/docs/workspace-data-place--docs).
- **Run an analysis** -- centrality, communities, paths -- from Analyze on the [Toolbar](?path=/docs/workspace-toolbar--docs).
- **Keep track of what has been done** in the [Graph place](?path=/docs/workspace-graph-place--docs).
- **Look at one node, one edge or one result** in the [Inspector](?path=/docs/workspace-inspector--docs).
- **Color, size, shape and label by the data** on the [Style tab](?path=/docs/workspace-style-tab--docs).
- **Read the data as a table** in the [Table dock](?path=/docs/workspace-table-dock--docs).
- **Save an image or the data** with [Export](?path=/docs/workspace-export--docs).
- **Keep your work** as a [Project](?path=/docs/workspace-project--docs).

To try all of it at once, open the [Full app](?path=/docs/components-app--docs) example.
