# Workspace

The workspace is graphty's window. Every entry in this section is one region of it, drawn in the
states a reader meets: empty, loading, after an analysis, with a menu or a popover open, and when
something goes wrong. Each story runs the real app with the real graphty-element, so what you see
is what [graphty.app](https://graphty.app/) shows.

## The regions, in the order you meet them

1. [Start screen](?path=/docs/workspace-start-screen--docs) -- open a project, a file or a sample,
   and Settings.
2. [Frame](?path=/docs/workspace-frame--docs) -- the window around everything: menus, the project
   name, the rail, keyboard shortcuts and About.
3. [Data page](?path=/docs/workspace-data-page--docs) -- bring in files or a web address and check
   them before they load.
4. [Data place](?path=/docs/workspace-data-place--docs) -- the sources and attributes the project
   holds.
5. [Graph place](?path=/docs/workspace-graph-place--docs) -- what has been done to the graph, and Find.
6. [Canvas](?path=/docs/workspace-canvas--docs) -- the drawing and its legend.
7. [Toolbar](?path=/docs/workspace-toolbar--docs) -- Analyze, Layout, View, Legend and Quick actions.
8. [Inspector](?path=/docs/workspace-inspector--docs) -- whatever is picked, with its values.
9. [Style tab](?path=/docs/workspace-style-tab--docs) -- how nodes and edges look, bound to the data.
10. [Table dock](?path=/docs/workspace-table-dock--docs) -- the data as tables.
11. [Export](?path=/docs/workspace-export--docs) -- an image of the drawing, or the data as a file.
12. [Project](?path=/docs/workspace-project--docs) -- saving, reopening and unsaved changes.

The keys for all of it are on the [Keyboard](?path=/docs/introduction-keyboard--docs) page, and the
files the Data page reads are on [File formats](?path=/docs/introduction-file-formats--docs).

Reference: [graphty.app](https://graphty.app/), and for the drawing, the analyses and the styles
underneath, the [graphty-element documentation](https://graphty.app/docs/graphty-element/).
