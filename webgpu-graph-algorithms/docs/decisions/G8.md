## 0. What the phase built (the deliverable of spec 13 row P8)

Three primitives shipped as planners over a scope (`design/decisions/2026-09-24-frontier-primitives-are-planners.md`):
`prepareCompact` (`compact` = flags + `exclusiveScan` + `compact-scatter`; `dedupe` = `dedupe-claim` + `dedupe-filter`
over an owner array, in a direct and an indirect form), `prepareFrontier` (the `Frontier` class: two vertex queues, one
24-word / 96-byte counters block, seven indirect slots per level for up to 32 levels, a sized edge queue, and
`frontier-finalize`, the device-side selector with four roles) and `prepareAdvance` (`advance-expand`, the block-mapped
expansion with the subgroup twin of `wg_scan_u32`, window-aware through `coreWindows`). Four drivers under
`src/algorithms/`: `bfs.ts` (`breadthFirstSearch`; two-phase `advance-expand` + `bfs-contract`, the fused
`bfs-fused` level, the direction-optimizing `bfs-unvisited-flags` + `compact` + `bfs-bitset-build` + `bfs-bottom-up`
sweep over the reverse view, the choice made on the device by `frontier-finalize` from the counters it already holds;
`parent` and the stable-radix-sorted `order` from `sssp-pred` in depth mode), `sssp.ts` (`sssp`: the near-far queue
over `sssp-relax` with `dedupe` on both piles, the host-derived delta, the two-pass PD-27 predecessor; the unit-weight
route onto BFS), `bellman-ford.ts` (`bellmanFord`: `bf-relax` over `edgeList()` with the bounded compare-exchange, the
decision round, the negative-cycle flag, the rounded-cycle refusal) and `closeness.ts` (`closenessCentrality`: the
bit-parallel 32-sources-per-word `closeness-sweep` and the exact 64-bit `closeness-reduce`; one `sssp` per source on
a weighted snapshot). The four seam members on `createAccelerator`'s object (`breadthFirstSearch`, `sssp`,
`bellmanFord`, `closenessCentrality`), conforming type for type to `@graphty/algorithms`' `AlgorithmAccelerator`
(`test/types/conformance.test-d.ts`), and the barrel exports of the four names and the three result types. Fifteen
kernels (45 ids in `KernelId`): `compact-scatter`, `dedupe-claim`, `dedupe-filter`, `frontier-finalize`,
`advance-expand`, `bfs-contract`, `sssp-pred`, `bfs-fused`, `bfs-bottom-up`, `bfs-bitset-build`,
`bfs-unvisited-flags`, `sssp-relax`, `bf-relax`, `closeness-sweep`, `closeness-reduce` -- 45 compile cases
(`EXPECTED_CASES_BY_PHASE.P8`), three of them twin kernels (`needs: ["subgroups"]`: `advance-expand`,
`bfs-bottom-up`, `bfs-unvisited-flags`). Four independent CPU oracles (`bfsOracle`, `dijkstraOracle` in f32 AND f64,
`bellmanFordOracle`, `closenessOracle`) with the level-consistency, smallest-predecessor, triangle-inequality and
PD-27 chain checks of `test/helpers/traversal-check.ts`. Six constants (`MAX_LEVELS_PER_SUBMIT` 32,
`FUSED_FRONTIER_MAX` 4,096, `FRONTIER_CANDIDATES` 7, `BEAMER_BETA` 24, `SSSP_DELTA_FACTOR` 32, `F32_INF_BITS`) and
the `wg_scan_u32` prelude helper pair. 58 sabotage rows over the fifteen kernels (section 7, G8-F1), 5 noise-floor
rows and 1 derived tolerance (section 4), the `bfs` benchmark group (seven rows) and the committed
`scripts/bench-append-session.js`. Three `node-limits` files (`advance-windowed`, `bfs-large`, `sssp-1m`), two browser
smoke cases (BFS and `sssp` on karate) and two graphty-element browser cases proving the seam reaches the members.
The departures are DEP-P8-A .. G (section 0.5 of the plan) and the five decision records of P8-T16
(`design/decisions/2026-09-24-{frontier-primitives-are-planners,bfs-claims-instead-of-culling,advance-is-window-aware,frontier-members-conform-to-the-seam,edge-queue-overflow-is-a-fused-retry}.md`).

Device memory per call, for the record (design 10.1's "BFS scratch" column was an extrapolation): BFS leases two
vertex queues (4n each), the 96-byte block, the 3,584-byte args buffer, the edge queue (4 x capacity, capacity
`min(arcCount, maxStorageBufferBindingSize / 4)` by default: 84 MB at 1M / 10M), `depth` and `pred` (4n each), the
sorted `order` (8n through the radix sort's ping-pong), the unvisited list and its `sweepIn` scratch
(`roundUp(n, 64) + ceil(n / 32)` words) and `compact`'s offsets (4n) plus block sums. SSSP adds the raw near / far
halves (8 x cap, cap = `roundUp(arcCount, 64)`) beside the frontier's own unused edge queue (4 x cap): 12 x cap bytes
of queue scratch, 168 MB at 1M / 10M for the halves alone, which is why the row needs the raised binding limit.
Closeness: 16 x `roundUp(n, 64)` bytes of bits, 4n frontier list, 4n iota, the 512-byte `perSource` block, the
96-byte counters, one set of `compact`'s offsets and sums (memoised by label, `reusingScratch`).
